# First steps for new students: visa, ID, student card and bank card

What to do after you arrive, in order: paperwork, medical tests, your border number, Absher and Nafath, your Zaair ID, then your student and bank cards.

Do these in order. By the end you'll have three things: your **[button: Zaair ID]** , your **student card**  and your **bank card** .

Tick each step when it's done. Your ticks are saved on this phone, so you can come back to see what's left.

## 1. University paperwork

Finish your admission and enrolment at the university. You'll be given a **medical test paper** , the first step towards your study visa ID (the Zaair ID).

## 2. Medical tests

- Take the paper to the **University Hospital** .
- Do the **blood test and X-ray** .
- Wait **2 to 3 days**  for the results.

## 3. Results and border number

After 2 to 3 days, go to **[button: Idarat as-Safar]**  (the travel and passport office). Collect your results and your **[button: Raqm al-Hudood]**  (رقم الحدود, your border number).

## 4. Absher and Nafath

- Download the **[button: Absher]**  (أبشر) and **[button: Nafath]**  (نفاذ) apps.
- Create an Absher account with your Raqm al-Hudood.
- Verify the account through Nafath.

This usually takes 10 to 15 minutes.

## 5. Your Zaair ID

- Once Absher and Nafath are set up, you'll receive your **Zaair ID** .
- Go back to **Idarat as-Safar**  and make sure they enter your Raqm al-Hudood into the system, so your details are officially recorded.

## 6. Student card

- Take your Zaair ID to **[button: Maktab 22]**  (the Istiqbal office) at the university.
- At the **Student Card Office** , show your Zaair ID. They print your student card on the spot.

## 7. Bank card

- In the same Istiqbal office, go to the **Bank Card Office** .
- Tell them you're a new student waiting for your bank card, and they'll add your name to the system.
- The card takes **2 to 3 weeks** . Names are announced on an Excel sheet.
- When your name appears, go back with your student card and collect your bank card straight away.

# Taking a semester off: ta'jeel and i'tithaar

How to postpone a semester (ta'jeel) or withdraw during one (i'tithaar), what each does to your stipend, room, visa and 4-year deadline, and how to apply on the portal.

Updated 21 Oct 2026. From a student's guide, version 4, 14 August 2023 (not official university information)

## Ta'jeel and i'tithaar in short

|  | Ta'jeel (تأجيل) | I'tithaar (اعتذار) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Postponing a semester before it starts | An excuse to stop studying during a semester |
| When | A short window before the semester (see the academic calendar) | After the ta'jeel window closes, during the semester |
| Needs approval of your reason | No | Yes. If the university doesn't accept it, you carry on studying |
| Counts as a semester studied | No | Yes, as not passed. You repeat it |
| 4-year Kulliyah deadline | Extended by the semester you took | Not extended |
| Stipend (mukafa'ah) | Stops | Stops |
| Dorm room | Hand it back and empty it | You may need to |
| Leaving Saudi | Yes | You may need to |

## Ta'jeel

- You can take it for **at most 2 semesters**  (one academic year) per course: one semester, or two in a row. If you take one, you can take another later on the same course.
- Moving from the ma'had to the Kulliyah counts as a new course, so you can take ta'jeel again.
- Students take it for things that can't wait: health or family emergencies, marriage, or anything that would otherwise make them fail or quit.
- **1 semester:**  you get an **exit re-entry visa** , and you're automatically back in the system the next semester.
- **2 semesters:**  you get a **final exit visa**  and must do the whole visa process again to come back.
- You usually **pay your own flights** . The university only pays the first ticket and the yearly summer return ticket.
- **When:**  usually a week or two before the semester. The academic calendar gives the exact window. Miss it and you can only apply for i'tithaar.

## I'tithaar

Applied for during the semester you're in. Your reason must be accepted, the semester counts as studied but not passed, and your stipend stops.

**The 4-year deadline doesn't move.**  For example, if you started the Kulliyah in September 2023 you must finish by September 2027. With one ta'jeel, the deadline moves to January 2028. With one i'tithaar, it stays at September 2027, so you'd need to fit the missed modules into later semesters or summers. If you don't finish in time, you risk not getting your degree.

## How to apply on the portal

The steps are the same for both. The [full guide (PDF)](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/assets/files/tajeel-and-itithaar-guide-v4-2023.pdf) has screenshots of every screen.

1. Log in at [cas.iu.edu.sa](https://cas.iu.edu.sa/cas/login) and open **Academic System** .
2. Under **أكاديمي** , choose **ادخال حركات اكاديمية** , then **ادخال حركات أكاديمية جديدة** .
3. Tap **أوافق**  (I agree).
4. Set **نوع الحركة**  (type) to **تأجيل**  for ta'jeel, or **الاعتذار**  for i'tithaar (it appears once the ta'jeel window has closed). Don't choose **إنسحاب** : that withdraws you from your course.
5. Choose the semesters:
- **1 semester:**  set both **من بداية الفصل**  and **إلى نهاية الفصل**  to the same semester, e.g. الفصل الأول to الفصل الأول. Don't pick semester 1 to semester 2.
- **2 semesters:**  set **إلى نهاية الفصل**  to الفصل الثاني.
6. Choose your reason (**السبب** ). Add notes under **ملاحظات**  and supporting PDFs under **مرفقات**  if needed (often for i'tithaar). Tap **التالي**  (next).
7. Check the summary and tap **حفظ**  (save), then **انهاء**  (finish).
8. **See the wakeel**  of your kulliyah or ma'had (responsible for student affairs) in case there are more forms to complete.
9. **Go to  [button: Idarat as-Safar]**  (the travel office) to request your visa.

**Checking your request:**  repeat steps 1 and 2, but choose **إظهار الحركات الأكاديمية** . After about a week from the end of the ta'jeel window, your dashboard's **الوضع العام**  (status) should change from **منتظم**  (regular) to **مؤجل**  (postponed).

## Questions

**Can I apply from abroad?**  Technically yes, as it's online. But someone has to empty your room and return the key, and you can't sort out any problems in person.

**Can I cancel a request?**  There's an X under **حذف**  (delete) in your request list that seems to delete it, but the guide's author hadn't tried it. Ask your kulliyah or ma'had, as they may need to put you back into your classes.

**Should I just stay home on my current exit re-entry visa if I want next semester off?**  No. Your visa expires and the university won't issue a new one while you're outside the country. Renewing it can mean a fine of 100 riyals for every month it's expired, paid in Saudi, and it gets harder if your  [button: iqama]  or passport also expire. Come back on your current visa and get a new exit re-entry visa from here.

# Converting your driver's license to a Saudi one

Swap your home country license for a Saudi license without a driving test, from translation to the driving school.

Updated 21 Oct 2026. From the MESH Telegram group

[Share on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/?text=Converting%20your%20driver%27s%20license%20to%20a%20Saudi%20one%20(MESH)%0Ahttps%3A%2F%2Fmesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev%2Fvisas%2Fdrivers-license%2F) [button: Save]

You can swap the license from your home country for a Saudi one without taking a driving test. It takes a few stops. Put all your papers in one folder before you start.

## What you need

- At least **4 passport photos**
- Your **driver's license** , translated (step 1)
- **Two printed copies each**  of your passport,  [button: iqama]  (residency card), driver's license and the translated license
- A **phone number linked to  [button: Absher]** , the government services app

## Steps

1. **Get your license translated**  at student affairs, on the 2nd or 3rd floor.
2. **Print two copies**  of your passport, iqama, driver's license and the translated license. Put everything in a folder.
3. **Go to King Fahad Hospital**  with 2 passport photos and get a **vision test and a blood test** . [Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YfqyFeawvXUyH3URA)
4. **Wait for the results.**  You'll get a notification on your phone when they're uploaded. Then you're ready for the driving school.
5. **Book an appointment on Absher**  with **Model Driving School** . It's the only school that swaps your license without a test.
6. **Go to Model Driving School** , near the airport, and ask for **استبدال**  (license exchange). It's a small office on the right as soon as you walk through the gates. [Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HKmiZ3w4jvgmNJ6r6)

> Tip
>
>
>
> Bring the whole folder to every stop, including the spare passport photos.

# Starting a business in Saudi Arabia

How to set up a MISA-licensed company yourself, what it really costs, the common mistakes, and a professional to contact if you'd rather hand it over.

## A company formation contact

**Aneed Ashraf:**  [aneedashraf.de/en](https://aneedashraf.de/en). He has worked in company formation and business setup in Saudi Arabia for more than 10 years, and says plainly that he isn't the cheapest option.

## What to expect

From his experience:

- **It takes time.**  Several authorities, approvals, platforms and requirements are involved, and not everything is in the provider's hands. Be wary of anyone promising a fully established company within a few days.
- **It's less predictable than in Europe.**  Requirements can change and unexpected issues come up, so experience in handling them matters.
- **Compare on more than price.**  Ask how they deal with problems, what's included and how transparent they'll be along the way.

## Doing it yourself: a MISA-licensed company

These notes come from a workshop by Ross, an Australian brother in Madinah who recently went through the process himself. Ministries change their portals and fees, so if anything here doesn't match what you see, trust the portal.

### Pick the right licence first

| If you have | Licence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A foreign company at least a year old, with audited accounts | **Service**  (most people) | Consulting, IT, marketing, training, e-learning. No minimum capital on paper, though banks may want about SAR 100,000 in the account |
| No foreign company yet, solo and pre-revenue | **Entrepreneur (Riyadi)** | Needs a sponsor letter from a Saudi accelerator or incubator. Renewed yearly for up to 5 years; plan to move to a standard licence by year 2 or 3 |
| Wholesale or retail of physical goods | Trading | SAR 30 million capital if 100% foreign, much less with a 25%+ Saudi partner |
| Manufacturing | Industrial | SAR 500,000 capital, plus Ministry of Industry approval |
| Law, accounting, engineering, healthcare | Professional Services | Needs the professional body's approval |

The standard licence fee is SAR 62,000 a year. In 2026 the first year was waived, leaving about SAR 500 in processing. Don't count on the waiver after 2026.

### The order of steps (about 7 weeks)

1. **Before anything, in your home country (2 to 4 weeks):**  notarise and apostille (or legalise at the Saudi embassy) your parent company's registration, articles of association, last year's audited accounts, a board resolution approving the Saudi investment and naming the general manager, and the GM's passport. Have them translated into Arabic by a certified translator in Saudi.
2. **Apply for the MISA licence**  on [investsaudi.sa](https://investsaudi.sa/) under your foreign company's name. Approval takes about 1 to 2 weeks.
3. **Reserve your Saudi trade name**  through the Ministry of Commerce / Saudi Business Centre.
4. **Amend the MISA licence to the reserved name.**  It's free and takes about an hour, but the commercial registration (CR) will be refused if you skip it.
5. **File the CR**  through the Saudi Business Centre (3 to 5 days). Order the **CR Extract**  (SAR 100) too, because banks won't accept the certificate alone.
6. **GOSI, Qiwa and ZATCA register automatically**  within about an hour of the CR. Log in to each to activate. Register for VAT from day one, and set up a free national address with Saudi Post.
7. **Visas and  [button: iqama] :**  once Qiwa is live, your company issues your general manager work visa. Stamp it at a Saudi consulate, enter Saudi, and your iqama follows in 2 to 4 weeks. If you're already here on someone else's iqama, transfer it through Muqeem instead (needs your current sponsor's release). Family visas can follow once your salary in Mudad is at least about SAR 4,000 a month.
8. **Open the corporate bank account**  in person (the general manager must sign). Apply to two banks at once. Bring the CR, CR Extract, Saudi and foreign articles of association, national address, MISA licence and your **printed**  iqama card.

### What it cost in one recent setup

About **SAR 11,900**  in government fees in year one, including apostilles (about 1,500), the Saudi Business Centre (1,775), the CR (about 1,163), Muqeem subscription, change of occupation and iqama print (about 2,840) and a second iqama transfer. The Chamber of Commerce adds about SAR 1,000 to 3,000 a year. Agencies quote SAR 30,000 to 50,000 for the same work.

> Check first
>
>
>
> **Mistakes that cost founders time and money:**  choosing the wrong licence type; skipping the MISA name amendment; a badly translated Arabic company name (it's the legally binding one, so pay a lawyer SAR 500 to 1,500 to check it); hiring two foreigners before your first Saudi employee, which drops your Nitaqat (Saudisation) rating; withdrawing deposited capital early; skipping VAT; and English-only contracts, since the Arabic version is what counts in court.

### The municipal licence trap

To transfer an iqama to your company, the system may expect a municipal (Baladi) licence, which needs a physical office. You can rent a serviced office (around SAR 4,000 a month), or hire a Saudi employee early. Founders report the second is cheaper and fixes your Nitaqat rating within about a week, which unblocks the transfer.

### Saudi or the UAE?

The UAE is cheaper to run and has lower taxes (9% corporate tax and 5% VAT, against 20% and 15% in Saudi for a foreign-owned company). Saudi wins if you're building a life here and selling into the much larger local market.

Qur'an classes cover Juz 'Amma and part of Juz Tabarak, so it isn't a hifz programme.

## Markaz Taibah

- **Where:**  Hayy Taibah. [Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NPFFeZu8MUvxfnK19)
- **Cost:**  free, with a small fee for books
- **Length:**  2 years, 4 levels
- **Curriculum:**  al-Arabiyyah Bayna Yadayk books 1 to 4, with Qur'an and writing at every level. Level 1 adds al-Qa'idah an-Nuraniyyah; levels 2 to 4 add fiqh, aqeedah, seerah and hadith; levels 3 and 4 add tafseer; level 4 adds imla'.
- **To apply:**  passport and your husband's iqama, then a placement exam. If there's no space, you start the following semester.
- **For mothers:**  free nursery; a bus is available for a fee

**What students say:**  high-level teaching with a strong focus on grammar and comprehension, in small classrooms. Some students finish the levels and still struggle to hold a conversation, so if speaking is your goal, students recommend Markaz Mishkat. Mothers report that the nursery is very small and children mostly watch television there.

## Markaz Academiyah al-Ma'rifah

Previously Markaz al-Manar, Markaz Ta'allum and Markaz Nebras.

- **Where:**  Hayy Qaswa. [Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YeJDMqFH7acrJsSPA)
- **Cost:**  free, with a small fee for books
- **To apply:**  iqama and/or visa; bring your passport. Placement exam. The Shariah programme expects you to know Arabic already.
- **For mothers:**  free nursery; a bus is available for a fee

Three programmes:

1. **Arabic programme (2 years).**  Starts with Ta'sisi (alphabet, reading, writing, Qur'an), then 4 levels on the Madinah books with nahw, reading, expression and writing. Levels 3 and 4 add aqeedah, hadith, seerah and history. Memorise Juz 'Amma and Juz Tabarak alongside.
2. **Shariah programme (1 year, 2 levels).**  Tafseer, hadith, fiqh, aqeedah, uloom al-Qur'an, nahw, tajweed, usool al-fiqh, then seerah, tarbiyah, mustalah al-hadith and research. Memorisation from Surah adh-Dhariyat (7 ajza by level 2).
3. **Qur'an programme.**  Needs strong Arabic and 7 ajza memorised. Taught by male teachers from the Islamic University, Masjid an-Nabawi and Taibah University, behind a screen, with a female teacher supervising.

**What students say:**  very positive. Good preparation for the Masjid an-Nabawi Ma'had or Kulliyah, and recommended if you don't know Arabic yet.

## Masjid an-Nabawi Ma'had (middle and high school)

- **Where:**  western women's side, gates 13 to 17, behind the brown fence. No children.
- **Cost:**  free
- **Length:**  6 years in two stages, each with a diploma
- **Mutawassit (middle school), 3 years:**  foundational classical texts in tajweed, tawheed, tafseer, hadith, fiqh, fara'id, seerah, nahw, imla' and Arabic calligraphy. Memorise Juz 'Amma.
- **Thanawiyyah (high school), 3 years:**  more advanced texts, adding usool at-tafseer, mustalah al-hadith, history and balaghah. Memorise 7 ajza.
- **To apply:**  registration opens once a year, usually in Dhul Qa'dah; watch their X (Twitter) page for the date. Arabic proficiency is required. Entry exam: a written test (60 marks) and an interview (40 marks) where you read from a book and write what they ask.
- **Thanawiyyah entry:**  7 ajza (Surah az-Zumar to an-Nas), and memorise Umdat al-Ahkam and ar-Rahbiyyah. A portion of Alfiyyat Ibn Malik may also be required (unconfirmed in 2023).

**Links:**  [student guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1aJGYB0Axa8Ge_s55zhVAfWP5ij_XCUVz/view) · [website](https://gph.edu.sa/public/?page=ins) · [first-year curriculum](https://gph.edu.sa/public/?page=page_897272) · [X (Twitter)](https://twitter.com/info_iopm) · [Telegram](https://t.me/almaehad_1)

**What students say:**  a blessed place with some very good teachers, though one student felt the teaching is uneven and many students focus on marks over learning.

## Masjid an-Nabawi Kulliyah (university)

- **Where:**  eastern women's side, gates 25 to 30, behind the brown fence. No children. The office is at gate 13 on the eastern women's side, top floor, and admin staff are also on site.
- **Cost:**  free
- **Length:**  4-year bachelor's degree in Shariah
- **To apply:**
- 15 ajza memorised from Surah an-Nas (10 or 11 may be accepted if you meet the other requirements, at the school's discretion)
- Strong reading and writing in Arabic, with a certificate from a high school or institute
- An entrance exam in fiqh, hadith, nahw, aqeedah and more
- Documents: school certificates, iqama, the printed online application, a letter of permission from your sponsor if you're a dependant, and 2 letters of recommendation (tazkiyah) from a school or Qur'an teacher
- **Admission steps:**
1. Register online through the link they post on X (Twitter).
2. Sit the written exam. If you pass, you're called for an interview focused on Qur'an.
3. The list of accepted students is published on their X account.
4. Bring copies and originals of the required documents (listed on their website), then start your studies.

**Links:**  [student guide](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1iHFyYMPHXrrCVL1ZN5kDSVpsquz-gR5t) · [website](https://gph.edu.sa/public/?page=page_986449) · [X (Twitter)](https://twitter.com/info_fopm_w) · [Telegram](https://t.me/PMCS42)

**What students say:**  studying in the masjid is a spiritual boost and quieter than you'd expect. Classes use a whiteboard, microphone and projector; there are no desks, so bring a small folding chair or prayer mat. There's also an e-learning platform. Teachers include lecturers from other universities and graduates with master's degrees and doctorates, with different styles. Some teach only Arabic, so ask fiqh and aqeedah questions to the right teacher. In the final year, 2 or 3 subjects are taught by male teachers over Zoom, with questions sent through the chat.

# Graduating: room handover, certificates, legalisation and your flight home

Everything to do after your final exams, in order: hand back your room, collect your degree, legalise your documents, collect your bisht and book your ticket.

Updated 21 Oct 2026. Mutakharrij (graduate) procedures guide, compiled by T.M., shared in the MESH Telegram group

[Share on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/?text=Graduating%3A%20room%20handover%2C%20certificates%2C%20legalisation%20and%20your%20flight%20home%20(MESH)%0Ahttps%3A%2F%2Fmesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev%2Fgraduates%2Fgraduate-procedures%2F) [button: Save]

**7 parts** 5 min read

1. [A. Hand back your room (taslim sakan)](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#a-hand-back-your-room-taslim-sakan)
2. [B. Collect your degree and finish at the university](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#b-collect-your-degree-and-finish-at-the-university)
3. [C. Legalisation at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#c-legalisation-at-the-ministry-of-foreign-affairs)
4. [D. Legalisation at your embassy or consulate](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#d-legalisation-at-your-embassy-or-consulate)
5. [E. Collect your graduation bisht and gifts](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#e-collect-your-graduation-bisht-and-gifts)
6. [F. Your ticket home](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#f-your-ticket-home)
7. [Already left without your certificate?](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/graduate-procedures/#already-left-without-your-certificate)

Do these in order. Each step needs the one before it.

## A. Hand back your room (taslim sakan)

The housing office (Idarat al-Iskan) is in **Wihda 22** .

1. **Get the handover form (istimarah)**  from the internal housing office (Iskan Dakhili) in Wihda 22. Say you want to do taslim sakan; they'll ask for your student number.
2. **Clean your room**  and check the wardrobe, drawers and furniture. These five must all be there: **mattress, bed frame, wardrobe, desk and chair** .

> Check first
>
>
>
> Nothing can be missing or swapped. Changing the desk or removing the bed counts as breaking the university's trust, with a **500 riyal fine** . The only extra item you can leave is a carpet.

1. **Arrange the inspection with your building's  [button: mushrif]  (supervisor)** , at his office or by phone. He usually comes after Maghrib or Isha, checks the room, may take photos or video, takes the key, signs the form and sends a copy to Student Affairs. **Keep the original form.**
2. **Next morning, go back to the internal housing office**  for final clearance. Your room is now handed over.

You can take your blanket and bedsheets home. Some students stay a few extra days if the mushrif hasn't asked them to leave, but it's better to stay with friends.

### Prepare these before you go back to the housing office

It saves you extra trips.

- **Academic transcript**  (Sijill Akadimi)
- **Conduct certificate**  (Ifadat Suluk)
- **Graduation certificate**  (Ifadat Takharruj)

Print **1 colour copy and 2 black-and-white copies**  of each. Also have a copy of your **[button: iqama]** , your **student card**  and your **passport** .

**Downloading the three documents:**  log in to the university portal in Chrome, open **Nizam Akadimi** , then **Shakhsi**  (personal), then **al-Mustanadat ar-Rasmiyyah**  (official documents), and tap each blue link. If nothing downloads, go to Chrome's Settings → Site settings → Pop-ups, and allow them.

## B. Collect your degree and finish at the university

### 1. In the Student Affairs building

1. **Wihda 22, internal housing office:**  hand in the room handover form so the system shows your room is returned.
2. **Wihda 22, Student Relations (Qism al-Alaqat at-Tullabiyyah):**  return your student card. Keep copies or PDFs of your passport, iqama and student card first.
3. **[button: Idarat as-Safar] , left-hand window:**  ask for your **final exit visa (khuruj nihai)** . Give your student number; they check your room and card are returned. Hand in your iqama and collect your passport if you haven't already. **You need the final exit visa to receive your degree.**
4. **Ticket window (Tazkirah):**  say you're a graduate with a final exit visa and ask for the **ticket reservation letter**  (it's later submitted to Etimad). Bring copies of your student card, iqama and passport.

### 2. Collect your degree certificate

Go up to the 5th floor, cross into the Admissions building (Imadat al-Qubul), and go to the 4th floor. At the **Taslim Shahadat**  office, give your student number and sign for your certificate.

### 3. University stamps

At the **Wihdat al-Ikhtibarat**  office (3rd floor), get the university stamp on the black-and-white copies of your transcript, conduct certificate and, if they allow it, your graduation certificate.

### 4. Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)

If there's time before Dhuhr, go straight to the **MOFA branch in Sultanah, near Al Rajhi Bank** . See section C.

### 5. Graduate allowance (150 riyals)

At the **Al-Otair Centre near Markaz al-Buhuth** , before Dhuhr, bring your original degree certificate and copies of your passport, iqama and student card. Graduates are usually given 150 riyals. Ask for Shaykh Badr (this may change).

## C. Legalisation at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs

You need this before your embassy or consulate will legalise anything.

**Usually legalised:**  bachelor's degree, Ma'had certificate, transcript, conduct certificate and graduation certificate. Every document must already have the university stamp. Only photocopies are legalised, never originals.

1. Apply on the official MOFA website.
2. Enter how many documents you have.
3. Pay **30 riyals per document**  (agents outside the office charge about 40).
4. Get a queue number.
5. Hand in the payment receipt and the photocopies.

## D. Legalisation at your embassy or consulate

After MOFA, your own country's embassy or consulate may need to legalise the documents too. Check its website for what it requires.

The guide this comes from describes a consulate general in Jeddah. Bring the MOFA-legalised documents and copies of your iqama, student card and passport, and register online beforehand if your consulate asks you to (the guide mentions *Lapor Diri* , the Indonesian self-reporting system). It took about 40 minutes there. Don't leave while you wait, because names are called out. Open Sunday to Thursday, 8:30am to 5pm.

## E. Collect your graduation bisht and gifts

Bachelor's, master's and PhD graduates may receive a **graduation bisht (mishlah)**  and books or other gifts, while available.

Go to the **new building behind Wihda 11, 2nd floor** , office marked **Mudir al-Idarah** , and say you've come for your graduation bisht and gifts.

**Already gone home?**  Someone else can collect them for you. Send a request through **Nizam Tawasul**  with your name, student card number, graduation date, and the full name and student card number of the person collecting.

## F. Your ticket home

1. Go to **Idarat as-Safar**  with your passport, iqama and student card.
2. Finish everything else first, including embassy legalisation, before booking.
3. Hand in the authorisation letter, choose your return date and wait. If all is in order, the ticket is issued straight away.

**Business class upgrade:**  527 riyals extra, paid with a Saudi bank card, with 2 × 32 kg baggage.

## Already left without your certificate?

A brother offers two paid services for graduates who left before collecting or legalising their degree: collecting the certificate and handing it to someone visiting Madinah for you, and doing the full MOFA legalisation. WhatsApp messages only: **+966 56 820 2297** . MESH hasn't checked this service, so agree the details and costs with him first.

May Allah grant success to all graduates.

When you finish the Ma'had, three things need doing: one before the break and two after it.

## 1. Choose your college (before the break)

- Around the **middle of your final Ma'had semester** , go to [alisc.iu.edu.sa](https://alisc.iu.edu.sa/) and pick your first, second and third choice of college.
- The Ma'had group chats announce the deadline for this. Don't miss it.

> Tip
>
>
>
> If the page gives you trouble, log in to the main university portal first, then open the link. Use Chrome, in desktop mode.

## 2. Get your new university card (after the break)

Go to the **Deanship of Student Affairs** , 3rd floor, to the room that makes university cards. Bring:

- Your old university card
- A passport photo
- Your  [button: iqama]  number
- Your **new university number** . It's your old number with the last digit changed to **3** . For example, 437182921 or 437182922 in the Ma'had becomes 437182923 in the Kulliyah.

## 3. Keep your room (after the break)

The university system now lists you as a graduate, so you have to confirm you still live in your room.

1. Go to **إدارة السكن**  (the housing office) and get the paper that confirms which room you're in. It's the same paper you got when you first arrived.
2. Find the **[button: mushrif]**  (supervisor) of your building and check you're still in his system with the right iqama number and your **new**  university number.

# Postgraduate admission 1448: master's, PhD and diplomas

Who can apply to the Islamic University's postgraduate programmes, how to apply, the conditions, which programmes are free, and what the paid ones cost.

Updated 21 Oct 2026. Admission guide for postgraduate programmes 1448, Deanship of Graduate Studies, Islamic University of Madinah

[Share on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/?text=Postgraduate%20admission%201448%3A%20master%27s%2C%20PhD%20and%20diplomas%20(MESH)%0Ahttps%3A%2F%2Fmesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev%2Fgraduates%2Fpostgraduate-admission-1448%2F) [button: Save]

**5 parts** 5 min read

1. [How to apply](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/postgraduate-admission-1448/#how-to-apply)
2. [Who can apply](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/postgraduate-admission-1448/#who-can-apply)
3. [Fees](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/postgraduate-admission-1448/#fees)

5. [Contact the Deanship of Graduate Studies](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/graduates/postgraduate-admission-1448/#contact-the-deanship-of-graduate-studies)

This summarises the university's official admission guide for the academic year 1448. Programme-specific conditions (memorisation, previous specialism, minimum grades) are in the [full guide (PDF, Arabic)](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/assets/files/postgraduate-admission-guide-1448.pdf). Read the part for your programme before you apply.

## How to apply

- **From inside Saudi Arabia:**  through the university's online admission portal, when it opens.
- **From outside Saudi Arabia:**  through the **Minahi**  (منحتي) scholarship portal for postgraduate studies, as announced on the Deanship's X account.
- All programmes are taught at the main campus in Madinah.
- The university has **no offices or agents anywhere**  that take applications for it.
- **Application fee: SAR 300** , non-refundable, paid by e-invoice on the portal. Your application isn't considered and you can't sit the exam until it's paid.
- You apply yourself and are responsible for keeping your login safe, giving a working email and phone number (with promotional SMS not blocked), and following your application on the portal and the Deanship's X account.

> Important
>
>
>
> A message through any of these counts as official notice from the university: the Deanship's X account, the admission portal (public page or your own page), your official email, SMS, the admission WhatsApp, or the admission unit's email.

## Who can apply

Saudis; legal residents (internal scholarships); GCC citizens; and external scholarship students.

**General conditions to apply:**  a degree from an institution recognised by the Ministry of Education; no record of conduct that bars admission; the SAR 300 fee; all required documents uploaded and matching by the deadline; medically fit; and any extra conditions set by the department.

**Conditions to be accepted:**  pay the first semester's tuition (paid programmes) before enrolment; pass any supplementary courses the department sets; complete all admission documents by the deadline (otherwise the nomination is cancelled); and pass the department's specialist admission exam and interview, where there is one.

**Typical programme conditions**  (check yours in the PDF): a grade of at least *very good*  (جيد جدا) in your previous degree, a written specialist exam of at least 60 out of 100, and a personal interview. External scholarship applicants from outside Saudi Arabia who don't already hold a scholarship in the country are exempt from the written exam.

### About your qualifications

- **Non-Saudi with a qualification from abroad:**  final acceptance needs the Saudi embassy's attestation of your qualification. You can still apply and be nominated without it.
- **Not graduated yet:**  you're compared with other applicants on your grade and cumulative GPA at the time you apply. A later rise in your GPA isn't counted.
- The university awards the degree only. It takes no responsibility for your employment or promotion, and doesn't require your employer's approval (that's your responsibility).

## How to use it

> Important
>

> Always start the same way: upload the book, have the AI read **the entire book**  with `Chunkism`, then **update the instructions**  with the chunk schedule it gives you. Only then make the mind map.

### Setting up (both)

1. **Create a Project (Claude) or a Gem (Gemini)**  and paste the prompt above into its instructions.
2. **Upload the book**  (PDF).
3. **Type `Chunkism`.**  The AI reads the whole book and replies with a schedule: every chapter split into chunks, each with a start and end landmark.
4. **Update the instructions.**  Copy the schedule, paste it between `<permanent_schedule>` and `</permanent_schedule>`, and save.

### On Claude: the whole mind map in one go

1. Ask for **the entire mind map in one reply** , following the schedule from Chunk 1 to the last chunk, as one Markdown file.
2. Import the file into **Xmind**  (File → Import → Markdown). Headings become branches.

### On Gemini: chunk by chunk

1. **Type `Chunk 1`.**  It confirms what it's looking for (the *System Lock*  line), then gives you that chunk as a Markdown block. Then `Chunk 2`, `Chunk 3` and so on, one per message.
2. **If a chunk gets cut off** , type `Continue`. It picks up from the last line.
3. **Paste each chunk into Xmind**  as you go, so the map grows chunk by chunk.

## Three sizes for revision

Once you have the full map, use it as the base for two shorter ones:

- **Full:**  everything, for learning the material the first time
- **Medium:**  for revising in the weeks before the exam
- **Small:**  for the last look before you walk in

Ask, for example: *"Using the full mind map as the base, make a medium version and a small version. Scale each one down proportionally, so every chapter keeps its share, and keep the same headings and order."*  Import each into Xmind as its own map.

## Tips and tricks

- **Read the System Lock line.**  If the start or end landmark it's searching for is wrong, stop and correct it before it maps the wrong pages.
- **Definitions stay whole.**  The instructions forbid cutting definitions short, so if one looks clipped, ask it to redo that part.
- **Evidence is left out on purpose.**  Verses and hadiths only appear when they're a heading in the book. The map is for the rules and concepts.
- **On Gemini, start a new chat every few chunks**  if answers get slow or sloppy. The instructions and schedule stay saved, so just type the next chunk number.
- **Keep the .md files.**  The exam maker can use them to build practice exams from the same book.
- **Always check against the book.**  AI can get names, numbers and rulings wrong.

- Ask only about content inside this scope, as the source states it. Do not add outside knowledge, and skip passages where the text is garbled or unclear.

**Questions**
- [40] true/false and [40] multiple-choice (4 options each).
- Difficulty: hard.
- Before writing, scan the scope and list what this subject actually tests: definitions, classifications, conditions, steps, numbers, names linked to ideas, disputes and the preferred view. Spread the questions across every section in proportion to how much it covers, not just the first chapters.
- **Any other subject:** definitions, classifications, causes and effects, comparisons and sequences.
- True/false: many statements should be "almost right", with a view attributed to the wrong person or group, a ruling or definition slightly altered, an order swapped, or a number changed. Aim for roughly 40% true.
- Multiple choice: distractors must be real items from the same section (the other opinions, the neighboring term, the similar sect, the adjacent step), never obvious throwaways. Include some "which of these is NOT…" questions.
- Do not quote long evidence (verses or hadiths) as the question unless the material requires memorizing it.
- Shuffle the options in a fixed, seeded order so the correct letter is spread evenly across the four positions and stays the same on reload.
- Every question needs a short explanation from the source: why the answer is right and, where useful, whose view or which term the wrong choice actually belongs to.

**Behavior**
- No login or account features: a plain static page that anyone with the link can use. Do not declare any runtime capabilities.
- Show no answers or feedback until the user submits.
- If questions are unanswered at submit, show an in-page warning; a second click submits anyway.
- After submitting:
- show the total score, the percentage, a score for each section, the number wrong and the number unanswered
- mark each question correct, wrong or unanswered
- highlight the correct option and show the explanation
- Add a "show mistakes only" toggle and a "retake" button that clears everything.
- Save progress in browser storage as a convenience only, wrapped in try/catch.

**Presentation**
- Language: [Arabic (RTL, Arabic-Indic numerals ١، ٢، ٣) / English].
- Show a small topic tag (chapter or section) on each question.
- Put a fixed bottom bar with a progress count and the submit button.
- It must work well on a phone, in both light and dark mode.
- Give the page a title naming the subject and scope, e.g. "اختبار الفرق – الباب الأول".

**Sharing**
- Publish it as an artifact so I get a link. Remind me to set the link to "anyone with the link" in the Share menu.

Before publishing, check the question counts, the spread of correct letters, and that every section in the scope is represented.

- **Exclude:**  anything he left out, e.g. *the section on X, the introduction*

For written questions, add a line such as *"Add 5 short written questions with a model answer shown after submitting."*
- **Language:**  Arabic or English
3. **Save it in the project instructions** , so every practice exam you make for that subject matches what he asked for. Before the next exam, just change the chapters and question types.

## Tips and tricks

- **Take it like the real exam.**  Answers only show after you submit. Then use **show mistakes only**  to revise just what you got wrong.
- **Ask for a fresh exam**  on the same scope once you're scoring well, so you're not just memorising the answers.
- **Narrow it down**  before the exam: *"Make a new exam only from the sections I got wrong."*
- **Check explanations against the book.**  Each answer explains itself from the source, but AI can still make mistakes, especially with names, numbers and who held which view.
- **Progress is saved on your phone** , so you can stop and come back. **Retake**  clears everything.

# About MESH

MESH stands for Medina English Student Hub. It's a resource hub for English-speaking students in Madinah, especially at the Islamic University, and for their families.

It started as a Telegram group. Most people here don't use Telegram, so everything moved to this site, sorted by topic so you can find it without scrolling through old messages.

## Who runs it

A small team of students, in their spare time. Nothing here is official. Rules and dates change, so always confirm anything important with the university or the right government office, and check when a guide was last updated.

## Put MESH on your home screen

MESH works like an app: it opens from your home screen, and once you've visited, every guide opens even without signal (files like PDFs need signal the first time).

- **iPhone and iPad:**  open MESH in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen** .
- **Android:**  open MESH in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app**  or **Add to Home screen** .

## How to help

If you know something useful, a good place to eat, or spot a mistake, [send it in](https://mesh.obi2b-contact-alt.workers.dev/ask/?type=tip). Every message is read.
