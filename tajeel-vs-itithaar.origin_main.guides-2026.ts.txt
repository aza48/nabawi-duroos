Its time is not counted in your maximum study period. I'tithaar is an excuse from a semester you have already started: you are withdrawn from all its courses with a W grade, not a fail, but that semester does count towards your maximum study period. If you live in the halls, a ta'jeel is not approved until you hand back your room, and after an i'tithaar you move out once your status changes. With an iqama, one semester off means leaving on an exit and re-entry visa and returning a week before the next semester. More than one semester means final exit within two weeks.
