## How to use this plan
This file holds the task specs. Claims, owners, reviews, merges, hand-offs and wake-ups follow `docs/AI-LOG.md` (rules at the top, owner decisions, "Who owns what", "Takeovers"); where this plan differs, AI-LOG wins. Checks and house rules: `CONTRIBUTING.md`. One task, one branch, one PR, touching only the files the task lists. Branch `<agent>/<task-id>-<short-name>` from the latest `main`, and add tests for any new logic. Safe by design: no database migrations in this plan, no new paid services, and nothing is lost if a feature is switched off. When your lane is done, take a task from "Spare tasks" at the end of this file.

## Lane A: duroos and schedule (akheeai)

### A1. Event and Person structured data (S, high)
- **Why:** Google still shows Event results; FAQ results ended in May 2026. Lesson pages and shaykh pages have no structured data today.
- **Do:** `Event` JSON-LD on each lesson page: name, next occurrence, place with address, organiser, and `eventStatus` set to `EventCancelled` or `EventPostponed` when today's override says so. `Person` JSON-LD on each shaykh page. Reuse `src/components/JsonLd.tsx`.
- **Files:** `src/features/duroos/routes.tsx`, `src/features/duroos/DetailPages.tsx`, tests.
- **Done when:** Google's Rich Results Test passes on two lesson pages and one shaykh page (paste the HTML of a local build), and a cancelled dars shows `EventCancelled`.

### A2. "Now and next" strip on the duroos page (M, high)
- **Why:** this is the question people open the site to answer: what is on now, and what is next. Transit, FotMob and the Haram mosque app all lead with it.
- **Do:** a new component above the existing content of `/duroos` and `/ar/duroos`:
  - the dars in progress, with "25 min in" and a thin progress bar
  - the next prayer's duroos, with a countdown
  - one status word each: starting soon, live, cancelled, moved

  Use the phases `getToday` already computes. The component renders on the server, with no layout shift.
- **Files:** new `src/features/duroos/NowNext.tsx`, `src/features/duroos/routes.tsx`. The cards are not touched.
- **Done when:** checked at five times of day with fixed clocks in tests (before Fajr, during a dars, between prayers, after Isha, on a cancelled day). Layout shift is 0.

### A3. My duroos on the phone, no account (M, high)
- **Why:** people follow a few shuyukh or books, and the full schedule is long.
- **Do:**
  - a star on each lesson page and each schedule row, saved in `localStorage`
  - a "Mine" filter on the weekly schedule
  - a "Follow this shaykh for alerts" link that uses the existing push topics

  No server storage. Clearing the browser clears it.
- **Files:** new `src/features/duroos/useStarred.ts`, `src/features/duroos/ScheduleBrowser.tsx`, `src/features/duroos/DetailPages.tsx`.
- **Done when:** a star survives a reload, and "Mine" shows only starred duroos. The page still works with storage blocked (private mode).

### A4. "Did this not happen today?" on every dars (S, high)
- **Why:** the community spots a missed cancellation before anyone else, and today it has no quick way to say so.
- **Do:** a small link on the lesson page and under each live or finished dars. It opens WhatsApp to Admin (+1 234 567 9076) with "The [book] dars with [shaykh] did not happen today, [date]" and the page link filled in.
- **Files:** `src/features/duroos/DetailPages.tsx`, new `src/features/duroos/ReportLink.tsx`.
- **Done when:** the prefilled message is right in English and Arabic, and the link has a 40 px tap target.

### A5. Reminders inside the calendar feeds (S, medium)
- **Why:** subscribed calendars already show cancellations (`STATUS:CANCELLED` exists). A reminder makes the feed useful on its own.
- **Do:** add a `VALARM` 20 minutes before each dars in the `.ics` feeds, and none for cancelled ones.
- **Files:** `src/server/calendar.ts`, `tests/calendar.test.ts`.
- **Done when:** the feed validates (icalendar.org validator) and imports into Google and Apple Calendar with the alert.

## Lane B: content and assistant (aiakhee)

### B1. First 100 days (M, high)
- **Why:** every strong international student office frames settling in as a sequence. MESH has the guides but not the order.
- **Do:** a `/start` page with stages:
  - before you fly
  - arrival day
  - the first 72 hours
  - the first week, the first month, the first semester

  Each step is one line with a link to the existing guide, and a tick saved on the phone. Reuse the tick code from `GuideBody.tsx` by copying the pattern, not by editing that file. An optional arrival date turns steps into "do by" dates, shown in Hijri and Gregorian. Export the steps from a plain data module (`src/content/start.ts`) so D3 can use them.
- **Files:** new `src/app/(site)/start/page.tsx`, `src/content/start.ts` and its feature component. The Arabic twin comes after the owner's decision on Arabic content.
- **Done when:** every step links to a live guide (a test checks every slug exists), and the page works with no date entered.

### B2. Glossary of local terms (M, high)
- **Why:** second-language readers trip over iqama, jawazat, ta'jeel, i'tithaar, mukafaa, zaer, Nafath, Ejar and muqarrarat.
- **Do:** a `/glossary` page with about 40 terms: English meaning, Arabic spelling and a one-line use. In guides, the first mention of a term gets a dotted underline that opens a short definition. Build it in `enhanceGuideHtml`, with no client JavaScript beyond a `<details>` or a popover.
- **Files:** new `src/content/glossary.ts`, `src/app/(site)/glossary/page.tsx`, `src/lib/guide-html.ts`, tests.
- **Done when:** terms link only once per guide, never inside headings or links, and the page passes axe.

### B4. The exact spot of every dars (M, high)
- **Why:** "Nabawi" is not enough inside the Haram. A gate number and area saves a newcomer twenty minutes.
- **Do:** research the gate and area for every active dars and write one location line each, in the existing `locationEn` and `locationAr` style ("Western expansion, facing Bab Quba (gate 5)"). Deliver it as a table in `docs/research/dars-locations-2026-11.md` for the owner to apply in Admin. No code change.
- **Done when:** every active lesson has a sourced line, or is listed as unknown.

### B5. A short answer first on every FAQ (S, high)
- **Why:** help centres that work lead with one plain sentence, then the detail.
- **Do:** give every FAQ a first paragraph of at most 25 words that answers the question outright.
- **Files:** FAQ seed content.
- **Done when:** a test checks that every FAQ's first paragraph is at most 25 words.

### B6. Quran and hadith references become links (S, medium)
- **Why:** references such as "al-Baqarah 2:286" or "Bukhari 1" appear in guides and answers, and readers want the text.
- **Do:** in `enhanceGuideHtml`, turn recognised references into links to quran.com and sunnah.com, which open in a new tab. Never rewrite text inside quotes or headings.
- **Files:** `src/lib/guide-html.ts`, tests.
- **Done when:** a test covers ten reference formats in English and Arabic.

## Left out on purpose (and why)
- **Document wallet, budget calculator, trip checker:** the fees and rules change often and could not be confirmed; wrong numbers would do harm.
- **Mutun tracker, streaks, attendance:** nice, but they are not why people come to MESH.
- **Accounts, forums, a "submit a dars" form with a new table:** they need moderation and database changes, so they wait for the native app.
- **Newsletter and a WhatsApp Channel:** these are operations, not code. The owner can start a Channel at any time.
- **Rankings, partner offers, anything that features one business:** against the house rules.
