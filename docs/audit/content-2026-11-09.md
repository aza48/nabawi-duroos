# Content and copy audit, 9 November 2026

Read-only audit of the public text on medinahub.org: the seeded guides and FAQs (`src/server/seed/content/*.ts`), `src/content/*.ts`, interface copy in `src/features`, `src/components` and `src/app`, and the live database rows, pulled from `https://medinahub.org/api/v1/guides` and `/api/v1/faqs` on 8 November 2026 at about 22:40 UTC (32 guides, 39 FAQs). Checked against the house rules in `CLAUDE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the three research reports of 9 November in `docs/research/`.

Things to know before acting on this:

- **Nine live guides have no seed file.** `new-student-steps`, `tajeel-and-itithaar`, `university-calendar-1448`, `graduate-procedures`, `graduating-from-mahad`, `postgraduate-admission-1448`, `sisters-studying-in-madinah`, `childbirth-options` and `kids-activities` were imported once from `src/server/seed/legacy/old-mesh.md` and then edited live. They exist only in the database, and `content:sync` never touches them. They are cited below as "DB `slug`", with the seed line they came from. Fix them in Admin, or move them into a seed file first.
- **Two seeded guides differ from live.** The live `finding-an-apartment` and `everyday-apps-and-services` still have the old text. The seed was fixed but never synced (section 8). The visa lines that `docs/HANDOFF.md` lists as "not live" are in fact live.
- **The Arabic fixes in `6414c2d` are on `main` but not in production.** `/ar/mahad`, `/ar/library/sharia`, `/ar/library/search` and `/ar/guides/start-a-company-in-saudi` all return 404 live.
- **No em or en dashes** were found in public source or live data. The dash test is doing its job.
- Line numbers refer to the working tree at `ec2a8d8`.
