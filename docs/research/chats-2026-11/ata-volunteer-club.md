# c5: Ata' Volunteering Club group (نادي عطاء التطوعي لعام 1448هـ)

Extraction for MESH. No personal names, numbers or handles are recorded here. Event details are kept only where they show a pattern that repeats.

## 1. Summary

- **Date range:** 9 November 2025 to 9 November 2026 (Gregorian). Covers the second half of 1447 AH and the start of 1448 AH. The group was renamed for 1448 on 8 September 2026, so it is the same club group carried over from 1447.
- **Nature:** an announcement group, almost all in Arabic, run by two or three club organisers. About 110 real messages among roughly 1,430 lines; the rest is joins and security code notices. There are almost no member questions. Most posts are either (a) calls for volunteers with a WhatsApp sign-up group, or (b) forwarded invitations from the Deanship of Student Affairs and the University to attend events (talks, conferences, ceremonies, trips) with prizes and buses.
- **Members:** clearly mixed Saudi and international. Country codes seen include Sierra Leone, Bangladesh, Cameroon, Sri Lanka, Algeria, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Turkey and India alongside Saudi numbers.
- **Silent periods:** nothing from 18 May to 8 September 2026 (summer and the Hajj season), and very little in Ramadan 1447 beyond Umrah bus organising. The group shows nothing about Hajj season volunteering, exam period volunteering or orientation week volunteering.
- **Reliability:** high for how the club and the hours system work (stated repeatedly by the organisers and in a forwarded official notice). Medium for "recurring" claims, because this is one year of data; a pattern is marked recurring only where it is tied to a fixed international day, the academic calendar, or an annual University ceremony. Prize and cash details are one-off and should not be published.
- **Noise ignored:** one restaurant advert, one app competition promotion, research survey requests, and individual prize winners.

4. Who runs volunteering at Masjid an-Nabawi for University students (the General Authority for the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques' volunteering administration, or a University programme), and are non-Arabic speakers taken?
