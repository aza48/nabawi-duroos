## 2. Findings by MESH target

### scholarship-stipend-and-allowances

1. **Monthly stipend is 840 SAR** for a full scholarship student (Ma'had and college alike, as stated). A new student asked why the first payment was "way over 834", and the answer was the normal 840 SAR plus the set-up allowance.
   - Posted 30 Jan 2026. Senior member, unchallenged; consistent with arrival and graduation amounts below. Confidence: high.
   - NEW for the amount. MESH (`guides-research-2026-11.ts`, and FAQ `stipend-amount` in `guides-2026.ts`) says only "The rate is set by the University and depends on your college". Worth adding "840 SAR a month in 2026" if the owner is comfortable publishing a figure.
2. **Set-up allowance (بدل تجهيز, badal tajheez): 1,680 SAR**, paid once to new students on arrival with the first stipend, meant for setting up (fridge, microwave, carpet), but free to use as you wish.
   - 30 Jan 2026. Senior member. Confidence: high.
   - CONFIRMS MESH "arrival allowance of two months' stipend" (2 x 840 = 1,680) and adds the Arabic name and the figure.
3. **Books allowance (بدل كتب): 850 SAR a year**, for college (Kulliyah) students only, because colleges do not hand out books the way the Ma'had does.
   - 30 Jan 2026. Senior member. Confidence: medium to high.
   - NEW to the stipend guide. MESH mentions "a books allowance" only in passing in the postgraduate table (`guides-research-2026-11.ts` line ~1056), with no amount and not in the stipend guide.
4. **Excellence allowance (بدل تميز, badal tamayuz): 1,000 SAR**, for college students who score a semester GPA of 4.5 or more in two semesters in a row.
   - 30 Jan 2026. Senior member. Confidence: medium.
   - NEW.
5. **Graduation allowance (بدل تخرج): 2,520 SAR**, paid on graduating from the college, meant for shipping books and belongings home.
   - 30 Jan 2026. Senior member. Confidence: high.
   - CONFIRMS MESH "graduation allowance of three months' stipend" (3 x 840 = 2,520); adds the figure.
6. **Payment timing:** in this period the stipend landed around the 21st to 25th of each Gregorian month (24 Nov 2025 per a member's forecast; "dropped" by 25 Dec 2025; "beginning of the week" before 31 Jan 2026; "drops tonight" on 21 Feb 2026).
   - Several members agree. Confidence: medium.
   - CONFIRMS MESH that there is no fixed date; could add "usually in the last third of the month".
7. **After returning from ta'jeel, or after being reinstated following dismissal (مفصول), the stipend can lag.** A member returned two weeks before the payment date and was not paid; the office told another member it would come about three weeks later, roughly a week before the next regular payment.    - 16 Jan, 31 Jan, 21 Feb 2026. Several members, students' representative. Confidence: medium.
   - NEW (MESH says only that it stops during ta'jeel).
8. **The stipend is paid to the University card, an Al Rajhi stipend card** with the University as sponsor. If the card expires, the money stays in the account but cannot be withdrawn at the bank; you must get a new card. The bank cannot replace it without the University's permission.
   - 27 Nov 2025, 24 Dec 2025. Students' representative and senior member. Confidence: high for that period.
   - **CONFLICTS** with MESH, which says the stipend is paid into your own bank account via an IBAN request: "In the Academic System, open Personal, then Rewards, and send an IBAN update request" (`guides-research-2026-11.ts`, `scholarship-stipend-and-allowances`, also `new-student-steps` and `living-on-a-visitor-id`). The chat in late 2025 to early 2026 never mentions IBAN requests; everyone withdraws from the University card. Both may be true (a stipend card for most, an IBAN option for some, or a change for 1448). Needs official confirmation before MESH changes either way.
9. **Replacing an expired or faulty University stipend card:** at Student Affairs (شؤون الطلاب), 3rd floor (Nov 2025); later reported at the far right kiosk in the unit at building 22 (وحدة 22) (Jan and Feb 2026). You leave your name and details; the new card took up to about two weeks.
   - 27 Nov 2025, 21 Jan 2026, 3 Feb 2026. Students' representative and several members. Confidence: medium (location moved during the year).
   - NEW.

    - 7 Feb, 22 Feb 2026. Single members. Confidence: low.

### iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees (and FAQ student-iqama-renewal)

15. **Official notice, 9 Feb 2026: multiple exit and re-entry visas.** The University issued multiple exit and re-entry visas to students (issuing continuing):
    - Regular students (المنتظمين): valid until the start of the next academic year's first semester.
    - Expected graduates: valid until the end of Shawwal.
    - The visa lets the student travel during its validity, including official holidays, without visiting Travel Management or submitting a request each time.
    - Travelling without permission and without an official excuse may lead to academic dismissal.
    - Source: official notice, quoted in the chat. Confidence: high.
    - NEW. MESH describes Travel Management collecting lists for each break and does not mention the multiple visa. Members added: it showed in Absher, not always at once in the app; students whose iqama expired before the end date did not receive one and had to renew the iqama first (iqama needed to run to about November 2026); classes for 1448 start 9 Aug 2026, so returning later means unexcused absences.
16. **Winter break re-entry visa (before the multiple visa): the "student travel wish" portal.** Steps posted 30 Nov 2025:
    1. Open the University website, then Travel and Visas (السفر والتأشيرات).
    2. Sign in with your University login (the portal is travelmanagement.iu.edu.sa).
    3. Choose Student travel wish, exit and return (رغبة السفر خروج وعودة).
    4. Choose Advertisement (الإعلان) and the one item listed.
    5. Pick any city and enter your planned dates, then Create (إنشاء).
    - If you received a new University number (moving from the Ma'had to a college, or into a master's or PhD), fill in Basic Information (المعلومات الأساسية) again first.
    - The portal opened about 30 Nov 2025; dates could be chosen from 7 Dec to 3 Jan; visas were issued on 8 Dec 2025. You may leave once your exams end. If yours is not issued, go to Travel Management (إدارة السفر) on the next working day; an unpaid traffic fine blocks it.
    - Senior member and several members. Confidence: high for 1447. May be replaced by the multiple visa in future.
    - NEW (portal and steps); CONFIRMS that fines block the visa.
17. **Emergency trip during the semester (iqama holders):** send a Tawasul message to your college; see the dean, who raises the request to Student Affairs (شؤون الطلاب); then go to the secretary at Student Affairs (first door on the left) to have the visa issued. It can all be done in one day if you start early on a Sunday. The "completed" text usually comes first; Absher shows the visa within minutes to hours depending on when the secretary processes it.
    - 30 Jan, 8 Feb, 10 Feb 2026. Several members. Confidence: medium to high.
    - NEW.
18. **Checking a visa on the Absher website** when the app is slow: sign in, My services, Inquiries, Entry/exit visa status, enter iqama and passport number, submit.
    - 10 Feb 2026. Senior member, confirmed working by another. Confidence: high.
    - NEW.
19. **Iqama renewal through Tawasul (step by step):** Tawasul system (نظام تواصل), menu (three lines, top left), New request, scroll to Travel Management (إدارة السفر), Request type (نوع الطلب): Iqama renewal request (طلب تجديد الإقامة), fill the subject and details with your name, University number and iqama number.
    - Regular (منتظم) students are normally renewed automatically; students on ta'jeel must send the request; anyone not renewed a month before expiry should send it. Members advised sending it anyway before travel or ticket season. Family iqamas: send a Tawasul message as well.
    - Posted 20 Jan, 3 Feb, re-posted 12 Feb and 18 Feb 2026. Senior member, confirmed by several. Confidence: high.
    - CONFIRMS FAQ `student-iqama-renewal` (`guides-gaps-2026-11.ts`) and adds the exact menu path and request name.
20. **Physical iqama card:** still available but for a fee; the digital iqama in Absher is enough.
    - 20 Jan 2026. Senior member. Confidence: medium. NEW.
21. **Changing the iqama photo:** go to Travel Management and ask for its Jawazat representative (مندوب الجوازات), or go with a Student Affairs representative to Jawazat near the train station. One member said it can be done in Absher.
    - 15 Jan 2026. Students' representative and members. Confidence: low to medium. NEW.
22. **Visitor ID holders and travel:** a Visitor ID holder who pays for his own ticket can leave and return whenever he likes (confirmed by the students' representative by voice note).
    - 2 Feb 2026. Confidence: medium. Partly CONFLICTS in tone with the 9 Feb official notice (travel without permission risks dismissal during term). MESH (`living-on-a-visitor-id`) says to check Absher and follow the University's travel notice, which is the safer wording. Keep MESH as is.
