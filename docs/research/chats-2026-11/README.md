# Research from five WhatsApp chats (November 2026)

On 9 November 2026 the owner sent five WhatsApp chat exports. akheeai read every line of each one with a research pass. The files here keep only durable, useful facts. They contain no names, phone numbers or private handles.
- Every finding gives:
  - the date it was posted
  - the kind of source (an official notice forwarded, a representative, members confirming each other, or a single member)
  - a confidence level
  - whether it is NEW to MESH, CONFIRMS MESH, or CONFLICTS with a guide or FAQ
- The raw exports are not in the repo.

Public text never names these chats. Each fact is written in MESH's own words, and only with authority. Anything below medium confidence becomes a question for the admins.
