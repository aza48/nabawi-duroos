# Chat extraction: "US Students Ann" (c1)

## 1. Summary

- An announcements channel for American students at the Islamic University of Madinah, run by the students' nationality representative (mandoob) and his deputies; members rarely post.
- Covers 9 Nov 2025 to 6 Nov 2026 (about 1,500 lines, much of it system notices, deleted messages and images that did not export).
- Most useful content is official University notices forwarded by the representative (Travel Administration, Deanship of Student Affairs, Housing Administration, Deanship of Admission and Registration, the Scholarship Fund), often with his English summary.
- Reliability: forwarded Arabic notices are high confidence; the representative's own explanations are medium to high (he checked several directly with the Travel Administration); there is almost no member chatter, so few "member answer" items.
- Biggest themes: summer tickets (cities, batches, email), exit and re-entry visas, Visitor ID renewals, ta'jeel and handing back rooms, the 60 day visa extension rule, housing discipline, and allowances.

### etimad-account (and FAQ `student-ticket-rules`), also scholarship-stipend-and-allowances "Your ticket home"

1. **Summer tickets go only to cities the University picks for each country. For the USA that meant New York (JFK) or Washington DC (IAD) only.** The representative pushed back at the Travel Administration and was told it is a new rule for all countries: the University no longer flies students to any city they ask for, only to set cities per country. Students had to choose one of the two (or write that they do not want a ticket).
   Posted 16 Feb 2026, repeated 5 Apr, 6 Apr and 12 Apr 2026. Source: representative relaying the Travel Administration. Confidence: high.
   Status: CONFLICTS. `guides-2026.ts` (etimad-account), `guides-research-2026-11.ts` (scholarship-stipend-and-allowances) and the `student-ticket-rules` FAQ in `guides-student-life.ts` all say "a capital city only, in your own country or in the country that issued the visa". New York is not a capital. Suggest: "to a city the University sets for your country, usually the capital or the main international airport".

2. **How summer tickets actually arrive.** Tickets were released in batches between mid April and mid May (first on 15 to 16 Apr 2026, most by 14 May 2026). Students booked as a group (G class, groups over 10) get one PDF that is emailed to just one person in the group and holds everyone's tickets. Tickets go to the email address on your Etimad profile. Check spam and junk, search for "Saudia", and look back up to a month. Some tickets were handed over on paper. If you get a ticket that is not yours, pass it to your representative. The travel order (أمر إركاب) for group tickets may sit under the group leader's name, so not seeing one of your own on Etimad is not always a problem.
   Posted 15 Apr, 16 Apr, 23 Apr, 4 May, 6 May, 14 May 2026. Source: representative. Confidence: high.
   Status: NEW (MESH says the e-ticket "reaches you by email or through the University's ticket system"; it does not mention group PDFs, the Etimad email or searching spam).

3. **Getting a clean e-ticket from a group booking:** in the Saudia app open Assistant, then Change or cancel flight, enter the booking reference and last name, continue as if changing (no change is made), and on the Saudia website choose Download e-tickets.
   Posted 17 May 2026. Source: deputy representative. Confidence: medium to high.
   Status: NEW.

4. **Ticket clashes with exams:** undergraduate students whose ticket date clashes with their exams send a Tawasul request to the Travel Administration asking to change the date, attaching a copy of the ticket and of the exam timetable.
   Posted 19 Apr 2026. Source: official forward (Travel Administration). Confidence: high.
   Status: NEW. Partly CONFLICTS with the etimad-account "Common problems" row "Your summer ticket is a group booking: Group tickets cannot be changed. Travel on the date given" (`guides-2026.ts`).

5. **Saudia office on campus.** From 13 May 2026 a Saudia Airlines office operated inside the University during working hours, 7:30 am to 3:00 pm, for students changing travel dates, including those going for Hajj or with an exam clash, at the student's own cost. Students were told to use only this office and not Saudia branches in the city ("students are not allowed to visit Saudia branches outside the University").
   Posted 12 May and 14 May 2026. Source: official forward (Travel Administration) plus representative. Confidence: high.
   Status: NEW. Also relevant to hajj-as-a-student.

6. **Graduating students are not on the general summer ticket list** (their final ticket is separate), and students who were already sent a ticket or travel order separately are not on it either. Students missing from the list who wanted a summer ticket filled a form via the representative (29 Apr 2026). Ma'had students were on the list too.
   Posted 5 Apr and 29 Apr 2026. Source: representative. Confidence: medium to high.
   Status: CONFIRMS (final ticket is separate, etimad-account table).

7. **Example timing:** the first US group flew Madinah to JFK on 24 May 2026 (dep 3:40 am, arr 2:45 pm), three days after finals ended on 21 May.
   Posted 4 May 2026. Source: representative. Confidence: high. Status: NEW (useful as a "typical timing" note only).

8. **The Travel Administration does not issue tickets during the academic year.** Travel in term is at the student's own expense.
   Posted 13 Sep 2026. Source: official forward. Confidence: high.
   Status: CONFIRMS (tajeel-and-itithaar says flights during a semester off are at your own cost).

### iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees (and living-on-a-visitor-id "Travelling abroad")

10. **University-issued multiple exit and re-entry visas (spring 2026).** The University issued multiple exit and re-entry visas to its scholarship students without them applying: for regular students valid until the start of the first semester of the next academic year; for expected graduates until the end of Shawwal. During its validity a student can travel, including in official holidays, without visiting the Travel Administration or applying each time. The notice warned that travelling without permission and without an official excuse can lead to academic dismissal, and the Dean added that the visa is a convenience that depends on regular attendance.
    The representative clarified (10 Feb 2026) that the date on the visa message (10 May) is a "travel by" date, not "travel after"; you may travel as soon as the visa reaches your phone, travelling after it is fine as long as you return before the stated return date, and the same visa covers Ramadan and the summer.
    Posted 9 Feb, 10 Feb, 12 Feb 2026. Source: official forward plus representative (checked with the Travel Administration). Confidence: high.
    Status: NEW. MESH says Travel Management "collects the lists for the summer break, approved leave and ta'jeel" (`guides-2026.ts`).

11. **Exit and re-entry visas for 1448: apply online.** On 13 Sep 2026 the Travel Administration opened exit and re-entry visa requests for regular students for 1448. Requirements: no traffic or other violations; children and dependants already added; passport valid for one year. Apply on the University website, E-Services (الخدمات الإلكترونية), Travel Management System (نظام إدارة السفر). Requests are processed Sunday to Thursday in working hours. Travel is allowed in official holidays or with the approval of your college or institute; the student carries full responsibility for late return or missed classes and exams; travel is at the student's own cost.
    Posted 13 Sep 2026. Source: official forward with the deputy's English translation. Confidence: high.
    Status: NEW (MESH has no online path or requirement list for the student's own visa). Partly CONFIRMS the "fines block it" and passport rules.

12. **The 60 day rule for students abroad on ta'jeel or i'tithaar.** Deferred or excused students outside the Kingdom must send a Tawasul request to the Travel Administration to extend their visa within 60 days of its expiry, and send someone in Madinah to pay the fees and finish the paperwork. After 60 days the residence status changes to "exited and did not return" (خرج ولم يعد); the visa can no longer be extended and the student must apply for a new visa as a new student. The deputy added: go by day 59 at the latest.
    Posted 9 Sep 2026. Source: official forward (Travel Administration) plus representative. Confidence: high.
    Status: NEW and CONFLICTS in part: iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees (`guides-2026.ts`) says "An exit and re-entry visa that runs out while you are abroad leaves you outside with no way back on that iqama". The official notice says there is a 60 day window to extend through the Travel Administration.

13. **Mid-year travel requests.** For the 1447 mid-year break the Travel Administration took travel applications from regular external scholarship students through the Unified Student Portal from 9 to 15 Jumada al-Akhirah 1447 (30 Nov to 6 Dec 2025), for travel from the end of exams until 14 Rajab 1447. No requests after the deadline.
    Posted 16 Nov 2025, reminder 30 Nov 2025. Source: official forward plus representative. Confidence: high (for that year).
    Status: NEW.

On 29 Apr 2026 anyone whose Visitor ID had expired or was about to was told to go urgently to the office of the secretary of the Vice Dean of Admission (Deanship of Admission and Registration). Some visas were cancelled during spring 2026 and reissued through the representatives (31 Mar, 10 May 2026).
    Source: official forwards plus representative. Confidence: high (that it happened); medium (that it repeats each year in this form).

### tajeel-and-itithaar

18. **A ta'jeel request is not processed until you have officially handed back your room.** Inside the Kingdom you hand it back in person; outside the Kingdom you must appoint a fellow student to hand it back for you.
    Posted 4 Jan 2026. Repeated with name lists on 3 Sep 2026: students whose ta'jeel was still "under study" had to hand back the room or the ta'jeel would not go through; students already deferred but still in the halls had to vacate by the Monday deadline, after which the Deanship removes their belongings at their own risk.
    Source: official forward. Confidence: high.
    Status: CONFIRMS and strengthens. MESH (`guides-research-2026-11.ts`) says "Room in the halls: Hand it back and empty it" and "someone has to empty your room and return the key", but does not say the request is blocked until the handover.

19. **How to appoint someone to hand back your room (wakalah):** in Tawasul on the University website, New request, Housing Administration (إدارة الإسكان), subject such as "Authorisation to hand back housing". Write: "I, student [name], authorise student [name], University number [..], to hand back my room: Unit .., floor .., room .., bed ..". Print it and take it to Housing Administration reception in Unit 22, or to the Deanship of Student Affairs. They give a form; the unit supervisor (mushrif) signs it after checking the room is empty; take it back to Housing Administration in Unit 22, which enters it in the system. Once the system shows no room in the deferred student's name, the ta'jeel is approved.
    Posted 4 Sep 2026. Source: official forward. Confidence: high.
    Status: NEW.

20. **Re-enrolment for a closed file (مطوي القيد).** In Sep 2026 the Deanship of Admission and Registration announced the re-enrolment (إعادة الانتظام) window for the second semester of 1448. Steps (deputy): University website, Academic System (النظام الأكاديمي), menu, أكاديمي, ادخال حركات أكاديمية, ادخال حركة أكاديمية جديدة, أوافق, set نوع الحركة to اعادة قيد, choose the reason that fits, التالي, then send.
    Posted 15 Sep 2026. Source: official forward plus representative. Confidence: high.
    Status: dates CONFIRM `src/content/key-dates.ts` (reenrol-1, 6 Sep to 22 Oct 2026). Steps are NEW. Then apply for a new visa on Minhati if outside the Kingdom (item 17).
