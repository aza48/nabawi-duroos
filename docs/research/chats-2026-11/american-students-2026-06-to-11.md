## 2. Findings by MESH target

### graduating-from-mahad (Moving from the Ma'had to the Kulliyah)

1. **Setting the password for the new University number.** Ma'had graduates get an email with a new University number but often no password. What worked: on the University website enter the new number, click "forgot password", confirm through Nafath and your phone number, then choose which account to reset (both the old and the new number are listed), pick the new one and set a password from scratch. Old passwords and the iqama number do not work. Posted 10 Aug 2026. Several members agree (at least five students had the problem). Confidence: high. NEW (the guide says "You receive a new University number" but nothing on the password).
2. **Nafath reset fails from abroad.** The Nafath step failed for every student trying from outside Saudi Arabia; it worked when done from inside the Kingdom or on the campus Wi-Fi. Students abroad either email IT at it.care@iu.edu.sa or wait until they arrive. Posted 7 to 10 Aug 2026. Several members agree. Confidence: medium. NEW.
3. **The IT office** that sorts out passwords is behind Wihda (building) 18. Posted 9 Aug 2026. Single member (students' representative). Confidence: medium. NEW.
4. **Your Nafath may show two IDs.** After the move, log in to Nafath to check whether the new Kulliyah number appears beside the old one; if it does, everything (including ta'jeel) works normally with the new number. Posted 1 Jul 2026. Students' representative. Confidence: medium. NEW.
5. **New card for Ma'had graduates:** get it at Wihda 22 or at the Deanship of Student Affairs (shu'un at-tullab), not at the new-student orientation. Confidence: medium. CONFIRMS and adds detail (the guide lists "a passport photo" and "the student card office").
6. **Stipend after the move can be late.** One Ma'had graduate had no stipend by 27 Aug 2026. Single member. Confidence: low. Adds nuance to the guide's "Your stipend continues into the Kulliyah".
7. **Ma'had timetables come out later than Kulliyah ones.** All Kulliyah students had timetables by 19 Aug 2026; Ma'had students were still waiting. Students' representative. Confidence: medium. NEW.

### sim-internet-bank and NEW FAQ: banking as a US citizen

53. **FATCA checks for Americans.** Saudi banks flag US citizens and ask for a US tax number (SSN). Al Rajhi asks you to enter your SSN when it flags the account; once entered it works. Al Ahli (SNB) was reported as not opening accounts for Americans, or only with the SSN, iqama, National Address and a University letter (ifadah), and only after a manager approved it; an existing quick-pay account there had to be closed first. Members found Alinma and Riyad Bank simpler, though Alinma wants a physical iqama copy for large transfers (around 5,000 SAR and up). Posted 2 Sep and 15 to 21 Sep 2026. Several members agree. Confidence: medium. NEW.

Posted 2 Sep 2026. Single member, echoed by the question about the old University banking (18 Aug 2026). Confidence: medium. NEW.

### NEW FAQ: renewing a US passport in Saudi Arabia

65. Adults renewing a routine US passport do not need to travel: follow the consulate's instructions online, pay online, post the papers, and the new passport comes back by courier (SMSA). Others (first renewal after 18, children, newborns) book an appointment at the US Consulate in Jeddah (or Dhahran/Khobar) through evisaforms.state.gov; it took about 2 to 3 weeks and the passport was delivered to the nearest SPL office. For a newborn abroad, contact the US embassy in the country of birth. Posted 18 Aug, 6 Oct and 14 Jul 2026. Three members and the representative. Confidence: medium. NEW for American readers; consistent with MESH's general passport FAQ.

## 3. Recurring yearly patterns

- **Stipend:** usually lands around the 23rd to 24th of the month (24 Jun, 26 Jul 2026), sometimes earlier; the Kulliyah books allowance comes once a semester, at the start or middle (mid-October in 2026).
- **Summer:** Travel Management stays open; Ma'had graduates receive their new University number by email in late July to early August and many cannot set a password until they are back in the Kingdom; flights booked by the University move several times.
- **Start of year (mid-August):** ta'jeel window closes as the semester begins; Kulliyah timetables appear about a week before classes, Ma'had ones later; new-student orientation hands out the student card and the bank card; the mat'am may open late when the catering contract changes; free beds go to new students only.
- **Semester travel visas (1448 onwards):** the University issues exit and re-entry visas in blocks with fixed end dates (10 Oct, 7 Jan, then one for the winter break, Ramadan and summer); applications open a few weeks before each block ends.
- **Calendar 1448:** no mid-semester break in semester 1; end-of-semester break 15 to 19 Dec; new winter break 8 to 16 Jan; year compressed to finish before Hajj; i'tithaar and course withdrawal deadline about 12 weeks into the semester (5 Nov 2026).
- **Monthly:** book the mat'am before each month starts.
- **Autumn:** SPL and couriers run shipping offers; graduates look for temporary rooms in Wihda 5 while waiting for final exit.
