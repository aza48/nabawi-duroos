# American Students in IUM: extraction, part A (lines 1 to 2000)

## 1. Summary

- **Lines:** 1 to 2000 of `_chat.txt`.
- **Dates:** the file opens with the group's creation notice dated 27 Jan 2016, then jumps straight to 1 Jun 2025. Everything of substance in this range runs from **1 Jun 2025 to 12 Nov 2025**, so it is recent, and nothing here needs marking "old" purely by age. - **What the conversation covered:** the 2025 Study in Saudi window and its crashes; ta'jeel and i'tithaar in August 2025 (window, rejections, a new one-ta'jeel rule for Ma'had students, extending the exit and re-entry visa while away, rooms of students on ta'jeel); the 1447 calendar; changing college; the mat'am under a new operator; gym and library hours; getting a printed iqama card; money (exchange rates, moving dollars into a Saudi account, ATM limits); renting outside (how to find flats, basement flats, rents); the Medical Center and dental care; driving licence medical check; parking at the Haram; Saudi Post deliveries; visit visas for American family members. ## 2. Findings by MESH target

### tajeel-and-itithaar (guides-research-2026-11.ts)

1. **Extend the exit and re-entry visa within 59 days if you take ta'jeel while away.** A forwarded notice for "students who requested ta'jeel while in their home country": they must extend their exit and re-entry visa within 59 days of its expiry, by sending a fellow student to Travel Management (إدارة السفر) to pay the extension fee and have it extended. If it is not extended within 59 days, the student's status becomes "exited and did not return" (خرج ولم يعد) and he must apply for a new visa like a new student; without a new visa he cannot come back to the University. Posted 25 Aug 2025. Source: official notice (forwarded by a member). Confidence: high. **NEW.** MESH's "Travel and your visa" table only says "Return on a valid exit and re-entry visa, or follow Travel Management's instructions."
2. **Cost of that extension:** 200 SAR for each month, plus a one-time 100 SAR fee. Posted 28 Aug 2025. Source: two members agree. Confidence: medium. **CONFIRMS** iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees ("If you extend while you are already abroad, the extra months cost double: 200 SAR a month on a single visa").
3. **Ma'had students get only one ta'jeel.** A Ma'had student's request was refused with the system message "تم رفض الطلب لاستنفاد جميع الفرص المتاحة بناء على اللوائح والأنظمة التي تتيح للطالب فرصة واحدة فقط" (refused because all chances are used up; the regulations allow the student one chance only), and the member was told by the Ma'had's dean that this is the rule from now on. Posted 20 Aug 2025. Source: single member quoting the official system message. Confidence: medium. **CONFLICTS / refines:** MESH says "Ta'jeel and i'tithaar added together cannot be more than three semesters over your whole programme." Add a line that the Ma'had applies its own, tighter limit of one ta'jeel.
4. **Ta'jeel requests can be refused.** Several requests were refused in August 2025; members guessed the cause was a shortage of rooms (speculation, do not publish). Advice given: speak to the dean (عميد) or wakeel (وكيل) of your college, or the Ma'had. Posted 19 Aug 2025. Source: several members. Confidence: medium. **NEW** (MESH says approval is needed but not what to do after a refusal).
5. **Where to see the result:** the same Academic System page where you apply has an option to show the result. Posted 21 Aug 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: high. **CONFIRMS** ("choose إظهار الحركات الأكاديمية").
6. **1447 window (history):** ta'jeel for semester 1, 1447 opened 17 Aug 2025 and closed 23 Aug 2025; the calendar showed i'tithaar opening on 24 Aug 2025 but it was not visible in the system the next morning. Posted 15 to 25 Aug 2025. Source: several members. Confidence: high. Use as a pattern: the ta'jeel window is the week before classes. **CONFIRMS** the pattern behind MESH's 1448 windows.
7. **The step-by-step PDF "How to take Ta'jeel and i'tithaar v.4 (August 2023)"** that circulates among students was still accurate in August 2025 ("nothing really changed"). Posted 22 Aug 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: medium. **CONFIRMS** MESH's step list.
8. **Your room while on ta'jeel is not guaranteed, and practice varies.** A building mushrif came to check whether a roommate on ta'jeel was still in the room (27 Aug 2025). One member reported a group of students on ta'jeel being taken by campus security and made to sign that they would give up the room and leave the Kingdom (7 Sep 2025); others on ta'jeel or i'tithaar for one or two semesters said nobody approached them and their room was untouched. Source: several members, mixed. Confidence: medium. **CONFIRMS** MESH ("Room in the halls: Hand it back and empty it"). Worth adding: "Students who stay in the halls during ta'jeel risk being asked to leave the room and the Kingdom."
9. **First ta'jeel on a Visitor ID:** request a new entry visa when you come back. A member said two semesters away may mean starting again like a new student. Posted 4 Aug 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: low to medium. **CONFIRMS** the "Coming back" row; the two-semester point needs confirming.
10. **Withdrawing from a single course** (اعتذار عن مقرر): Academic System, then أكاديمي, then اعتذار عن مقرر. It needs approval and does not affect the GPA. انسحاب means withdrawing from the University. Posted 28 to 29 Oct 2025. Source: students' representative and a member. Confidence: high. **CONFIRMS.**

15. **You may fly home as soon as your last exam is over:** the exit and re-entry visas are issued before exams end. But do not book an early flight if you might need a resit. Posted 12 Oct 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: medium. **NEW.**
16. **When you must be back:** by 11:59 pm Saudi time on the last day your visa shows in Absher. Posted 27 Jul 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: high. **NEW** as a plain rule.

18. **Courses offered and teachers:** Academic System, Electronic Registration, "المقررات المطروحة وفق الخطة". If the timetable change page errors, refresh and keep checking because seats free up. Posted 22 and 25 Aug 2025. Source: two members. Confidence: medium. **NEW.**

### finding-an-apartment / cost-of-living (guides-2026.ts)

55. **How flats are really found:** pick a building in the area you like and go after Asr, when the building supervisors (haris) sit outside, and ask each one; most empty flats are never listed online, and members found real-estate offices of little help. Taking over a graduate's flat and furniture is the other common route. Posted 8 Sep 2025. Source: several members. Confidence: high. **CONFIRMS and adds** to "Where to look".
56. **Basement flats (بدروم):** below ground floor. Cheaper to cool and lower air-conditioning bills; but little daylight, risk of leaks after rain, damp and mould, insects in older buildings and ants in summer. Top floors have the highest AC bills. Posted 9 Sep 2025. Source: several members. Confidence: high. **NEW.**
57. **Rents seen (Aug to Sep 2025):** a 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom ground-floor flat in Hayy ad-Difa' (about 10 minutes' drive from the University), 23,000 SAR a year, payable in two halves; a 3-bedroom flat in Al-Faisaliyah next to the University, 25,000 SAR a year plus a service fee; an en-suite room in Al-Anabis, 600 SAR a month. Source: listings in the group. Confidence: medium. **CONFIRMS** cost-of-living (three or four bedrooms 3,000 to 4,500 furnished, empty 40 to 50% cheaper).
58. **Rabwa and Al-Aridh:** a member said Al-Aridh is the official district name and Rabwa its everyday name. Posted 1 Oct 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: low. Confirm before using.

### sim-internet-bank / send-money-home FAQ / everyday-apps-and-services

59. **Exchange rate for cash dollars:** banks and exchange shops near the Haram gave about 3.73 to 3.74 SAR per dollar for clean new $100 notes (Sep to Nov 2025); the rate moves in the visiting season. Older $100 notes get a slightly lower rate at most places; a few exchange shops take older notes and sometimes $20s. Source: several members. Confidence: high. **NEW** (MESH says only that the riyal is fixed at 3.75).
60. **Moving dollars from a US account into a Saudi account:** members' route was US card to urpay, then urpay to Mobily Pay, then to their Saudi bank, because urpay to a bank costs 5.75 SAR and Mobily Pay to a bank about 0.58 SAR; adding money through Apple Cash first avoided card top-up fees for some; one member said Barq charges nothing. A US debit card with no foreign transaction fees (tell the bank before travelling) avoids card charges. Posted 1 to 28 Sep 2025. Source: several members. Confidence: medium. **CONFIRMS** the app list in send-money-home and adds the fee detail. Keep impartial: list the routes, not a winner.
61. **Daily cash withdrawal limit:** 5,000 SAR a day from a Saudi bank card at that bank's own ATM. Posted 13 Oct 2025. Source: students' representative. Confidence: medium. **NEW.**
62. **Western Union pickup:** take your iqama; staff sometimes ask for it. Posted 2 Jun 2025. Source: single member. Confidence: medium.
63. **Absher kiosk** to change your Absher mobile number: one in a mobile operator's shop on Sultanah Road (closest to the University, sometimes under maintenance), and in malls such as Al-Noor and Al-Manar. Posted 8 Sep and 7 Oct 2025. Source: several members. Confidence: medium. **CONFIRMS** change-absher-mobile; a member said the kiosk is required, but MESH's newer guide lists app methods too, so keep MESH.
