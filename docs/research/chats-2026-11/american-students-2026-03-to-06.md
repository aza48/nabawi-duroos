# American Students in IUM chat: extract C

## 1. Summary

- **Lines:** 4001 to 6000 of `chats3/am/_chat.txt`.
- **Dates:** 18 Mar 2026 to 10 Jun 2026. That covers the end of Ramadan 1447, Eid al-Fitr, the second semester of 1447 (i'tithaar deadline, finals, results), the first summer under the new 2026 ticket rules, Dhul Hijjah and Hajj 1447, and graduation for batch 62.
- **Main topics:** the new summer ticket system (tickets sent by Saudia email to one student per group, Etimad irkab numbers, no change of destination, the 3 month validity, cancelling, the round trip), visas for students on ta'jeel or i'tithaar and for new students, Muqeem and Absher visa checks, renewing a passport, the stipend date and the stipend kiosk, exam timetables and the semester dates, moving from the Ma'had to the Kulliyah, graduation invitations and the bisht, families (istiqdam closed, a visit-visa wife cannot use the University hospital), zamzam on flights, scooters on campus, and Hajj in the semester you graduate.
- **Noise:** about half the range is sales posts, money-exchange requests (riyals for Zelle or Cash App), job adverts, a private death notice, a missing-child notice and stickers. All left out.
- **Reliability:** most answers come from 3 or 4 regular members, and the students' representative (the US mandoob) answers ticket and visa questions. There are no official notices as text, only references to images (a ticket rules sheet, a scooter notice, a Ma'had graduation PDF, a medical center notice), so the content of those images is known only from what members said about them. Everything is 2026, so it is current. Treat single-member claims as medium or low.

## 2. Findings by MESH target

### etimad-account (Your University ticket) and scholarship-stipend-and-allowances

1. **Tickets come by email from Saudia to one student per group.**
   - **Posted:** 16 Apr 2026 and 19 Apr 2026.
   - **Detail:** In 2026 the yearly tickets were issued as group bookings. The e-ticket was emailed from Saudia (members cite info@saudia.com) to one student in each group, not to every passenger. Everyone was told to search their email, including spam, for "flight confirmation". Some students received only their own ticket, and some received a group of 4. Tickets were released in batches: a first batch in mid April and a second batch in early May. Members kept asking about the second batch until 6 May.
   - **Source:** several members agree, and the students' representative confirmed.
   - **Confidence:** high.
   - **Status:** NEW. MESH (guides-2026.ts) says only "it reaches you by email or through the University's ticket system".

2. **Group tickets are for bachelor's students only.**
   - **Posted:** 19 Apr 2026.
   - **Detail:** Master's and PhD students get individual tickets.
   - **Source:** single member, a frequent answerer.
   - **Confidence:** medium.
   - **Status:** NEW.

3. **You cannot look up a group ticket with your own irkab number.**
   - **Posted:** 16 Apr 2026.
   - **Detail:** A student who called Saudia's government tickets department could not retrieve his ticket with his passport number, name and destination. The airline needs the Etimad irkab (travel order) number. Your own irkab number can be seen on Etimad, but on a group ticket the booking sits under the group leader's order, so your own number may not find it. A student who made a new Etimad account with a new iqama could not see his number.
   - **Source:** several members.
   - **Confidence:** medium.
   - **Status:** NEW. This is useful for the "Common problems" table in etimad-account.

4. **Changing the phone number or email on Etimad.**
   - **Posted:** 22 Apr 2026.
   - **Detail:** From the main Etimad page (not the one under أوامر الإركاب), click Edit profile and scroll to the bottom to change your email and phone number.
   - **Source:** single member.
   - **Confidence:** medium.
   - **Status:** NEW.

5. **The 2026 rules confirmed by members.**
   - **Posted:** 28 Mar 2026 to 2 May 2026.
   - **Detail:** The date or time can be changed, with the student paying any price difference (members quote "point 4" of the official rules sheet). The destination cannot be changed. There is no leftover credit and no turning the ticket into a balance for a family member: "it all goes back to the University". The ticket must be used within 3 months of the date it was booked for, so it cannot be kept for the winter break. Even under the old system a ticket could not be turned into airline points without flying. Under the old system (2025 and before) the date, time and destination could be changed, and an unused ticket's value could be put towards another ticket or a family member's ticket. One member's unused old-system return ticket lasted 11 months.
   - **Source:** several members agree.
   - **Confidence:** high.
   - **Status:** CONFIRMS guides-2026.ts rules 2, 5, 7 and 9, and guides-research-2026-11.ts.

6. **New students do not get a summer ticket automatically.**
   - **Posted:** 31 Mar 2026, 7 Apr 2026 and 22 Apr 2026.
   - **Detail:** The ticket and its dates were described as "automatic". But students who missed the sign-up form sent months earlier asked whether they could still get a ticket, and the representative said only those who had applied on Etimad should expect a ticket email. In 2026 the University set the dates: the return was booked for 19 Aug 2026 for everyone in a group.
   - **Source:** several members, and the students' representative.
   - **Confidence:** medium.
   - **Status:** CONFLICTS in part. etimad-account (guides-2026.ts) says: "Give Travel Management your dates and city when it collects the travel lists. Choose a date you can really keep." In 2026 the request was an earlier form or Etimad application, and the University chose the dates. MESH should say the ticket must be requested when the University asks (months ahead), and that the dates may be set by the University.

    - **Source:** the students' representative and another member.
    - **Confidence:** medium.
    - **Status:** Partly CONFIRMS guides-2026.ts ("Unused student tickets are cancelled by Travel Management... best cancelled early"). Adds that cancelling is done in person and that there is no penalty.

### iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees, living-on-a-visitor-id and phone-sims-and-absher

14. **Checking an exit and re-entry visa that does not show in Absher.**
    - **Posted:** 30 Mar 2026 and 11 May 2026.
    - **Detail:** Two routes, both from members:
      - On Muqeem at vv.muqeem.sa: make an account, enter your iqama number and passport number, verify, and it shows whether you have a visa.
      - On the Absher website (not the app): My Services, then Inquiries, then Entry/exit visa status. Enter your iqama and passport numbers and press submit.
    - **Also:** the Absher app has a glitch where a valid visa sometimes does not show. One member was asked for his visa details at the airport and gave them from Muqeem.
    - **Source:** several members.
    - **Confidence:** high.
    - **Status:** NEW.

    - **Posted:** 3 Apr 2026 and 12 Apr 2026.
    - **Detail:** Some students received a message that their visa's "last travel date" was 17 Apr 2026, or that it had been cancelled. The representative said to ignore the message: the visa is reissued for the summer and students could still travel after 17 Apr.
    - **Source:** the students' representative and several members.
    - **Confidence:** medium.
    - **Status:** NEW. This is a 2026 one-off but useful as a reassurance FAQ.

19. **Renewing a US passport while studying.**
    - **Posted:** 31 Mar 2026 and 16 May 2026.
    - **Detail:** Renew through the US embassy or consulate (members point to Jeddah). ## 3. Recurring yearly patterns

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Ramadan | The stipend is paid early, a few days before Eid al-Fitr, instead of around the 23rd to 25th. |
| Eid breaks | The University Medical Center and many offices close; use King Fahd Hospital emergency. Offices reopen a few days into the week after Eid. |
| Mid to late March (second semester) | The i'tithaar deadline. In 2026 it fell on 28 Mar, and the website struggled on the last night. |
| Late March to early April | The visa application window on Minhati for new students and for students returning from ta'jeel or i'tithaar. In 2026 one week was left on 30 Mar. Minhati opens only from a Saudi IP. |
| Early April | The cultural fair (Mahrajan), and the guidance evening for Ma'had level 4 students choosing a college. |
| Shawwal (April in 2026) | The University Career Day at Qa'at as-Salam, then an alumni forum. |
| Mid to late April | Graduates collect the bisht, sash and invitation card. Request 2 family invitations from the Deanship of Admission and Registration. |
| Mid April to early May | Summer tickets released in batches by Saudia email, to one student per group. Check spam for "flight confirmation". |
| About 3 to 4 weeks before finals | The final exam timetable appears in the Academic System (19 Apr in 2026). |
| May | Second semester finals, which move earlier each year with Hajj: about 6 to 21 May in 2026, from 25 Apr in 2027 per MESH. Ma'had results; the move to the Kulliyah is automatic. |
| May | The Study in Saudi application deadlines for the next intake (preferences about 20 May, submission 31 May in 2026). |
| Dhul Hijjah | Hajj registration is closed by mid May. Graduates can still do Hajj in their final semester. |
| Summer | The ELP summer English camp needs native-speaker helpers. Flat takeovers and furniture sales peak. |
| August | The new year's start often moves later than the first announced date (9 Aug announced, 16 or 23 Aug likely, in 2026). Return tickets were booked for 19 Aug. |
| Monthly | The stipend is paid around the 23rd to 25th. |
| About a week before each semester | The ta'jeel request window opens. |

## 5. Top 10 additions for MESH, ranked

1. **etimad-account, how the summer ticket arrives:** a group booking emailed by Saudia to one student per group; search all your email and spam for "flight confirmation"; tickets come in batches from mid April; bachelor's students only, as master's and PhD tickets are individual; it is a round trip, so do not book your own return.
2. **Fix the "capital only" line in etimad-account and scholarship-stipend-and-allowances:** US students fly to JFK or DC. Add that the onward domestic flight is separate and its bags are paid by you.
3. **Fix the istiqdam-deposit FAQ:** as of May 2026, istiqdam has been closed for years and the University does not help with visit visas. A wife on a visit visa cannot use the University hospital.
4. **New FAQ, "My exit and re-entry visa does not show in Absher":** check on vv.muqeem.sa, or on the Absher website under My Services, then Inquiries, then Entry/exit visa status. Keep the details ready for the airport.
5. **New FAQ, "My stipend did not arrive or my card expired":** the usual date is the 23rd to 25th, paid early in Ramadan. Go to Building 22, ground floor, kiosk on the far right. A replacement card takes about 1.5 weeks and the balance carries over.
6. **tajeel-and-itithaar:** add that Minhati and the visa page open only from inside Saudi Arabia (a Saudi IP), and that the return visa window closes around early April.
7. **graduate-procedures:** add 2 family invitations from the Deanship of Admission and Registration, the bisht, sash and invitation card collected in April before finals, and that Hajj is possible in your graduating semester.
8. **graduating-from-mahad:** the move is automatic; the new University number comes by email at the start of the next semester; get the password at the IT (تقنية) building; attend the level 4 college guidance evening.
9. **Duroos pages:** link the official schedule at prh.gov.sa (المسجد النبوي, dourous-madina).
10. **New short FAQ, "Can I take zamzam home?":** one container per passport on Saudia. Several US domestic airlines carry it free; ask at check-in.
