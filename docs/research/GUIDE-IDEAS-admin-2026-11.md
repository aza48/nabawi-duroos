# Guide ideas for the owner (admin agent, November 2026)

A running list of guides MESH could add or deepen, kept by the admin agent while it reads the IU students' Telegram forum (2015 to 9 Nov 2026) and gomedine.com. Each idea says where the material comes from and what it fills. Ranked within each group by how many readers it helps. Ideas marked **(building)** are being written now; **(done)** links the slug.

Sources: `docs/research/telegram-iu-2026-11/` (TG) and `docs/research/gomedine-2026-11/` (GM). Existing slugs are in `src/server/seed/content/guides-*.ts`.

## A. New guides
1. **Registering your courses each semester: add, drop and withdraw.** The registration windows, how the Academic System shows open sections, prerequisites, adding a course you failed, dropping without a fail, and what a withdrawal does to your record. Students ask this every January and August. (TG)
2. **Your GPA, warnings and dismissal.** How the grade points work, what an academic warning is, how many warnings lead to dismissal, how to raise a GPA, and honours. (TG)
3. **Changing your college or major.** The window, the conditions (GPA, places), who approves, and what transfers with you. (TG)
4. **Make-up and replacement exams.** Who qualifies for the replacement exam (اختبار بديل), the accepted excuses, where the list of accepted excuses is posted, and the dates. Complements `missed-a-final-exam`. (TG)
5. **Volunteering as a student.** nvg.gov.sa, the Skills Record, clubs. (already in akhiai's task list from the WhatsApp chats; TG adds detail)
6. **Using Tawasul (تواصل), the University's request system.** Every request it handles, how to write one, how long replies take, and how to follow up. (TG)
7. **The University email and Microsoft 365.** Activating it, installing Office free, and the phishing to watch for. (TG)

8. **Your first semester, week by week.** The printed and Tawakkalna ID (Unit 22), reading your fixed first-year timetable (no add or drop in semester one), the first stipend (nothing in month one, double in month two), the University stipend card and moving money off it with the Barq app. (TG c1)
9. **Getting your stipend off the University card.** The card takes no deposits; how students move the money out, cash withdrawal, and what to do if the card is lost (Unit 22). (TG c1)
10. **Getting from the University to the Haram.** The University buses (times, no run before Maghrib), shared taxis from Gate 4 to Bab as-Salam, city bus 150 from the Taibah University B stop. (TG c1; also GM)
11. **Unit 13 and paid University housing.** Who it is for (Saudis, internal and partial scholarships), the price per semester, how to renew. (TG c1)
12. **The Student Fund.** One loan of up to 2,000 SAR repaid at 200 a month, not in the final year, and what else it does. FAQ or section. (TG c1)
13. **Honours and how retakes count.** Any fail, including DN for absence, rules out honours; both grades stay in the GPA. FAQ. (TG c1)
14. **What you can keep in your University room.** The safety inspections: no gas cylinders, cooking appliances or loose wiring; books for shipping go to University storage. (TG c7, to confirm against today's rules)
15. **Ijazah and sama' sessions at Masjid an-Nabawi.** Who runs them, where men and women sit, what to bring. (TG c7, check it still runs)
16. **Maximum study period and exceptional chances.** What happens when you run out of semesters, and how the Academic Committee grants extra ones. (TG c7)
17. **Class suspensions for weather and where they are announced.** (TG c7)
18. **Lost your iqama, Visitor ID, passport or student card.** Steps for each. (TG c7, c1)
19. **If a student passes away.** What the University and the family do; burial in al-Baqi'. A sensitive but much-needed page. (TG c7)

More ideas are added below as the readers finish.
