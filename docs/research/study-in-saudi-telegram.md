# Study in Saudi Arabia | Madi Abu Yahya (t.me/notjustmadinah): what it tells MESH

Researched 7 November 2026 from the scraped channel text (745 posts, #1 on 12 Feb 2022 to #1086 on 6 Nov 2026). Written in our own words. No dashes used as punctuation. Post numbers look like `#474 (11 May 2025)`; they are the channel's own ids, so any post can be opened at t.me/notjustmadinah/<id>.

## How to read this (limits and bias)

- **Who writes it.** The owner is a current Islamic University (IU) student who finished a bachelor's in December 2025 and in August 2026 started a one-year higher diploma at IU. He talks to the admissions office in person, so many posts say "I asked the deanship". That makes it the best English-language running record of the real applicant experience, but it is still a third-party source, not the University.
- **Commercial interest.** He sells paid Arabic translation of documents, short consultations, relocation help and sometimes lists flats. Posts urging "translate early" or "apply through us" are marketing. The facts in them (windows, rules, statuses) are still mostly corroborated by other posts and by iu-official.md. Treat his opinions on odds and timing as opinion.
- **Official or not.** Facts he attributes to the University or Ministry through a screenshot or an email text he translated are stronger than his guesses. Facts relayed from an Arabic-language channel run by another applicant adviser (named in the posts as the Abu Thumama Academy channel) are second-hand.
- **Coverage.** I read posts #1 to #61 line by line. For #62 onward I read a condensed copy: long lines were capped (250 to 650 characters, shorter earlier, longer for 2025 and 2026) and from Aug 2025 each post was capped at 9 lines, with housing and perfume adverts and social-media boilerplate removed. I then went back to the raw text for the posts where the cap cut off a key fact (#713, #781, #922, #1005, #1006, #1014, #1044, #1068, #1084, #1012, #842, #776). Some detail inside long late-2025 and 2026 posts (for example the full college FAQ, the Hamza test page, the Taseel programme requirements) was not read and is marked "not read".
- **Weighting.** About 75 percent of this report is 2025 and 2026. Pre-2025 material is kept only where it explains how the system got here or where it still helps (statuses, rejection patterns, documents).
- **Private details left out.** Phone numbers, names of private individuals, donation and bank details, lists of applicant names (for example a list posted in #473) and promotional prices of the owner's own services are not reproduced.

## 0. The big picture in one screen (what changed, newest first)

1. **Autumn 2026 intake: IU did not open.** The Study in Saudi portal ran a second window from 16 Sep to 6 Oct 2026 (for the January semester). IU's programmes were never added, "something that never happened before" (#1048, #1055). The channel expects the next general window around April 2027 (#1055, 7 Oct 2026).
2. **Spring 2026 window.** Opened 19 or 20 Apr 2026, programme choice closed 21 May 2026 at 8:59 PM, the submit button stayed live to 31 May, portal closed 1 Jun (#795, #833, #853). Offers and "final" decisions then arrived within weeks, much faster than before.
3. **The system changed in 2025.** One portal for everything (Minhati no longer takes new residential applications, #393, #457), a new "educational visa" instead of university sponsorship and iqama (#551 to #560, #667), no deferral, and a housing shortage that pushed new IU Arabic Institute (Ma'had) students online for a year (#603, #1014).
4. **Applications now return an answer in weeks to months** (3 months for a May 2025 applicant, #760) instead of one to three years, but all offers must be accepted within 5 days (#842) and universities can silently let applications expire ("period allowed for handling has ended", #504, #591, #681).
5. **Housing is no longer a given.** IU's 2025 intake was told scholarship benefits exclude housing (#474). Several other universities offer "free tuition, stipend, flights, no housing" (#817, #910, #1011, #1069).
6. **New paid in-person Sharia options in Madinah** now exist for men and women, with no age limit (a private college, #943, #1044), alongside five paid Arabic institutes (#564, #969).

### 2.2 Portal rules that matter (2025 to 2026)

- **Documents on Study in Saudi, 2025:** father's and mother's ID and the birth certificate were dropped, the CV was added, police clearance and medical were made optional (#463). **2026:** main documents are passport, school certificate and police clearance; medical report, recommendation letters and CV are supporting documents (#796). Arabic translation of the certificate is recommended; a 2026 answer from portal support on translation was shared (#821, content not read).
- **Name:** write only your own name exactly as in the passport, no father or grandfather names (#789, Apr 2026). On accepting an offer you retype first and last name in English exactly as in the passport; one letter of difference can block the visa (#842).
- **Photo:** passport-style, plain white background, neutral face, 4x6 recommended but 3x4 or 2x2 acceptable; do not wear white against the white background; use a photo studio (#238, #475, #828). Poor photos cause rejection.
- **Scans:** scan documents rather than photographing them; poor quality looks unserious (#140, #828). Files under 1 MB were required for KSU in 2024 (#343).
- **Educational grades:** since May 2026 you can type your grade and the maximum (#824). Some programmes now ask the certificate to be attached separately (#828).
- **Police clearance** can take weeks in some countries, so start before the window (#1021). In Jan 2023 an explanation letter was accepted when it was missing (#23); not verified for 2026.
- **Certificate legalisation:** some universities now ask for equivalency or attestation (UQU in 2026). A UK applicant reports the Saudi Cultural Bureau in London stamps GCSE and A-level documents without appointment (#877). Owner thinks legalisation raises chances (#976). Unverified.
- **Extra certificates help for Islamic faculties:** Quran memorisation (even partial), Arabic language, mutun and ijazah certificates (#58, #255, #289, #437). An admissions officer told him an applicant claiming 10 juz was rejected for lacking a certificate (#801). A graduates' recommendation now counts (#730).
- **Hamza test** from the King Salman Global Academy for Arabic Language: a certificate can be attached before submission and helped one successful sister (#836, #839, #914). Test dates in May 2026 were 24 and 25 May.

### 2.3 What happens after you apply (2025 to 2026)

1. **Statuses (Study in Saudi, general):** under review; returned for update (resubmit link, #479, #480); under process (تحت الإجراء); initially accepted with "awaiting preference confirmation"; completing admission procedures; later visa steps. In 2024 the labels were different (SCH vs UAP numbers, #272); since 2025 the portal shows which university accepted you, the programme, tuition and "support ratio" (#415). "No matching records" means none accepted you and the record was deleted (#431).
2. **Offer rule:** an email names the university and gives 5 days to accept; accepting cancels your other applications; ignoring it cancels that offer but other universities keep reviewing (#676, #842, #864). Check spam folders (#856).
3. **IU extra step:** a separate Minhati application (documents list #568), sometimes within 24 hours, using the new application number (#936). For 2026 applicants the Minhati login arrives only after initial acceptance (#982), then a follow-up email gives 24 hours to complete (#988). A Saudi VPN fixed a dead link for some (#876).
4. **IU interview:** online on Microsoft Teams, language choice with a translator offered (#538); questions are reported to be short (motivation, qualifications, activities, which scholars you know, #397); candidates may not share the questions (#687). Placement test is separate (see section 1 row 9).
5. **Minhati stages:** application submitted, update requested, update completed, under review, eligible for exam (non-Arab countries), initially nominated on Study in Saudi, congratulations accepted (#621).
6. **Final acceptance** comes from the Ministry, not the University (#1006). Then the visa application form on the portal must be filled or no payment link arrives (#577); outside-Saudi rule (#994).
7. **Under review for months** (since May) means no offer; by August the chances are low and in Sep 2026 rejection emails started going out while the portal status still said "under review" (#1000, #1018).
8. **Odds:** about 8 to 10 percent of applicants overall (#100, #183, #289, #681); a May 2024 poll of 1,054 readers gave 21 percent accepted among applicants who answered (#175).
9. **Rejected with reasons:** since 2026 many universities say why (#879).

### 2.4 IU specific events 2025 to 2026

- **2025 intake:** IU told 2025 applicants that housing was excluded (#474). By Aug 2025 many were offered the Arabic Institute online first, with a placement test on 20 Aug 2025 (#581). After a year online with good results they were expected to come to Madinah (#603). The owner wrote that online learning is weaker than classroom study, about 20 percent from lessons and 80 percent self-study (#603).
- **Backlog:** applicants from 2022 to 2024 with only "initial acceptance" got interview emails from July 2025 (#538) and final acceptances from late July 2025 (#555) to Dec 2025 (#665, #696). A 2022 applicant got a final acceptance in Aug 2025 (#571), another in Jun 2026 (#937).
- **Dec 2025 deferral rule:** visa applications that named August 2026 arrival were returned; you must arrive in January or risk the acceptance (#713).
- **Autumn 2026:** IU offered initial-accepted people who were not placed in the college a free one-year online Arabic programme, with priority next year if you attend and pass (#1020, #1014). Minhati may show an agreement to join the Arabic Institute until you reach the required level (#1038). No stipend while online (#1014). Maximum online period is one academic year (#1014). Those whose status says "initial acceptance on Study in Saudi" do not need to study online, just wait for the Ministry (#1037).
- **Aug 2026 warning:** applicants with only initial acceptance may not get final acceptance for the semester starting in August and may be pushed to the next one (#1006); "no online study for those who already studied remotely for a year" (owner's view).

## 4. Student life facts

- **Allowance:** 840 SAR a month for scholarship students (see section 1 row 5). No allowance for online study (#1014). Family adds nothing (#411).
- **Housing:** unmarried students traditionally got free dorms; the 2025 intake was told no housing (#474) and many universities no longer provide any (#1011). Old IU dorm buildings are being rebuilt (#474). Family housing exists for istiqdam students with a "very long" waiting list (#411).
- **Rent in Madinah (owner-sourced, 2024 to 2025):** shared room 1,200 SAR a month (Feb 2024, 2,200 in Ramadan) (#195); studio flats 1,000 to 1,800 SAR; three-room family flats 2,000 to 2,600 SAR; hostel beds 800 SAR a month (#528, Jul 2025); students sharing a flat 300 to 400 SAR each (#474). Compare against the current numbers in the MESH apartment guide before publishing any figure.
- **Cost of a family:** a shared spreadsheet "IUM Married Student Budget" was posted (#503, Jun 2025); the owner says Russian-speaking students spend about half, African students a quarter to a third.
- **Flights:** free summer round-trip tickets were named among the scholarship benefits in 2023 and 2025 (#85, #474); arrival ticket is requested on Manhati after the visa (#220). Under the educational visa some universities suspended tickets (#599).
- **Visa and residency (new system):** educational visa replaces sponsorship and iqama (#667). Work rules from the Jan 2024 launch: up to 20 hours a week in term, 40 in summer, two years of practical training after graduation, family on visitor visas (#216). You can leave and re-enter without asking the University for exit/re-entry permission (#985). No iqama means you cannot buy a car (#985). Hajj and domestic prices for "residents": students counted as residents in 2025 and the packages started at 8,092 SAR (4,099 in 2024) (#483, #507); whether educational-visa holders keep this access is not stated.
- **Banking:** educational-visa students can now open accounts (#626); one university helped them directly (#1051).
- **Renting:** Ejar registration for educational-visa students through accredited agents (#1012).
- **Family routes (Jan 2025):** (1) general visitor visas; (2) university-issued family visitor visa valid one year, family must leave and re-enter every 3 months or pay a fee that may not be available in Ramadan and Hajj, can cover parents and siblings; (3) istiqdam residency for wife and children: IU needs at least the third bachelor semester and a GPA, deposit 10,000 SAR refundable at graduation (#411), but a Nov 2025 post says "30,000 riyals in ours" (#679). The two figures conflict. Istiqdam gives free healthcare and school places. Other universities often waive the deposit and the waiting period (#411, #679).
- **Health:** all IU students take an X-ray and blood test on arrival; some have been sent home (#243). A health-services video and PDF were shared (#353).
- **Earning as a student:** tutoring Arabic and Quran, guiding tours (500 to 600 SAR a tour), English teaching, remote work, taxi (#405, #409).
- **Ramadan timetable at IU (2025):** first lesson 9:30, lessons shortened to 35 minutes, breaks 5 minutes, classes paused for the last ten days (#444).
- **Fitness and sports:** martial arts gyms near the University (#638).
- **Graduates:** 59,550 graduates 1961 to 2022 in one count (#412); a 2025 count shows 18,025 current students, 74,053 graduates, 92,078 in total (#550); 62nd cohort in 2026 had 3,339 graduates from 127+ countries (#815). IU now hosts graduates for 5 days of Umrah hosting from Aug to Nov 2026 (#735).
- **Dropping out:** the channel repeats that many leave the Ma'had, and in a Jun 2026 post says far fewer than half of entrants graduate (#10, #392, #854).

## 6. Questions this channel answers that MESH does not (candidate FAQs)

Short answers in our words. Add "reported by a student channel, date, source" or link to the University where possible.

1. **Can women study at IU in person?** Reported as no: IU's in-person route is for men, women can do the online degree (#196, #302, #319). Other Saudi universities (Princess Nourah, Umm al-Qura, Taibah, Qassim) take sisters, and the new private Sharia college is open to women. Source #908, #943.
2. **What is the age limit?** 17 at least, 25 in the official rule; students report exceptions both ways, but most accepted students are 18 to 25 (#289, #964).
3. **What documents do I need now?** Passport, school certificate with an Arabic translation, and (2026) police clearance on Study in Saudi; optional medical, recommendations, CV (#796). IU's own Minhati application separately asks for a good conduct certificate, birth certificate, tazkiyah, medical report and photo, all with Arabic translations (#568).
4. **What photo is accepted?** Passport style, white background, neutral expression, not a white thobe, 4x6 preferred (#475).
5. **How many programmes can I choose?** Up to 25 across universities in 2026, and each is marked free, partial or paid (#796).
6. **I got an offer. What now?** Accept within 5 days, retype your name exactly as in the passport, wait for the Ministry's final approval (#842, #1006).
7. **Final acceptance came. What is the visa process?** Complete the visa form on the portal, pay the visa fee (USD 230 reported in 2026), wait for the e-visa by email; embassy visit not required; medical and fingerprint steps unclear, so carry a health certificate (#577, #1005).
8. **I am in Saudi on a tourist or visit visa, can I apply for a student visa?** No: if you are inside Saudi when you apply, the application is rejected automatically (#994, #996).
9. **Can I postpone my start after acceptance?** Under the educational visa you are expected to arrive in the semester you were accepted for; postponement was refused in 2025 and Dec 2025 (#536, #713).
10. **I have initial acceptance but no final. How long?** Older applicants waited one to three years (#103, #393); for 2025 and 2026 applicants usually weeks to a few months, but some IU applicants were still waiting in August (#760, #1000, #1006).
11. **My status says "under review" since May. What does it mean?** No university has accepted you; by August chances are slim and rejection emails follow in September, then reapply in the next window (#1000, #1018).
12. **What does "Awaiting preference confirmation" mean?** You have an offer; confirm or decline within 5 days (#842).
13. **Do I need the Minhati portal as well as Study in Saudi for IU?** Yes: the University sends login details after an offer and wants a second application with extra documents; without a live Study in Saudi application the Ministry cannot issue your visa (#565, #783, #982).
14. **Is the Arabic test pass or fail?** No, it only sets your Ma'had level; cheating leads to wrong placement (#35, #919).
15. **Will I get free housing?** Not guaranteed. IU's 2025 intake was told no housing; check the "support" description on your offer (#474, #1011).
16. **Is the monthly allowance still 840 SAR?** Students say yes through Nov 2026; there is none during online study (#651, #1014).
17. **Why did IU not take applications in autumn 2026?** Many applicants already hold initial acceptance and wait to continue; the owner expects the next general window around April 2027 (#1055).
18. **I was rejected. Why, and when can I reapply?** Some universities now give a reason; many accepted students applied three to five times; add Quran, mutun, Arabic and course certificates (#879, #1025).
19. **What is an educational visa and what is the difference from iqama?** Electronic student visa, free in and out, 20 hours work rule, no car purchase or contract in your name until the Ejar route; not the sponsored iqama (#216, #985, #1012).
20. **Can I open a Saudi bank account on a student visa?** Yes since 2025 (#626).
21. **Can I rent officially without iqama?** Yes via an accredited agent and Ejar (#1012).
22. **How can I bring my wife and children?** Visitor visa from the University (renew every 3 months) or istiqdam residency (IU: after the third bachelor semester, deposit reported as 10,000 to 30,000 SAR, free healthcare and schooling) (#411, #679).
23. **Which Arabic institutes in Madinah are open and what do they cost?** Ahl al-Athar 350 to 450 SAR a level; As-Safwah 450 SAR a month evening men only; Taba morning from about USD 107 a month for men and women; MAP is a one-month course at IU; details in section 3.
24. **Do I need residency to join the Prophet's Mosque Institute?** The 2024 conditions said yes; for educational-visa holders no answer is published (#295, #218).
25. **Can I study Sharia in English in Madinah?** Yes, at a private college for a fee (#943, #1044).
26. **What happens on the first day of the placement test?** Blackboard, 100 multiple choice questions, 75 minutes, log in with the Minhati email (#1010, #923).
27. **Are Hajj packages cheaper for students?** Residents in 2025 paid 8,092 to 13,150 SAR; some universities send a few final-year students for free (#483, #507). Educational-visa eligibility not confirmed.
28. **How long can I study online in the Ma'had?** One academic year at most; after that you wait for final acceptance (#1014).
29. **Do university certificates need attestation?** Several universities ask for equivalency or Saudi Cultural Bureau stamping on high school documents in 2026 (#877).
30. **What is the "financial guarantee" box?** For scholarships upload a screenshot showing 100 percent support and zero fees; for paid study use a bank statement (#526, #696).

## The 15 most valuable findings

1. IU did not open on Study in Saudi for the 16 Sep to 6 Oct 2026 window (a first), and the next general window is expected around April 2027; the spring 2026 window closed 21 May (submissions to 31 May) (#1055, #833).
2. The 2026 portal lets you choose up to 25 programmes across 10 or more universities and shows free, partial or paid for each (#796).
3. Offers must be accepted within 5 days; accepting cancels the rest; unaccepted or unreviewed applications are silently cancelled or rejected (#842, #676, #504).
4. IU now runs a second Minhati application with extra documents (good conduct, birth certificate, tazkiyah, medical, photo, all translated) after you apply, plus a Teams interview and an Arabic placement test (#568, #538, #1010).
5. New students use an electronic "educational visa": USD 230 reported in 2026, no embassy visit, medical rarely enforced, and you must be outside Saudi when applying or it is rejected automatically (#1005, #994).
6. Deferral is effectively gone under the new visa (arrive in the semester you were accepted for); the "up to two years" line on IU's page does not match what students experience (#713, #536).
7. Housing is no longer promised: the 2025 IU intake was told scholarship benefits exclude housing; many 2026 offers (UQU, KAU, Imam Saud) also exclude it (#474, #817, #1069).
8. For 2025 and 2026 many new Ma'had students study the Arabic Institute online first for up to one year, with no stipend and a 25 percent absence limit (#603, #1014).
9. Residential IU is for men only; women are limited to the online degree at IU but can apply to Princess Nourah, Umm al-Qura, Taibah and Qassim (#302, #196, #908).
10. The 840 SAR monthly allowance is still reported by current students through Nov 2026 (#651, #985); the educational visa also removes the need for exit/re-entry permission but gives no iqama (#985).
11. Ejar now lets educational-visa students register rental contracts through accredited agents, and banks open accounts for them (#1012, #626).
12. Applicant odds are about 8 to 10 percent; many successful applicants applied three to five times; Quran, Arabic and mutun certificates and, since Feb 2026, an IU graduate's recommendation help (#681, #730).
13. The five Madinah Arabic institutes and their fees: Ahl al-Athar 350 to 450 SAR a level, As-Safwah evening 450 SAR a month, Taba morning from about USD 107 a month, plus MAP and the Haram Institute (applications 5 to 20 Safar) (#776, #922, #1068, #995).
14. A private College of Law and Islamic Sciences in Madinah offers Sharia in Arabic or English to men and women with no age limit, about 28,848 SAR a year on the portal and 30 to 40 percent off (#1044, #1045).
15. IU online courses (associate and intermediate diplomas and bachelor's) closed on 7 Nov 2026 after an extension from 31 Oct; the Endowment-funded free Sharia BA opens roughly twice a year with short deadlines (#1084, #992).
