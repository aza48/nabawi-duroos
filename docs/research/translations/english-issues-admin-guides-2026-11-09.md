# English issues found while translating admin's 14 guides (web, 9 Nov 2026)

The Arabic follows the English as written. Fix the English, then the Arabic draft.

## studying-online-at-the-islamic-university
1. The Fiqh al-Ibadat 1 example says a course's credit hours "set its weight in your GPA", but the dashboard paragraph and the problems table say the electronic courses give no GPA. Limit the sentence to the Sharia bachelor's.
2. "Both bachelor's degrees": the table lists only one bachelor's programme.
3. The semester and GPA "stay empty" in one place and "show a dash" in another.

## what-you-study-by-level
1. Law: "Saudi law, fiqh and usul from Level 1", but Usul al-Fiqh 1 is in Level 2.
2. Hadith plan, dropped courses: "Study of Saudi Law" and "Innovation" look like short names for المدخل إلى دراسة الأنظمة and ريادة الأعمال والابتكار. Confirm them.
3. The colleges write some Arabic course names differently (مقدمة / المقدمة في مصطلح الحديث, قيم / القيم الإسلامية, نظم / نظام الزكاة والضرائب). Check whether that is the University's own spelling.

## your-first-semester-week-by-week
1. "Weeks 2 to 5: 17 August to 17 September", but 17 August is in week 1 (classes start Sunday 16 August), so week 2 starts on 23 August.
2. The Month 2 stipend row (19 and 20 September) comes after the National Day row (23 and 24 September).
3. The link text "What are muqarrarat?" does not match that FAQ's question, "Where are the muqarrarat for my college?".

## university-student-system
1. One item has three names: "Nationality and birth details correction", "Nationality and place of birth correction request", and "Nationality, date or place of birth".
2. The intro promises Arabic menu labels exactly as they appear, but Final exams, View all recordings, Activity, Calendar, Grades and Join session have none.

## gpa-warnings-and-dismissal
1. "the next section shows how" points to dismissal, but the advice is in "Raising a low GPA".
2. "DN hurts your GPA more than an F", but the table gives F no points value.
3. The minimum GPA that warnings depend on is never given as a number.

## registering-your-courses
1. Add and drop runs 17 to 21 December 2026, but classes begin on 20 December, inside that window. That contradicts "a short window before classes begin" and "classes start straight after".
2. First year: "you cannot drop a course" sits beside changing sections in add and drop from the second semester. Say whether a section change counts as a drop.

## university-to-the-haram
1. "In the first semester of 1448 the Friday buses left at about 9:30 and 10:30 am" is in the past tense, but that semester is now.

## lost-your-iqama-passport-or-card
1. The intro says report a lost iqama within 24 hours, but the iqama section has no Absher reporting step.
2. The digital iqama is accepted at banks, yet "carry your passport for a bank counter" (probably meant for Visitor ID holders).

## getting-your-stipend-off-the-university-card
1. "Between the 21st and the 25th", then the weekend rule treats the 25th as the fixed day, which clashes with "not arrived by the 26th" in the problems table.

## university-housing-and-student-services
1. "Electric scooters ... Lock it" mixes plural and singular.
2. Loan repayment "starts on your stipend from the following months" does not say which month.

## check-visa-and-iqama-online and family-records-on-absher
1. Saudi Post delivers any new or renewed iqama card, but the new-baby section says "collect the card at Jawazat".
2. The table puts the exit and re-entry check under "Muqeem visa inquiry", but the steps use a separate "exit and re-entry inquiry".
3. The table leaves out the image code that the steps ask for.
