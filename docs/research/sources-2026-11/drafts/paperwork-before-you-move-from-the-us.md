## Facts in the guide, with sources

### The apostille chain
- Saudi Arabia acceded to the Apostille Convention (instrument deposited 8 April 2022); in force for Saudi Arabia on 7 December 2022. US is a party; both parties so apostilles work US to Saudi.
  - * https://www.hcch.net/en/news-archive/details/?varevent=887 (entry into force for Saudi Arabia)
  - * https://www.hcch.net/en/news-archive/details/?varevent=857 (accession)
  - * https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41
  - * https://sa.usembassy.gov/notarial-services-offered-in-saudi-arabia/ (US Embassy: in force 7 Dec 2022, both US and Saudi Arabia parties)
- No further Saudi MOFA or embassy stamp on an apostilled foreign public document: SAMA rulebook cites a MOFA cable no. 226041-44-001 of 24/08/1444 AH asking that the apostille be accepted as authentication without further certification by MOFA or diplomatic and consular missions; SAMA told banks the same.
  - * https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/approval-apostille-authentication-foreign-public-documents
- Courts: the Ministry of Justice told its judicial facilities to accept the apostille in place of the old authentication of foreign documents (Al-Watan newspaper).
  - https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1118709
- Dependant visa evidence (marriage and birth certificates): the apostille suffices, no Saudi embassy attestation (Fragomen client alert).
  - https://www.fragomen.com/insights/saudi-arabia-update-on-apostille-process-for-attested-certificates-copy.html
- Federal documents (signed by a US federal official) are apostilled by the US Department of State Office of Authentications; state-issued documents are certified by the state that issued them. Form DS-4194, name the country of use; fee 20 USD per document (not per page), non-refundable; mail 5+ weeks; walk-in 2 to 3 weeks; appointments only for life-or-death emergencies.
  - * https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/replace-certify-docs/authenticate-your-document/office-of-authentications.html
  - * https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/replace-certify-docs/authenticate-your-document/apostille-requirements.html
  - https://eforms.state.gov/Forms/ds4194.PDF
- "Do not notarize your document. Your document will no longer be valid if it is notarized" (State Department, about documents sent to it).
  - * apostille-requirements page above
- Some states need a county clerk's certificate first on county-issued vital records (New York: local or county birth, death, marriage records must first be certified by the County Clerk; NYS Department of Health records go straight to the apostille). Educational documents: the institution's official certifies the document as an official record or true copy before the apostille. Notarised private documents such as a power of attorney go to the state of notarisation.
  - https://dos.ny.gov/apostille-certificate-authentication
- Apostille does not replace an Arabic translation: several secondary sources agree; the existing MESH guides already require Arabic translations for the University and the licence swap.

### FBI background check
- The national US record is the FBI Identity History Summary; requested online through eDO (Electronic Departmental Order) at edo.cjis.gov. The owner's source said "eFOIA"; that is wrong, eFOIA is the FBI's Freedom of Information portal.
  - https://www.edo.cjis.gov/
- Fingerprints electronically at participating Post Offices; register with the FBI first, then with USPS Fingerprinting; bring the IdHSC confirmation email and photo ID; not available nationwide, check the location first.
  - https://ips.usps.com/IdentityCapture/Terms
  - https://le.fbi.gov/cjis-division/cjis-link/pilot-program-allows-electronic-fingerprint-submission-for-idhscs-at-select-post-offices
- FBI authenticates results with the FBI watermark and the signature of a division official; results can then go to the State Department for an apostille; a mailed hard copy option gives one sealed response per request.
  - * https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/more-fbi-services-and-information/identity-history-summary-checks/identity-history-summary-checks-faqs
- Only the State Department apostilles FBI checks; a Secretary of State returns them (State Department and several apostille services agree).
  - * travel.state.gov pages above; https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/living-abroad/criminal-records.html
- IU asks for a police clearance (شهادة خلو من السوابق) from the security authorities of the applicant's country: `docs/research/iu-pdfs-2026-11/bachelor-admission-guide.md` and the live MESH guide `applying-to-the-islamic-university`.

### Family
- Proof of relationship for a family visit visa: marriage certificate or a child's birth certificate naming both parents: existing MESH guide `visas-for-family-and-visitors`.
- School documents (birth certificate attested and translated, vaccination record, school reports for two or three years): existing MESH guide `kindergartens-and-schools`.
- CRBA: if one parent is a US citizen and the other is not, the US parent shows five years of physical presence in the US before the birth, two after age 14. Evidence: school transcripts, tax returns with W-2, Social Security earnings history, pay receipts, passport stamps (not alone); driver's licences do not count. US Embassy Saudi Arabia birth page (dated April 2026).
  - * https://sa.usembassy.gov/birth/
  - * https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/living-abroad/birth.html
- "Two or three certified copies, each apostilled" is written as practical advice (an apostille attaches to one document, and the State Department and states charge per document). It does not claim that Saudi offices keep originals.

### Driving
- State Department designates only AAA and AATA to issue IDPs.
  - * https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/before-you-go/driving-and-road-safety.html
  - https://fam.state.gov/fam/07fam/07fam1430.html
- AAA: 18 or over, valid US licence, two passport photos signed on the back, 20 USD (in person and by mail; one AAA club adds 5 USD return postage), valid one year, not renewable, valid only with the US licence, issued no more than six months ahead, usually same day in a branch, 2 to 4 weeks by mail.
  - https://www.aaa.com/vacation/idpf.html
  - https://www.aaa.com/vacation/docs/IDP_Application2a.pdf
  - https://www.aaa.com/tripcanvas/article/how-and-why-to-get-an-international-driving-permit-from-aaa-CM203
  - https://northeast.aaa.com/travel/plan-your-trip/international-driving-permit.html
- Licence swap with an iqama: existing MESH guide `drivers-license` (US is on its list of swap countries). Linked only.

### Power of attorney and US Embassy notarials
- Notarised private documents, including a power of attorney, are apostilled by the state where they were notarised (State Department; NY DOS).
- US Embassy Riyadh and Consulates General Jeddah and Dhahran: notarials by appointment only, in person, open to all nationalities, do not sign before the appointment, documents typed in advance, no remote service; fee 50 USD per consular seal.
  - * https://sa.usembassy.gov/services/notarials/
  - * https://sa.usembassy.gov/appointment-information-for-notarial-services/
  - * https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/records-and-authentications/authenticate-your-document/Notarial-Authentication-Services-Consular.html

### UK and Canada
- UK: ACRO issues police certificates and does not apostille; the FCDO Legalisation Office adds the apostille.
  - https://www.acro.police.uk/s/acro-services/police-certificates
  - https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised
- Canada: in force 11 January 2024 (instrument deposited 12 May 2023). Competent authorities: Global Affairs Canada (federal documents including RCMP, plus Manitoba, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, PEI and the three territories) and Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario, Quebec and Saskatchewan for their own documents.
  - https://www.hcch.net/de/news-archive/details/?varevent=953
  - https://international.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/services/document-authentication/requirements
  - https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2023/12/improving-authentication-services-in-canada.html

## Conflicts and how they were settled
1. "eFOIA" in the student's answer: wrong. The FBI service for an Identity History Summary is eDO (edo.cjis.gov). Used eDO.
2. Can the eDO electronic PDF be apostilled? Apostille services disagree (some say a print of the unaltered PDF is accepted, others say ask for a paper copy). The FBI FAQ confirms a mailed sealed hard copy option and the watermark plus signature. Settled by telling readers to ask for the paper copy and send that, which works under either reading. The PDF question is left out.
3. MISA: its FAQ and an industrial licence e-service still say the foreign company's commercial registration is "certified by the Saudi Embassy", which predates or ignores the apostille; MOFA's cable says apostilles need no mission stamp. Not settled for company documents, so the guide names no route and tells readers to get MISA's current list.
4. A July 2026 federalapostille.org page says Saudi Arabia is not a Convention member. Contradicted by HCCH, the US Embassy and SAMA; ignored.
5. Agency sites (Qogent) say a student visa police certificate needs Saudi embassy legalisation; outdated since December 2022. Ignored.
6. IU police clearance: a Study in Saudi post (Aug 2025) and member reports call it optional in some years, and members say a state police letter was accepted at application with the FBI check asked later at the visa stage. Not official, so the guide says only that the University asks for a police clearance and that the FBI check covers the whole country.
7. Existing MESH guide `getting-married-in-saudi-as-a-resident` says a foreign power of attorney is "certified by the Ministry of Justice". Al-Watan reports MOJ now accepts the apostille as the authentication. Not edited (another agent's file); flagged below.
