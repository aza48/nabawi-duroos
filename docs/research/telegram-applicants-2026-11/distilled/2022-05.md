# 2022-05 distilled
Read: core/2022-05.txt lines 1 to 802, every line (first #30229, last #33185). Reader: admin.

## Summary
May 2022 was a waiting month for IU and other Saudi routes. Applicants asked which universities were open, when IU would reopen, and when King Saud, Qassim, Imam and Hafr al-Batin results would come. King Saud (Arabic institute and degrees) opened on 16 May 2022 and Taibah opened around 25 May. Umm al-Qura stayed closed. Long threads covered bringing a wife, living costs for married students, age limits, tazkiyah and character letters, the medical letter, and attestation confusion. Many lines were Arabic-grammar study, marriage debate and off-topic chat, which are left out.

## Facts
- [IU-APPLY] IU admission is expected around September (2022 view of one member). (#30271; single)
- [IU-APPLY] The IU registration window in 2021 ran from October to December. (#30328, #32389; several)
- [IU-APPLY] In May 2022 IU was not open; the portal did not open earlier in 2022. (#32387, #32388, #33034; several)
- [IU-APPLY] A member said IU no longer does interviews since covid; applicants just apply online when registration opens. Another advised that going to Madinah in Ramadan and speaking to staff may help, and was told a nominee should have come before Ramadan. (#33182 single; #32610 single)
- [RESULTS] Members who applied to IU said they were rejected within about 4 months in the 2022 cycle, while earlier applicants had waited years. (#31480; single)
- [RESULTS] The IU list for those nominated via Minhati 2 had not fully come out by 11 to 12 May 2022. Names released recently were mostly from Minhati 1 (earlier years). Status is checked by logging in to the Minhati 2 site, not by a name list. "Initially nominated" students then wait for the ministry. (#32006, #32029, #32033, #32034, #32035, #32043, #32048; several)
- [RESULTS] Advice on "initially accepted": wait for the final acceptance, names keep coming out. A post-Ramadan (Shawwal) IU list was expected; none confirmed by 8 May. (#31647, #31650, #31653, #31655; single)
- [PORTALS] The old Minhati 1 system had published lists up to the previous year; members asked for its link. (#32992, #32417; single)
- [RESULTS] A member said if you are accepted by more than one university the first to accept you is the one kept and the rest are rejected. Another had heard being nominated at IU makes others reject you. Unconfirmed; a former student liaison was also unsure. (#32740, #32739, #32749, #32750, #32957; single)
- [IU-APPLY] A claim that the son of an IU graduate gets 100% acceptance was repeated second-hand and not confirmed. (#31709, #31710, #31713; single)
- [RESULTS] Deferring an accepted seat: members said up to 2 years is allowed but not advised, since the place can be lost. One knew a case of more than 2 years. (#31996, #31997, #31999, #32002, #32004; several)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Age limit: most Saudi universities want under 25 and a high school certificate not older than 5 years, set by the Ministry of Education. (#30585, #30591, #31387, #31389; several)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Several said older applicants (late 20s to 50s, one aged 55) do get in, but usually through connections; rare. Others urged simply applying. Some said low-applicant countries (Portugal and other small countries named) may be accepted at 30. (#30741, #30745, #30749, #30984, #32594, #32597, #32602, #30687; several, partly hearsay)
- [REQUIREMENTS] A bachelor's degree cannot replace a high school certificate older than 5 years. Suggested alternatives without age caps: Pakistani madrasas (free, a final degree is described as equal to an MA) and other countries. (#32997, #32998, #33002, #33005; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Al-Qasimiyyah (Sharjah, UAE) was said to accept only applicants aged 20 or below; it had 3 graduating classes then. (#32526; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Applicants asked whether GED or homeschooling is accepted; no clear answer. One said UK apprenticeships were accepted because a route was written for them. (#32396, #32397, #32408, #32409, #32411; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] The Faculty of Qur'an at IU requires being a hafiz. For other faculties, about 10 juz was said to be needed to graduate. (#30805, #30815; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Nationality matters: low-applicant countries are said to have better chances; UK chances said to be low because of volume. Members suggested Dutch, Canadian, Kosovan passports were easier. Unverified. (#30706, #30711, #30715, #30722, #30723; several)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Women are not admitted in the group; Umm al-Qura and others were mentioned for women, and a women's group was suggested. A woman needs a mahram to apply, a member said. (#30948, #30958, #30964, #32248; single)
- [DOCS] Documents for the 2022 IU window, per a member: the high school certificate and marksheet suffice, not every secondary year. (#30679, #30681; single)
- [DOCS] Documents took 1 week to 1 month to prepare; start before the portal opens. One member applied 4 days before the close after urgent passport renewal, another in the last minutes. (#32215, #32217, #32218, #32220, #32222, #32227, #32228, #32229; several)
- [DOCS] Translation: a name change not matching the high school certificate can be handled by a translated proof of the name change, or a letter in Arabic added to the file. A high school cannot change the name on an old certificate. A translation company was suggested for English to Arabic. (#31962, #31964, #31990, #31993, #33102, #33115; single)
- [DOCS] Applicants without a birth certificate (UK passport, born abroad) asked if it blocks the application; no answer. (#31974, #31977; single)
- [DOCS] Tazkiyah and character letters: one nominee said both his teachers got a character certificate from their madrasa on official letterhead. The police report is a criminal record check; the "husn al-seerah" letter comes from a school principal or teacher. One member got his character letter from his local masjid chairman and it worked. A legal-precedents document was not mandatory at application but may be needed after acceptance. (#31674, #31675, #31680, #31692, #31695; several)
- [DOCS] Nominees were asked to share blurred examples of accepted tazkiyah letters; applicants asked what a tazkiyah must contain and if official paper is needed. Unanswered. (#31668, #31991, #32308; single)
- [DOCS] The medical report: a GP "fit to fly" letter (a simple paragraph, about GBP 25) was used at application, not the long medical record. A full medical check is needed later for the visa or arrival. A Muslim doctor's general check was also used. (#33022, #33023, #33024, #33025, #33037, #32381; several)
- [DOCS] King Saud (KSU) application, May 2022: documents had to be attested by the foreign or education ministry of the country of issue. "Country of issue" is where the certificate was issued (UAE, not UK, for one member). Members asked if the Saudi embassy attestation can be skipped; one said embassies ask for the acceptance first. (#32448, #32459, #32460, #32461, #32486, #32504, #33176, #33178, #32963; several)
- [DOCS] KSU portal asked for 1st, 2nd and 3rd high school year certificates; members were unsure. One member asked how to remove or replace uploaded documents and saving errors ("please reselect"). The KSU form needed a CV; members asked if that means tazkiyah. KSU did not require tazkiyah or testimonial for one member. (#32517, #32518, #32422, #32446, #32535, #32727, #32524, #32525; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] King Saud University: Arabic institute for non-speakers, then degrees; application opened 16 May 2022. Browser check page and portal link were shared (https://ali-admit.ksu.edu.sa). Members said it has a Sharia faculty and an Islamic studies department with majors. (#31477, #31488, #32608, #32546, #32561, #32891, #32892; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Whether KSU application needs Arabic translation of documents was asked three times with no answer. (#32571, #32984; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Taibah University (Madinah) opened for men and women around 25 May 2022 (bachelor's). It has no Arabic institute and requires good Arabic before applying. Portal: eas.taibahu.edu.sa. Last date unknown. (#32970, #33015, #33020, #33027, #33130; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Umm al-Qura was closed for external applicants since about end of 2020; members repeatedly asked when it opens. On 23 to 24 May a link for an Arabic Language diploma opened (only for Arabic institute; a Qur'an certificate was asked for; unclear). Some said the portal opened to accepted students only. (#32465, #32477, #31359, #31364, #31365, #31366, #31369, #31372, #31375, #31377, #32851, #32895, #32917, #32918, #32935; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Imam Muhammad bin Saud University: Arabic institute only was open around 20 to 24 May. Members thought its institute strong; books were shared as a zip. (#32755, #32692, #32694, #32934, #32935; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Qassim University announced new accepted names for external scholarship students on about 29 May 2022. Applicants got only an application number, not an email; results come as a name list and there is no status page. Hafr al-Batin results were also awaited. (#32732 to #32738, #33130, #31269, #30572; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] KAU (King Abdulaziz) results were asked about on 3 May; unanswered. (#30386; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Qassim and Umm al-Qura, Imam were suggested as alternatives to apply to alongside IU. (#30687, #30948; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Other options discussed: Kuwait University external scholarship (a reseller offered paid registration), Al-Qasimiyyah Sharjah (praise limited), Islamic University of Minnesota (in person and online), Pakistani madrasas, Egypt, Mauritania, Gambia. (#31445, #31446, #32110, #33045, #33046, #33047, #32219; single)
- [SCAMS] Forwarded posts offering paid registration for scholarships "for a symbolic fee" with a Pakistani phone and private email were posted; one member asked if anyone had tried it. (#31445, #33130, #33151; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] If accepted, members said an email asks where to collect the visa. (#30390; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] The tourist visa (90 days) cannot be repeated: valid 90 days within 1 year. Another member said a wife can come on a travel visa every 90 days, with a loophole of leaving the country briefly that is risky. A question on the period when tourist visas stop (around Dhul Qa'dah/Hajj) had no answer. (#32577, #30409, #31016, #31019, #31022, #33095, #32084; single)
- [LIFE] Bringing a wife to Madinah: it is possible but expensive, said to need a deposit of about GBP 5,000 or 30,000 riyals plus other costs, for the wife only (not parents or siblings). Another wrote GBP 15,000+ in total. Not confirmed and may change year to year. (#30408, #30418, #30419, #30430, #30431, #30931, #30982; several, unverified)
- [LIFE] KAU family housing needs completing 1 semester with a high GPA. IU: a year of the preparatory stage first was said to be needed before bringing a wife. (#30411, #30412, #32584; single)
- [LIFE] Married IU students live in Al-Aridh (university apartments, far from the Jamiah, close to the Nabawi) or Sultana (nearer the Jamiah). One married student estimated USD 350 to 500 a month for a family with a car and rent, with rent around SAR 800 and USD 500 summed up for a wife in Madinah. Estimate collected second-hand. Student stipend quoted as 840 riyals a month. USD 8,000 a year was another figure. (#30229, #30459, #30460, #30463, #30468, #30482, #31352, #31003, #30985; several)
- [LIFE] On a student visa you cannot be employed; freelance work (English teaching, online courses) is what students do. A TEFL/CELTA certificate is advised for English teaching. Working on a work visa needs an iqama and sponsor. (#30545, #30547, #30596, #30642, #33043, #33030; several)
- [LIFE] Exact rules on iqama for family or older students living in Saudi without a university: sponsor-based iqama; one can attend duroos in Riyadh or Haramain Mahad (requires Arabic and iqama). Unconfirmed. (#30458, #31029, #31419; single)
- [LIFE] Online IU or distance study: asked, no answer. (#30945; single)
- [MADINAH] Bookshops in Makkah (Asadiyyah in Aziziyah) for cheap books; Imam Uni and Madinah Arabic books used for self-study. (#30318, #30375; single)
- [PREP] Arabic study order shared: Madinah books, Arabiyyah bayna yadayk, then Ajrumiyyah, Qatr al-Nada, then Alfiyah (Ibn Uthaymeen went straight to Alfiyah); some say Ajrumiyyah should be done in Arabic. For sarf: matn al-bina and lamiyah. (#32025, #32026, #32031, #32623 to #32635, #32650 to #32661, #32719; several)
- [PREP] Programmes advertised: Knowledge College 5-year Islamic studies and Arabic Level One (Madinah Series, 2 months), Arabic Code Academy (1 year), ILMA Madinah Arabic Book 1, Ma'had as-Sunnah English, Riyad al-Salihin course. (#30963, #31131, #31453, #32065, #32622, #32621; single each; adverts)
- [PREP] Age 25 and Qur'an: one applicant knew 13 juz and planned to continue hifdh before applying. (#30799, #30813; single)
