# 2022-06 distilled
Read: core/2022-06.txt lines 1 to 507, every line (first #33198, last #34442). Reader: admin.

## Summary
June 2022 was a waiting month. Applicants asked when the IU portal reopens (answers: around September, delays likely), what the new "initially nominated" status means, and what happens after it (visa number, police report, attestation, embassy). Other topics: Qassim, King Saud, KAU, Imam and Umm al-Qura lists and portal errors, bringing a wife, work after graduating, Egypt and other study options, mutun memorisation, and a Kuwait short course. One Pakistan-based "service company" offering paid help to apply was flagged as suspicious.

## Facts
- [IU-APPLY] In 2022 the IU application window had closed on 4 December 2021 (some said the portal opened around November 2021). Members expected it to reopen around September 2022, with October to November as the latest, and warned the university is not punctual: this year it opened a month later than announced. (#33261, #33263, #33753, #33777, #34159, #34380, #34382, #34383; several)
- [IU-APPLY] Applying in person or by in-person interview at IU was not possible in 2022; applications were online only. (#33751, #33753, #33755, #33756; several)
- [IU-APPLY] 2022 system as described: after applying you check your account and the status keeps changing. Soon after applying there is an Arabic placement exam of 100 multiple choice questions, publicly announced beforehand. One member said skipping it is harmful and that some applicants were rejected because of it. (#34164; single)
- [RESULTS] After about 4 to 6 months (another member said about 9 months) the status shows either a rejection (red Arabic text with an apology) or "initially nominated, awaiting final approval" (مرشح مبدئياً وفي انتظار الموافقة النهائية). The latter means the university accepted you and sent the file to the ministry, about 99 percent done. Previously people waited years with no news. (#34164, #34165, #34387, #34388, #34161, #34162, #33357; several)
- [RESULTS] Some think the status change applied to IU only because IU had the long delays; others' unis are said to be more punctual. (#34172, #34173; single)
- [RESULTS] A final acceptance list comes after the nominated stage. People asked in June when the final list would be published; the answer given was around September, with flights around February, as in the previous year (some flew in August, others in February). Those who applied 2 to 3 years earlier and were just accepted were expected to fly around August to September. Minimum wait about 1 year from applying. (#33781, #33787, #34161, #33609, #33681, #34135, #34237; several)
- [RESULTS] Applicants from the "Minhati 2" channel who were nominated were gathered into a group for admission updates. (#33545, #33563, #33896; single)
- [PORTALS] Minhati 2 was described as a new system where you are told accepted or rejected rather than waiting years; one member said waiting was at least 9 to 10 months before. (#33357, #34385; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] Steps after initial acceptance for UK applicants (June 2022): wait for the visa number, get an ACRO police report, take documents to the embassy and the cultural centre (both in London) for attestation, then the visa is stamped at the embassy. (#34143; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] Some accepted students were waiting a long time for the visa; one member estimated 8 to 9 months at most for the visa for fully accepted students. (#33358, #33360; single)
- [RESULTS] If accepted by more than one university, one member said the first acceptance automatically cancels the others. (#34185, #34186; single)
- [RESULTS] Question whether a rejection from Madinah affects next year's chances got no answer. (#34093)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Applying at 17 was said to be allowed by IU's website; one member applied at 15 and began at 16 (a brother said); another was accepted into the process before turning 18. Many universities state a limit of under 25, but some people "slip through". (#34016, #34078, #33993, #34012, #33958; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Applicants must be fluent in Arabic for IU, and Taibah has no Arabic institute so fluency is required there. For the other Saudi universities non-Arabs go to institutes unless fluent, and the university tests your level. (#33251, #33281, #33629, #34045, #34046, #34406; several)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Taibah asks for three high school certificates; asked what to do with only the final year one, and whether a certificate more than 3 years old is accepted. No answer. (#33665, #33952)
- [REQUIREMENTS] KAU scholarship post (2022-2023): bachelor 4 years, master 2, PhD 3; fully funded; applicants 17 to 25, PhD under 30; secondary certificate within 3 years; requirements CV, transcripts, passport, statement of purpose, two academic recommendations, certificate certified by the Saudi embassy, English proficiency; Arabic proficiency for Arabic, Sharia, psychology and sociology. Benefits listed: return ticket, accommodation, health care, monthly allowance. (#34035; single, forwarded post)
- [REQUIREMENTS] A member said the mahram question for female applicants and women's admission got no answer in the month. (#33645)
- [REQUIREMENTS] A 23 year old whose diploma is 7 to 8 months past the 5 year limit asked if it matters; the answer was just apply. (#33695, #33696)
- [DOCS] Attestation: coloured photocopies, not the original, are attested. Question whether Arabic translation is also needed got no clear answer. Another asked whether to scan translated or original documents. (#33208, #33213, #33636)
- [DOCS] A tazkiyah from a local imam is acceptable, the more recognised the better. (#33949, #33951; single)
- [DOCS] UK applicants had trouble with King Saud's form asking for final high school grade and credit hours, which GCSEs do not map to; unanswered. (#34120, #34124, #34126)
- [DOCS] A name that differs between documents and passport: member said Azhar scholarship requires same name on all documents; no answer found for Saudi universities. (#34267, #34271, #34275)
- [DOCS] Taibah: question whether to send the passport copy in Arabic or original got no answer. (#33462)
- [PORTALS] King Saud portal: members got an error page (photo) and a message when clicking the link; advice was to fill every box including optional ones and retry. Applications are checked by logging in with details and application number. Someone who applied in Jan/Feb doubted the portal was still open in June. (#33342, #33788, #33795, #33845, #33846, #33854, #33857, #34201; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Qassim published three lists of accepted international scholarship students by mid-June 2022 (the lists were in an applicant-run channel). Qassim was said to offer free apartments for married couples. People kept asking where to check status for Qassim, Imam and Umm al-Qura. (#33312, #33674, #33800, #33656, #34036, #33840; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Prince Sattam bin Abdulaziz University opened its portal for international scholarship bachelor applicants on 10 June 2022 (eg.psau.edu.sa). (#33644; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] KAU: term ended early June, acceptance list expected after Hajj; asked about deferral and student visa emails with no answer. KAU is also said to have an Arabic institute. (#33448, #33604, #33961, #33962, #34037; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] A member said accepted for Imam University but had no reply to the message sent as requested; the announcements page for the 1444 year did not exist at the time. (#34413, #34419, #34430; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Taibah application link requested; no answer. Umm al-Qura: how to know you are accepted was answered with a link to an applicant-run channel. (#33245, #33648, #33650)
- [OTHER-UNI] Spouse scholarships: members suggested Qassim, King Saud and Umm al-Qura. (#33637, #33640; single)
- [SCAMS] A "Qassim accepted list" post asked people to reply to a private email and gave a Pakistan number (0092). One member called it suspicious; another said it is a Pakistan-based student service company that charges a fee to apply on your behalf and did not make the list. Prince Sattam post also offered paid registration for a "symbolic fee". Treat paid application agents with care. (#33644, #33818, #33820, #33826, #33827; several)
- [LIFE] Wife: one member said you can bring a wife after 3 semesters (or after one year of the college) with a deposit (around 10,000 US dollars, or 30,000 riyals, said to be refunded after graduating) plus rent and bills; another said it is near impossible for most students. The more realistic route is a 3 month visa renewed by exiting to Bahrain. Whether the wife's visa renews during study: said it renews automatically with the student's. Polygamy: a member said only Saudis may marry more than one wife by Saudi law. (#34052, #34056, #34058, #34098, #34108, #34109, #34110, #34115, #34121; several)
- [LIFE] Tuition at IU is free for accepted students and covered by a full scholarship; a self-paying option was said not offered. (#33945, #33947, #33948; single) Whether you must apply separately for a scholarship after acceptance was asked and not answered. (#33973)
- [LIFE] Student life: students get paid and are not required to work; living costs in Saudi have risen. Working after graduating (jobs for scholars) was debated. (#34050, #34084, #34110, #34208, #34213)
- [ARABIC-INST] Whether students must go through the institute (mahad) first: one said even those who know Arabic must do at least 1 semester; another said placement exams per level, and strong students go straight to the college. Claim that teachers are all non-Saudi was called false by another. A member said overseas (kharjiyyah) students are taught by Saudi teachers, while teachers of the local-student institute are mostly PhD students. (#34064, #34065, #34068, #34074, #34075, #34076, #34101; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] 3 month tourist stay: American citizens said no visa needed for 3 months; whether one can leave and re-enter for another 3 months was asked repeatedly with no answer. Also a student asked about moving to Saudi permanently: no citizenship for non-Saudis; passport only through a Saudi father. (#33383, #33386, #33560, #33567, #33602, #33819; several)
- [STUDY] Dropping out of IU: question on process and timing got no answer. (#34180)
- [PREP] Online tajweed course channel and Arabic course links were shared; Arabic institutes abroad for vocabulary vs grammar (Alimi, Lisan al Arab for vocabulary; Ibaanah, Furqan for grammar; Fajr broader). Egypt and Subk al Ahad discussed for Arabic and Quran, about 300 to 400 dollars a month for all living costs, or about 4000 dollars a year for intensive study with food and housing, with safety warnings about arrests. (#33264, #33265, #33454, #33455, #34358, #34377, #34408, #34409; single or several)
- [PREP] Mutun: Zaad al-Mustaqni is memorised alongside Bulugh al-Maram; "Mutun book 5" contains Bulugh, Zaad and Alfiyah of Ibn Malik. A member advised starting hifz only after fixing tajweed. (#34238, #34241, #34300, #34303, #33271)
- [PREP] Kuwait Islamic Cultural Center 3 month Arabic and Islamic course, men 18 to 35, tentative 30 Sept to 1 Jan 2022, 8 hours daily, accommodation provided, airfare not, with interviews in late June. A member doubted a UK contact. (#33867, #33879, #33911, #33994, #34029, #34039, #33931; single)
- [MADINAH] English-language lessons in the Prophet's Mosque exist (a PhD student was given permission); private halaqat are not publicised. A non-student can attend public duroos in person. (#33706, #33707, #33737, #33738, #33739; single)
- [OTHER] A UK student asked about Madinah College (London part time) versus the University; answered they cannot be compared. (#34229, #34230)
