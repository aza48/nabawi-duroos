# 2022-02 distilled
Read: core/2022-02.txt lines 1 to 1716, every line (first #13145, last #19699). Reader: admin.

## Summary
February 2022 was the IU Arabic placement test month for applicants who used the new Minhati 2 portal. Applicants waited on the status "under study" (تحت الدراسة), then many saw "eligible to take the Arabic test" (مؤهل لاختبار اللغة العربية); the test ran on Blackboard on 26 Feb (retake 28 Feb 2022) and the group guessed scores, levels and what the statuses mean. At the end of the month some saw a rejection message. The group also debated whether acceptance at one university cancels the others, the age limit, faculties, bringing a wife, and which other universities were open (King Abdulaziz, then Umm al-Qura for postgraduate only, Imam and Qassim lists).

## Facts
- [IU-APPLY] IU curriculum for the institute (Mahad) is on the IU site: https://iu.edu.sa/site_Page/33302 (posted 1 Feb 2022). (#13145; single)
- [IU-APPLY] In the 2021/22 cycle the IU window closed in December 2021 (opened around September); Imam University closed in November 2021; Qassim was open end of Dec to early Jan. Most universities open once a year, autumn to winter. (#13226, #19180, #19160, #19181; several)
- [IU-APPLY] The IU guide PDF numbered the stages: apply, Arabic placement test, examination of applications, nomination, embassy/ministry. One member was told on 3 Feb that the "selection window" closed that day. (#13226, #13227, #13267, #13268, #13278, #13279, #13281, #14709; several)
- [RESULTS] Expected acceptance timing in Feb 2022: list "March to April" per the guide, with possible delays; later talk of the start of Ramadan or Shaban (before Ramadan). In 2021 the list for IU came after the Arabic test. (#13226, #14862, #14915, #14710, #18747, #19505; several)
- [PORTALS] IU's new portal is minhatee2.iu.edu.sa; the old Minhati 1 site had earlier applicants. Applicants from earlier years not nominated needed a fresh application on the new portal (said by a member who saw university replies on Twitter; the new portal had closed by Feb). One screenshot showed an older applicant with "initially nominated and pending final approval", meaning no new application was needed. (#15214, #15237, #15451, #15488, #15493; several)
- [PORTALS] Statuses in the IU portal: "under study" (تحت الدراسة) for months; many saw it for 5 to 10 months (one said 5 months to see any change and 10 months to be accepted). "Eligible to take the Arabic test" appeared from about 17 Feb 2022 for many (including from the US, Pakistan, India) and not for others. The portal showed errors during updates around 18 Feb and 27 Feb. (#14930, #14932, #14934, #15123, #15459, #16770, #16772, #16748, #15488; several)
- [PORTALS] Meaning of statuses (from members and a translated FAQ): "under study" is the university checking eligibility for the Arabic test; after the test, documents are finalised and the status becomes "initially nominated" and the file goes to the Ministry of Education; the next step is accepted. Initially nominated means the university accepted and the ministry is clearing the name, with a very high chance. Under the new system once one university sends your name to the ministry no other can send it. A translated FAQ listed other statuses (rejection apology, moved to next year, application incomplete, update application, missing documents such as birth certificate). (#16688, #17072, #17090, #17098, #14398, #14401, #14422, #18777, #18778; several)
- [PORTALS] Members disagreed on whether "eligible for the Arabic test" is the new name for initial nomination. One said no, with a case of an incomplete-document account getting the status while the complete one stayed "under study". Another said the test is a final stage; others said it is only placement. (#17063, #17075, #17179 to #17184, #17072, #17075; several)
- [PORTALS] The application (registration) number is the Blackboard login; username and password are both the application number. It shows in the Minhati portal near the status; keep a screenshot. The first 4 digits are the year; the rest is the order (one member estimated over 37,000 applications at about 24 Feb). (#15876, #15881, #17381, #17442, #17443, #18030, #16935, #16920; several)
- [IU-APPLY] Arabic placement test: online on Blackboard (ium.blackboard.com), 100 multiple-choice questions, 85 minutes, with audio (reading, listening, vocabulary/grammar). Dates: 26 Feb 2022 at 7 pm Saudi time for Europe/Americas (UK 4 pm), earlier slots for Asia (3 pm KSA, all Asian countries except Indonesia) and Africa (5 pm KSA), repeated 28 Feb 2022 at 6 pm KSA for those who missed it. The slot follows your nationality, not residence; a later clarification said people living outside their passport continent follow the time of the place they live in on the same continent. An official post first listed "Africa" twice by translation error; the second row meant Asia. Test link and details were posted on the university's Twitter. (#15015, #15024, #15026, #15028, #15181, #15206, #15296, #16045, #16905, #16909, #16900, #17236, #17237, #17238, #17240, #17248, #17480, #17505, #17530; several, official)
- [IU-APPLY] The test is for those whose status changed to "eligible"; the statement circulated said those who took it in the previous semester need not retake, and those who did not take it are removed from competition; it is also said to place you in the level, "nothing to do with acceptance", so do not cheat. Failing to attend excludes you. (#16065, #16064, #16905, #16909, #17431, #17541, #18229, #18983; several)
- [IU-APPLY] Test experience: log in with application number in advance, set the language to English with the globe icon, open "Exams and tests", use a computer (phones and iPad risky), sound is needed, save and submit button at the end (if missing, wait for auto submit). Refreshing during the test kicked one person out. Score is visible under grades (the alarm/bell icon, "my grades") but some could not see it; results shown are /100. Retake notices appeared for some on 27 to 28 Feb; a retake notice email image circulated was said not to be from IU. Some saw only "greater or less than 20%". (#16453, #16492, #17436, #17607, #17776, #18040, #18041, #18075, #18383, #19469, #19547, #19548, #19552, #19091, #19102, #19104, #19105; several)
- [ARABIC-INST] Placement and levels as members described: score 80 or more goes straight to the college (Kulliyah) per the guide; 40 to 45 about level 2 (1.5 years); a score of about 50 to 60 level 3; levels 1 and 2 are year one, 3 and 4 year two; level 4 is the end. Scores above 73 were told they would go to Kulliyah or level 4. The Mahad is 2 years for those who start at level 1. The test is repeated on arrival. (#16007, #14965, #14969, #14966, #17733, #18170, #18594, #19695, #19697, #19699, #15857; several)
- [ARABIC-INST] A member warned if the test is deliberately failed it is judged on arrival; a text from the guidelines said cheating (wrong answers on purpose, someone else sitting, translation tools) leads to cancellation. Others said no one can tell and they will not mind. Members also said placing too high without Arabic is a risk because you cannot go down a level, only up (one said they can try to go down a level). (#13561, #13569, #18768, #18823, #16286, #16288, #16305, #19667; several, partly single)
- [ARABIC-INST] Members in Madinah said the Mahad is easier but can be packed; the deanship pushes people to the college; in 2022 there was said to be a quota. One said students who rely only on the Mahad struggle in the college and hire a tutor; classes are split, one day in person, one day online, about 15 to 20 per group, laptop not provided and wifi weak. Rooms are shared by 2 with a partition wall. (#13416, #13430, #13438, #13455, #13495, #13497, #16359, #16374, #19295, #19299; several)
- [ARABIC-INST] A candidate who started the preparatory year at level 1 after scoring 40 was offered one or two levels higher and chose to start from the beginning. Placement stages came with a semester difference. (#14854, #14966, #14967; single)
- [ARABIC-INST] Other universities mostly use Bayna Yadayk; Imam has its own books (a link to Imam Level 1 books was shared; Imam Mahad books on archive.org). (#14602, #14732, #18667, #18666; single)
- [RESULTS] After the test, on 27 Feb 2022, some applicants received a rejection message (apology from the Deanship of Admission) inside the IU application page via "View"; others said it is not final. Some posted a rejection despite a score of 88 (second-hand). People who did not get the "eligible" status were told to write to admissions or wait. (#18884, #18886, #18887, #18916, #18983, #19124, #19140, #19184; several)
- [RESULTS] Reapply the next time it opens after a rejection; one tutor was said to have been accepted on the 5th attempt, another took 6 tries. Members said people from some countries wait 2 to 4 years. (#13326, #13343, #13347, #19140, #18930; several)
- [RESULTS] Whether acceptance at one university cancels others: conflicting. One head of admission said once your name is sent to the ministry no other university can accept you; a KAU student said not true; another said the ministry only allows one bachelor scholarship so the earlier acceptance wins (a case: nominated at Madinah and King Saud, King Saud's came first and Madinah cancelled). A translated statement said if you are accepted at several you can choose, but if accepted electronically only by one, the others are cancelled. The Saudi embassy in Germany said it does not know. (#14277, #14283, #14323, #14398, #14401, #19203, #19356, #19358, #19382, #19398, #19399, #19400, #19415, #19426, #19434, #19436, #19437, #19471; several, conflicting)
- [RESULTS] Qassim: a member got an email after applying in Dec (via the saudi consulate) asking him to send information; a member said it was an inquiry, not an acceptance. Qassim has no status portal; applicants were told a mail would follow; others asked how to cancel a Qassim application (no answer). (#19160, #19189, #19193, #19196, #19220, #19241, #19245, #14313, #14414, #14432; several)
- [RESULTS] Imam University list: lists were published 29 Dec 2021 and 19 Jan 2022 with few Western countries; people hoped for a further list on 9 Feb. Page: https://units.imamu.edu.sa/deanships/admission/announcements/Pages/S1443.aspx. (#14292, #14298, #16419, #16425; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Age limit about 17 to 25 for IU and most universities; some pass 25 but rarely; some from 26, 30s, over 40 and one 55 accepted; low-applicant countries (Portugal, Ireland, Albania were named) get exceptions; a member asked if a person born in Ireland living in the US counts as Irish (no official answer; a message to the IU translation service was said to say passport matters). (#14120, #14572, #14573, #14789, #14793, #14794, #13353, #13383, #19015, #19016, #19025, #19040, #19456, #19542, #19580, #16436, #16441; several)
- [REQUIREMENTS] The official guide says high school (GCSE equivalent); a member briefly thought it changed to A levels, then corrected it. Minimum 8 GCSEs was said by one student but not a deal breaker; only the English GCSE or one certificate is typical for the form. Difficulty converting German scores into GCSE points was mentioned. (#14092, #14122, #14123, #15793, #15796, #15832, #16074; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Being a hafiz helps only a little per a deputy for British applicants; the Faculty of Sharia was said to require memorizing 10 juz. The Faculty of Qur'an requires being a hafiz. (#13352, #13354, #13362, #14058; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Applicants from earlier years who applied by 2 years ago on the old site need a new application unless still "nominated". Late application is said not to hurt. Applications could not be edited in Feb (a rumour that edits opened was not confirmed). (#15162, #15214, #15237, #15797, #15798, #15799, #15829, #15833, #18954, #18956; several)
- [DOCS] Tazkiyah: members were confused how to send two (combine into one PDF or put in different sections). One got only the first one through. A sample "normal tazkiyah" was shared. A criminal record certificate was newly added to the required documents for after acceptance. (#15831, #15840, #15845, #15556, #17151, #17159, #17178; several)
- [DOCS] Documents get attested at the embassy when applying for the visa; original certificates may carry a monogram. Photo size asked by an Indian member (51x51, 35x45) with no answer. Applicants from the UK asked who certifies. (#16186, #16199, #19266, #19338, #33111; single)
- [DOCS] Weak Arabic fields (skills self-rating) were discussed; one member rated all "weak". (#17555, #17567; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] King Abdulaziz (KAU) was open around 5 to 9 Feb 2022 (Arabic program for non-speakers); question asked about its application period end on 20 Feb. (#14036, #14040, #14583, #14591, #15977; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Umm al-Qura: unified admission portal https://uquweb.uqu.edu.sa/admission/ui/register opened 23 Feb 2022 for masters, PhD and postgraduate diplomas only, plus nursing bachelor; not for Islamic studies bachelor. Arabic institute is applied separately before the bachelor's. Women need the mahram in the country. (#16517, #16584, #16585, #16609, #16612, #16660, #16664, #16665, #19117, #19118; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Princess Nourah University opened for female external scholarship applicants (2022/23, hijri 1443/1444): https://op.pnu.edu.sa/Scholarships/ExternalApplication.aspx. PNU announcement page was also shared. (#16514, #15748; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] A list of universities people apply to: IU, Imam, King Saud, King Abdulaziz, Qassim, Tabuk, Hafr al-Batin, Al-Jawf, Najran, Al-Qasimiyyah (Sharjah), Umm al-Qura. Imam and IU said to be the main ones, list not in order. (#19445, #19583, #19585, #19596; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Qassim is said to be strong in fiqh, with teachers of the Ibn Uthaymeen school; one said Qassim has about 7 to 8 Americans so western chances are high. (#14430, #14436, #19268, #19277, #19261, #19262; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Postgraduate: a member said a masters must be related to the bachelor's (Dawah to Hadith not allowed). Kulliyatul Sharia swapped Bidayat al-Mujtahid for al-Rawd al-Murbi'. Faculties listed: Qur'an (hafiz needed), Hadith, Sharia, Dawah and Usul al-Din, Arabic. Sharia was said to be most crowded; Hadith the hardest. (#14046, #14058, #14072, #14076, #13788, #13854, #18775, #18776, #14064; several)
- [OTHER-UNI] Elsewhere: Pakistani jamia (Islamabad Salafiyyah, 7-year hadith course), Egypt, Mauritania (mahdhara, no scholarships, wives possible), Somalia, Jordan, Yemen (safe in some areas). One said in KSA joining one university blocks others while in Pakistan you can leave. (#13926, #14363, #14367, #14447, #14527, #14672, #14770; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] On arrival at the airport with no contact, a student was held for an hour but let in; knowing someone there helps. (#14587, #14628; single)
- [FIRST-WEEKS] A member said a student scoring 40 started at level 2; another said he arrived and was tested again. Some students get the 2-month stipend lump sum on arrival to settle in; stipend of two months at once. (#14459, #14460, #18594; single)
- [LIFE] Bringing a wife: the university will not sponsor her visa until the first year of the college; a deposit of about USD 6,000 (or 30,000 riyals) is paid and returned monthly with the allowance. A "business visa" at about USD 2,000 for 5 years was mentioned for US citizens (leave every 6 months, e.g. by driving to Bahrain), unverified. Students cannot work; private freelancing and e-commerce are common. Some leave wives home and bring them in breaks. (#14478, #14480, #14487, #14521, #14522, #14588, #14512, #14477, #14499, #14503, #19568, #19577, #19576; several)
- [LIFE] Medical service at IU is free; asked about other universities (no answer). Living costs were asked. Housing: one said rooms from the 23rd to 25th apartments, an 8-year-old building room shown in a video. Madinah has a gym (not confirmed), and bookshops are cheap (said 70% cheaper). Squat and western toilets asked. (#14467, #13948, #13952, #13959, #13883, #13889, #13936, #17761; single)
- [LIFE] The Jamiah may cover one Hajj visit per student, said two members, unconfirmed. (#15723, #15731, #15733; single)
- [LIFE] Delay: asked how to defer after acceptance; no answer. Classes start in September if accepted. (#16362, #15220; single)
- [MADINAH] Haram duroos are after Fajr, Asr and Maghrib; check Twitter or an app. A shaykh's chair moved to the Fahd extension; Madinah feels calmer than Makkah. (#14952, #14953, #15720, #14951, #14061, #14938; single)
- [PREP] Advice: finish at least Madinah Arabic books 1 to 3, Bayna Yadayk, Ajrumiyyah and some sarf before going; many warn against skipping the Mahad. Placement test audio is intermediate level (ordering at a restaurant). (#14865, #14866, #14872, #14955, #14956, #14986, #15286, #16278; several)
- [PREP] Qur'an revision methods were shared (revise last 10 days daily, recite in sunnah prayers, listen to each ayah 7 times). Resources: Madinah books side books page, Madinah Student UK posts on the test (https://www.madinahstudent.co.uk/arabic-placement-test-info), a vocabulary group, Dus Dawah videos, Imam Uni books. (#13466, #13976, #13982, #13538, #16470, #16644, #18658, #18667; single)
- [SCAMS] Forwards from "student services to Saudi universities" accept registration for a "symbolic fee" via email and phone; these are paid agent offers. (#16158, #16514, #16657, #17334, #18970; single)
- [STUDY] Online study: IU held a semester of Mahad online for those accepted before covid; no normal online program was known. In-person interviews have not been held since covid. (#14269, #14270, #16687, #17412; single)
