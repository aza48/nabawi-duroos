# 2022-04 distilled
Read: core/2022-04.txt lines 1 to 1075, every line (first #27041, last #30226). Reader: admin.

## Summary
April 2022 (Ramadan 1443) was the month the Islamic University of Madinah (IUM) began releasing results from the old Minhati ("Minhatee 1", applicants from 2018 to 2021) and started changing statuses on the new Minhati ("Minhatee 2", 1444 intake). Applicants argued about what the lists, statuses and dates meant, and many rejections were announced from 24 April. Other universities (Imam, Umm al-Qura, Qassim, King Saud, King Abdulaziz) released lists or opened narrow windows, causing confusion over who may apply. Travel rules (vaccination, e-visa, insurance), documents (tazkiyah) and honest reviews of the Madinah Arabic institute and curriculum were also major topics.
