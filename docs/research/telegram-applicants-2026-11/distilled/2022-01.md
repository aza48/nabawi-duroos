# 2022-01 distilled
Read: core/2022-01.txt lines 1 to 1048, every line (first #9116, last #13143). Reader: admin.

## Summary
January 2022 was the wait for IUM's Arabic placement test after the new Minhati ("Minhatee 2", 1443/1444 cycle) application window closed on 4 Jan, plus a busy week when many applicants rushed to Qassim, King Saud, King Abdulaziz, Tabuk, Najran, Jouf and Hafr al-Batin portals. The big recurring debates were the "one scholarship per student" rule (the Ministry of Education cancels your other applications once one university accepts), the age cutoff, and whether Al-Azhar is a safe alternative. A student who had arrived at the Madinah Arabic institute in late January shared first-hand arrival details (quarantine, rooms, costs, allowance). Admins repeatedly told members to read the pinned messages.

## Facts
- [IU-APPLY] The Minhatee 2 login for IUM applicants is minhatee2.iu.edu.sa; status is checked by logging in. (#11493, #11495, #11521; several)
- [IU-APPLY] In 2021/22 the IUM application window ran September 2021 to 4 Dec 2021 (one member said Madinah opened a month later than announced). Applicants described the cycle as: apply, Arabic placement test, acceptance list in about April, embassy visa in about June, fly in August or September, so about 8 to 10 months at best. Some said it can take over a year, or 2 to 3 years on average with reapplications. (#10632, #10668, #10681, #10695, #10696, #10773, #11991, #11994, #11995, #11999, #12001, #12046; several)
- [IU-APPLY] Before the new Minhati system you could apply at any time of the year; the new system restricted applications to September to December. Next IUM window: members said September 2022 (one said after Ramadan; contested). (#11237, #11891, #11895, #12046, #12051, #12052; mixed)
- [IU-APPLY] The Arabic placement test: the guide said the test would be 16 Dec 2021 to 4 Jan 2022; by 4 to 5 Jan nothing was announced, and it kept slipping through January. The Deanship replied to two emailing applicants (6 Jan and 30 Jan) to wait and follow the Deanship's Twitter and the university website. Applicants were told it would be announced on Twitter and in Arabic and English, with some days of notice. A member said a student had told him the test was delayed by almost 6 months and would come "in February". (#9221, #9641, #9665, #9666, #9667, #9751, #9835, #9838, #9869, #9873, #10182, #10188, #10564, #10590, #11496, #11530, #12128, #12130, #12199, #12363, #12459, #12795, #13043; several, partly official)
