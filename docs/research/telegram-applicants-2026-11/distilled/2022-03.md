# 2022-03 distilled
Read: core/2022-03.txt lines 1 to 1834, every line (first #19714, last #27038). Reader: admin.

## Summary
March 2022 was the first month of the new IU "Minhati 2" system and the whole group waited for results. The Arabic Placement Test (a multiple choice test on Blackboard) ran in three sittings, the last on 5 March. Statuses then changed from "eligible for the Arabic test" to "under study" (تحت الدراسة) for most from 12 to 27 March, and a few applicants were told they were "initially nominated". Rumours, fake screenshots and second-hand claims from the deanship dominated; no official list was out by 31 March. Also covered: the old Minhati 1 country lists (IU's admission site), bringing a wife and the deposit, deferral risk, documents after acceptance, King Saud, Imam, KAU and Dar al Hadith routes.

## Facts
- [IU-APPLY] Early March 2022: IU applicants who applied on the new Minhati 2 system were sent an Arabic Placement Test (online, 100 multiple choice, no "don't know" option). Members said it was easy and guessable, and that not everyone was made eligible for it. (#19733, #19734, #19739, #19754, #19755, #19766, #19867; several)
- [IU-APPLY] The test is taken on Blackboard: username is the application ID (or email) and the password is the PIN the university gave; some got "wrong password" errors and were advised to retry on exam day and email IU admissions. (#20645, #20651, #20668, #20671, #20672, #20675, #20677, #20680; several)
- [IU-APPLY] IU announced (on its channels, forwarded 3 March) a makeup test for 1443 AH external applicants who missed earlier dates: a third and last sitting on Saturday 5 March 2022 at 18:00 Saudi time, with video guides on entering Blackboard and viewing scores. (#20379, #20381, #20448, #20449, #20495; official)
- [IU-APPLY] Members said sittings were on a Saturday and a Monday, those who missed both could do it on 5 March; if the test is still listed in your Blackboard you can still take it, if it is not listed it was submitted; a test cannot be retaken once submitted. (#20417, #20419, #20431, #20435, #20483, #20486, #20511, #20565; several)
- [IU-APPLY] Some applicants who missed the test (or whose status never became "eligible") were rejected soon after; several members said the test is mandatory and skipping it cancels the application. People from Denmark and elsewhere said some applicants did not know about the test and were rejected. (#20479, #20483, #21018, #21029, #21035, #21437, #22110; several)
- [IU-APPLY] The test score can be seen by going to the home screen and opening grades straight after the test; later it was not visible to anyone. Applicants who did not see the score were told it does not affect the application, and that if the test no longer shows on Blackboard the score was at least 20. (#21623, #21786, #24818, #24835, #24837, #24838, #24842; single/several)
- [ARABIC-INST] Members understood the score bands as: 0 to 20 first level of the institute (mahad), 21 to 40 second, 41 to 60 third, 61 to 80 fourth, 81 to 100 straight to the college (kulliyah). Members disagreed on whether one can ask to start lower after arrival (said possible but through 3 to 4 people) and whether students are re-tested on arrival (one friend in the January intake was not; another heard it is to catch cheating). (#19775, #19776, #19866, #19795, #19801, #19804, #19938, #20917, #20918, #20945; several, mixed)
- [ARABIC-INST] Grammar (sarf and nahw) starts in institute level 1 in part and fully from level 2. One member said institute is 2 semesters a year; others said IU might change from 2 semesters to 3 terms. (#19716, #19871, #19878, #19879, #22862, #23422; single)
- [RESULTS] 1 March to 7 March: the acceptance list was expected "March to April" or during Sha'ban (began about 4 March 2022) up to Ramadan (about 2 April). Notification on the portal and the list can come about 3 weeks apart; people see their status on different days and the list follows. (#19759, #19774, #20043, #20545, #20611, #20616, #20617, #20635, #20646, #20663, #21213; several)
- [RESULTS] Status "eligible for the Arabic test" (مؤهل لدخول اختبار اللغة العربية) shows for everyone not yet rejected and is not a rejection. A rejection shows as "rejected" (مرفوض) in red on the Minhati 2 account. (#20088, #20110, #20111, #20512, #22169; several)
- [RESULTS] Old Minhati 1 acceptances (people who applied in earlier years) appeared from 7 March on country lists at admission.iu.edu.sa (IuperviousAbroadInquery.aspx): by 9 March only 10 countries were listed, the UK list had only 4 names, and lists were updated in stages, old names removed. Minhati 1 and 2 logins do not work across each other. (#21812, #21796, #21484, #21485, #22700, #22701, #22707, #22724, #22726, #22727, #22728, #22729, #26675, #26782, #26785; several)
- [RESULTS] Old-system applicants "still under consideration" were told by the university to reapply on the new system; one nominated since January 2019 was still waiting. Stories of acceptance 3 to 5 years after applying. (#19901, #19946, #19947, #21499, #21860, #21888; single/several)
- [RESULTS] "Initially nominated, pending final approval" (مرشح مبدئياً وفي انتظار الموافقة النهائية) was explained by a member as accepted by the university and awaiting the Ministry of Education; a member said the ministry stage takes 2 to 3 months up to a couple of years. "Final acceptance" (القبول النهائي) is a separate later step. (#21822, #21855, #23881, #25603, #25687, #25688, #25699, #25700; single/several)
- [RESULTS] From about 12 March 2022 statuses began changing one at a time, from "eligible" to "under study" (تحت الدراسة), with German passport holders changing together and most UK, Danish, Irish, Australian and Canadian applicants first; by 27 March "all" members reported under study, North American applicants last. Members disagreed on whether under study is good, bad or the same as "eligible". (#23362, #23407, #23479, #23483, #24762, #24808, #24815, #25427, #25941, #26109, #26162, #26674, #26713; several, mixed)
- [RESULTS] On 22 March a university deanship reply forwarded by a member said "eligible" and "under study" are the same and everyone is waiting for nomination, with the list within three weeks; other second-hand claims said 5 of 7 under study numbers checked at the deanship were accepted, that India had about 1200 applicants and around 90 admitted, and a 10 percent overall acceptance rate. The forwarder said he did not know if it was authentic. (#25542, #25543, #25544, #25551, #25552, #25553, #26646, #26658; single, unverified)
- [RESULTS] IU's own answer to an external applicant on 22 March was "we have not been notified of this by the competent authority" and a mandoob said not to trust videos or posts unless from the official Twitter account. Official IU admissions Twitter handle: @AdmissDeanship. (#25493, #25500, #26554, #24651; official/experienced)
- [SCAMS] On 14 March a screenshot of "initially nominated" for a Tanzanian applicant was investigated by an applicants' channel and declared fake; IU had not given nomination to anyone yet. Another screenshot of a status change was doubted by members. Members agreed to only share verified status changes. (#24110, #24117, #23513, #23529, #23560, #24413, #24417; several)
- [RESULTS] Some members were told by admissions staff (a visitor on umrah on 30 March) that decisions were already made and would be released in one to two weeks; a Canadian mandoob said mandoobs did not have concrete information. (#26897, #26554, #24440; single)
- [RESULTS] Late March: Minhati 2 website was down at times, and on 25 March the Blackboard exam link disappeared from it while the system was down, taken as a sign of an update. (#20870, #21131, #25848, #25856; single)
- [RESULTS] A member said an IU student told him that students who applied in 2021's last period have no more slots for the Arabic test, and the next chance is when admissions reopen. (#24731, #24734, #24738; single)
- [IU-APPLY] Order of choices (faculty) selected in the application does not matter and can be changed later; applying for hadith does not lower chances. (#23230, #23236, #23237; single)
- [IU-APPLY] UK applicants did not know what to enter for GPA; people guessed numbers (for example 3.1 or 4.5 from a top mark) and a student-life account's advice was doubted. A UK degree instead of GCSE and A levels was asked about. (#21748, #21749, #21750, #21755, #21759, #22042, #24714, #24715; unanswered)
- [IU-APPLY] A member who put his passport expiry as 2020 instead of 2030 was still accepted. (#23978; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Country quotas exist: UK about 35 to 45 students in earlier years, Ireland and Denmark are low-application countries. About 45,000 applicants were quoted for 2022. Children of Saudi residents cannot attend through the external route and have a separate list. (#20994, #21132, #23954, #23959, #20832, #20836; single)
- [REQUIREMENTS] Age limit quoted as 25 by the Education Ministry but exceptions are made; a high school certificate older than 5 years may still be accepted, and one can sit an equivalent such as the GED. (#20691, #20715, #20727, #26300, #26302; single)
- [DOCS] After acceptance for the visa: the police (criminal record) check is only for the Saudi embassy visa, not for the university, and is not needed at application. (#22284, #22302, #22314; several)
- [DOCS] The "testimonial" (tazkiyah) can come from a sheikh who graduated from a Saudi university, a local masjid, Islamic organisation, student of knowledge, online Arabic or Quran teacher, or a Somali masjid imam; strong references help but one student got in with two local imams. (#22289, #25109, #25112, #25113, #25770, #25771, #25778, #25780; several)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] A UK member accepted around 14 March described the steps: the student visa must be requested through a visa company; an internationally recognised police check valid only 4 weeks; a medical letter on a template with about 10 tests including a chest X-ray; the acceptance letter printed from the portal. Another recalled being turned away at an embassy 4 to 5 hours away a week before term. (#21804, #24458, #24466, #24467, #24471, #24475, #24481; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] Applicants in the UK were told that when the list comes out they will receive instructions for a WhatsApp group; a country mandoob (the older students' representative) guides new students. Flights were expected in July for September; the new academic year was said to start after Eid al-Adha. (#22224, #22225, #21442, #21444, #25705, #24642; single)
- [VISA-TRAVEL] Tourist visa for family: a 1 year visa allowing stays up to 90 days, e-visa for Western passports in minutes, exit to Bahrain and return for another stay. (#20460, #20461, #20465, #20470, #20471, #20475; several)
- [IU-APPLY] Deferral after acceptance: some said 1 to 2 years is possible (one knew someone who deferred 4 years); others including a graduate said the place is not guaranteed if you defer and that IU is stricter now. Advice from an IU graduate: do not risk it, nothing in Saudi is fixed, and finish regular university later because Madinah can be lost; official confirmation would lower the risk. (#20385, #20386, #20443, #20450, #24548, #24551, #24584, #26922, #26923, #26926, #26927, #26988, #26992; mixed)
- [LIFE] Wife: from the second year (after one year of the college); a deposit of £5,000 or 30,000 riyals is held and returned at graduation or given back in monthly parts on top of the stipend; rent, bills and food are the student's own cost; no university housing for families; the wife can be kept on a tourist visa with exit to Bahrain; overstaying risks expulsion. (#20457, #20458, #20460, #21995, #21998, #22000, #22033, #22038, #22047, #22052, #24703, #24712, #24717, #25720, #25734, #25739, #24727, #24729; several)
- [LIFE] The monthly stipend was quoted as 840 riyals. Healthcare is covered by the university including a student's wife (one student's wife gave birth and fees were covered). Dorm rooms are for students only. (#25739, #23081, #23088, #20769, #20770; single)
- [LIFE] The Haram institute, Dar al Hadith in Makkah: opened registration for the 1444 AH year for students abroad; conditions at darallhadith.com require fluent Arabic (no mahad) and one member said a Saudi iqama. Another said a sheikh told him it is now open to foreigners. (#22317, #22319, #22366, #22586, #22606, #23016, #25238, #25239, #25248, #25249; mixed)
- [OTHER-UNI] King Saud University: a member emailed admissions with name, passport number, application number and username and got a reply the next day saying not accepted; others tried the same. People could not log in to the King Saud portal. (#25202, #25225, #25231, #25235, #21458, #21481; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] King Abdulaziz University (Jeddah) sent result emails around 3 March; one was a rejection; applicants checked their spam folder. (#20493, #24089, #24091; single)
- [OTHER-UNI] Imam University: a list page for the 1443 year (S1443) had been updated twice, last on 19 January; 5 American students were reported accepted on 29 March; it takes fewer foreigners than Madinah. Umm al-Qura was expected to open soon (a few foreign students per year said). Majmaah University offered an international bachelor scholarship at edugate.mu.edu.sa (age 17 to 25, certificates attested after final acceptance). Qasimia in Sharjah opened on 31 March. (#19852, #26782, #20579, #20583, #25899, #25916, #25917, #20779, #19818, #27019; mixed)
- [OTHER-UNI] For women: Princess Nourah, Imam and Qassim were named; a mahram must be in Saudi, and it is easier if he is in the same city. (#26040, #26126, #26153, #24741; single)
- [STUDY] Masters and PhD at IU come with a scholarship; one said a bachelor in sharia then masters in hadith is possible; entrance tests for masters said to be allowed only once; no age limit if accepted. (#25355, #25361, #19807, #19809, #19811, #22488, #22522; single)
- [STUDY] Hifz load: one said 2.5 pages a week, another said Imam University's usool al-din asks 6 pages a week, a graduate said no requirement; finishing kulliyah at IU needs at least 10 juz said one member. (#23575, #23576, #23577, #23583; single)
- [PREP] Arabic course resources named: Madrasatuna and Arabic Enthusiasts (for the Madinah books), Bayna Yadayk with translation, and an Egypt-based teacher with a masters in nahw, sarf and balagha. (#19781, #20039, #22920, #23549, #23648; single)
- [OTHER] Many hours of debate on Azhar vs Madinah, Mauritania mahdaras and living costs (for example Nouakchott rent about £200 for an unfurnished 2 bedroom), and Somalia, Yemen and Egypt as other options; Islamabad schools with a mahad. (#20190, #20196, #20208, #25438, #25439, #25501, #22633, #22640; single)

## Unanswered or confused questions
- When will the acceptance list come out and does "under study" mean initial acceptance? Asked dozens of times from 1 to 31 March; only second-hand claims. (#19743, #19758, #23044, #24011, #25057, #25411, #26055)
- Does taking the Arabic test raise the chance of acceptance, and does a high or low score matter? Mandoobs said test sitters have high chances but it is hearsay. (#20122, #21014, #21434, #22110, #22711)
- Can one defer a year or more after acceptance, and is the place kept? (#20385, #20450, #22250, #24495, #25805, #26880)
- When to travel if accepted in 2022 and what extra documents are needed after acceptance? (#22252, #24519, #24685, #24479)
- How to compute GPA or grade for UK and other certificates, and what to do with a degree instead of school certificates. (#21748, #22042, #24714)
- Can the wife come in the institute stage, and how much money is really needed? (#20485, #24704, #25715)
- Missed the Arabic test because the file did not open or the email never came: another chance? (#20423, #22929, #24731)
- Status "details of the test" (تفاصيل الاختبار) meaning. (#24506)
- Which other universities are open now? Answers: none open in March, Umm al-Qura soon. (#22966, #23448, #25898, #26964, #27014)
