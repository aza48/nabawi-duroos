# Study in Madinah outside the Islamic University degree track: research notes

Researched 7 November 2026 (about 26 Jumada al-Awwal 1448 AH). All facts are paraphrased. Confidence: H = read on the provider's or an official page, M = read on a secondary or undated page, L = search-snippet only or contradicted elsewhere.

Fetch notes: gph.edu.sa and mutunalijaz.com would not resolve from my tool; alharamain.gov.sa gave a certificate error; themadinahinstitute.com did not resolve; spa.gov.sa article pages returned only headlines; x.com and Telegram posts were only partly readable. Anything that needed those is marked L or "not verified".

## 1. Corrections to MESH programmes

Compared against src/content/programs.ts and the sisters guide (guide is dated Dec 2023 and says so).

1. **Masjid an-Nabawi Ma'had and Kulliyah registration timing (programs.ts "nabawi-women", and the sisters guide).** MESH says registration opens once a year, usually in Dhul Qa'dah. The 1448 call for the Haram Institute and its Madinah branch ran online from 5 to 20 Safar 1448 (20 Jul to 4 Aug 2026), with the written exam on 11 Rabi' al-Awwal (24 Aug) and the interview on 13 Rabi' al-Awwal (26 Aug). Fix: say "the 1448 call was in Safar; dates change yearly, watch the Haram's unified admission portal". Sources: https://www.forsawazifasa.com/2026/07/haram-institute-admission-1448.html (Jul 2026, M, quotes the official dates), SPA headline https://spa.gov.sa/N2637583 (20 Jul 2026, H for the headline and Safar deadline), https://www.almowaten.net/2026/07/ (Jul 2026, M).
2. **Same entry: the Kulliyah is described only for women, eastern side, "four-year Shariah bachelor's".** Official 1447 call said the college takes men and women in the Shariah department; other departments such as Qur'an and its sciences were men only. Hold on gate numbers and "good Arabic" as unverified (2023 guide). Source: SPA https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2351711 (1447 call, headline read, detail via search, M).
3. **Memorisation requirement for the Kulliyah is disputed.** Guide says 15 ajza. The Institute's higher section (bachelor's) 1448 call says 10 ajza for Shariah and Qur'an departments and 3 for others, plus an Arabic proficiency test for non-native speakers. The college page snippet says 15 consecutive ajza. Fix: state "10 to 15 ajza depending on programme and year, check the current call". Sources above, L to M.
4. **Eligibility for non-Saudis is not mentioned.** The 1448 Institute call requires Saudi nationality or a valid residence permit (iqama), a written sponsor or employer consent for non-Saudis, attested foreign certificates, medical fitness, full-time study (no other formal programme at the same time), and a minimum age of 12 for the intermediate stage and 15 for the secondary stage. A tourist or visitor visa does not qualify. Higher section needs a secondary certificate of at least 85 percent issued within 5 years. Source: forsawazifasa link above (M), ewdifh https://www.ewdifh.com/jobs/85645 (L). Important to add: a student who is only in Madinah on a visit cannot join the Ma'had.
5. **Official website for the women's Ma'had.** programs.ts links gph.edu.sa/public/?page=ins. Search shows the same site has a women's section page (page_869032) saying girls and women with a primary certificate can continue to middle and secondary stages with no fixed age limit for the women's section. That conflicts with the "minimum 12" rule in the general call, so ask the women's office. Source: search listing https://gph.edu.sa/public/?page=page_869032 (not opened, L).
6. **Qur'an and mutun halaqat (programs.ts "nabawi-halaqat").** Fine in spirit, but missing the real registration system. It is the Haram Educational System at qm.edu.sa (accounts via reg.qm.edu.sa, help via help.qm.edu.sa). Programmes now include in-person and remote halaqat, tajweed correction (separate for men and women), a Qa'idah Madaniyyah course, Usul al-Qira'at course, a "Mutun Talib al-Ilm" series with 8 levels, and apps. Source: https://qm.edu.sa/ (H, current in 2026 because it advertises the 1448 mutun competition). Add: the site no longer says "free certificates and sometimes rewards" in a way I could confirm; I could not verify "financial rewards".
7. **Mutun al-Ijaz.** MESH says 550 SAR a month, checked Oct 2025. A Telegram post found by search quotes 400 SAR for the full course with a start of 31 Aug 2025, which suggests either different packages or a changed price. Mark as unverified and older than a year. Source: https://t.me/s/mutunalijaz1?before=53 (snippet, L). English site mutunalijaz.com could not be opened.
8. **Ahlul Athar.** Details (13 levels, 350 SAR) are from March 2026, plausible. Warning: arabic.ahlulathar.net is a different, India-based Zoom course using Madinah books (rupee pricing, Urdu instruction). Do not link it as the Madinah institute. Source: https://www.arabic.ahlulathar.net/ (H). The Madinah institute opened April 2025 per Study in Saudi: https://x.com/study_in_saudi/status/1914323407530778699 (snippet, M).
9. **Al-Fusha Academy.** I could not find an "Al-Fusha Academy" for Arabic as a second language. alfusha.edu.sa is Al-Fusha Private Schools (nursery to secondary, girls and boys, Al-Muhazzar, admissions 0148426244, acc1@alfusha.com). Study in Saudi's Telegram list names "Fusha" with fees in a Drive folder. Fix: ask the author of programs.ts what the source was, and rename or link the correct institution. Source: https://alfusha.edu.sa/ (H, says site under development), https://t.me/s/notjustmadinah/565 (undated, M).
10. **Taba Institute.** Official site is "معهد كفاءات طابة العالي للتدريب" (Kafa'at Taba Higher Institute), address Al-Eskan, phone/WhatsApp +966 53 275 0874. Its 2026 homepage shows no fees or schedule, so the "400 SAR a month, 1,000 SAR for three months" figure cannot be confirmed from the site; keep the Oct 2026 date but say the source is the Study in Saudi video. It also lists a Scientific Foundation programme (up to 12 months after level 4), teacher, translation and media diplomas, and group programmes. Source: https://ktabaa.com (H, © 2026).
11. **MAP price.** programs.ts says "From £2,699 (international)". The MAP site itself says prices are not published and vary by course length and country of residence. Winter 2027 intensive is 21 Jan to 20 Feb 2027 (80+ hours), booking open; enrolment notice dated 15 Jan 2026. The site says flight and visa are excluded from the package but also that MAP helps arrange flights and visa; MESH's step 4 ("ask MAP about help") is right. The page also calls the 21 Jan to 20 Feb run a "Ramadan intensive" and "Winter 2027 intensive". Ramadan 1448 starts about 9 Feb 2027, so only the last part of the course overlaps Ramadan; MESH's "Ramadan intensive" label is imprecise. Source: https://map-arabic.com/ (H, 2026).
12. **Mishkat, Taibah Markaz, Ma'rifah (sisters).** All dated 1 July 2026 in programs.ts but mostly rest on a December 2023 guide. I could not find any official 1448 announcement for any of the three. Marefah Academy ("أكاديمية معرفة", Jam'iyyat Ma'rifah) pages show an Arabic programme for non-Arabic-speaking women called "بداية الأثر", with teaching, books, follow-up and transport, in Madinah (Al-Iskan, opposite Abdul Latif Al Sheikh Mosque, phones 0148461300, 0548645000, 0535050343, email marefah1437@gmail.com). So location "Hayy al-Qaswa" may be out of date. Mishkat's published address in a 2023 directory is Al-Aridh phone 3852 7579 and its official channel is instagram @meshkcec (L). Sources: https://store.marefah.org.sa/marefah-academy/p/161741 (H, undated), https://www.marefah.org.sa/pages/أكاديمية-معرفة/ (H, undated). Downgrade the "checked" date or label them "2023 reports".
13. **IU "Taibah" confusion.** "Taiba Institute for Arabic and Islamic Studies" (Facebook taibaarabic) and Taibah University's Arabic-for-non-natives unit are separate from Markaz Taibah. Make sure the Taibah University link does not get mixed up.
14. **Study in Saudi portal text in the IU entry.** "Choose up to 25 programmes across 10+ universities" and "stipend 840 SAR" were not re-verified here. The latest real window I found: 19 Apr to 21 May 2026 (scholarship track), and IU distance-learning intake for the second semester of 1448 closing 31 Oct 2026. Sources: https://ummid.com/news/2026/4/22/madinah-university-admission-2026-begins-steps-link-to-apply.html (Apr 2026, M), https://iu.edu.sa/elc/announcements/02-2 (H via search, 2026).

Language: not stated (Arabic with support; MESH says Madinah Books). Register: map-arabic.com/registration/. Contact: WhatsApp +44 7939 993613, map-arabic.com/contact-us/, UK office 124-128 City Rd, London EC1V 2NJ.

## 10 most valuable findings

1. The 1448 Haram Institute and Madinah branch call ran 5 to 20 Safar 1448 (20 Jul to 4 Aug 2026), not Dhul Qa'dah as MESH says.
2. Non-Saudis need a valid iqama and sponsor consent for the Haram Institute; visitors cannot join.
3. MAP does not publish prices; the £2,699 figure needs its source confirmed. Winter 2027 intensive is 21 Jan to 20 Feb 2027.
4. Bayinat Institute publishes prices (1,000 and 2,000 SAR a month) and has separate women's, men's and children's classes.
5. Ahlul Athar's other website is an India-based Zoom course, not the Madinah institute.
6. "Al-Fusha Academy" seems to be Al-Fusha Private Schools, which has no Arabic-for-foreigners page; the sisters' Arabic claim needs re-sourcing.
7. Haram halaqat registration runs through qm.edu.sa and reg.qm.edu.sa; programmes include the 8-level Mutun Talib al-Ilm series and a Mutun competition in Sha'ban 1448.
8. Qortoba's Madinah programme is suspended, and Al-Isbaah's fifth session began 13 Oct 2026 (men only, no visa help).
9. Taibah University's international route is Study in Saudi only (age 17 to 24, non-residents), with a paid external track; last official call found was May to June 2025.
10. The best live duroos feed is t.me/drosalmadina (live Mixlr and YouTube links), alongside the Presidency schedule page prh.gov.sa.
