### 1.1 Electronic university courses (distance): second-semester 1448 intake

| Date | Item | URL |
|---|---|---|
| 19 Oct 2026 | Applications open to non-Saudi students, men and women, who live outside Saudi Arabia, for the electronic university courses in the second semester of 1448 | https://iu.edu.sa/announ144859 |
| 21 Oct 2026 | The six steps to apply, with the closing date of Saturday 31 October 2026 | https://iu.edu.sa/news14480510 |
| 1 Nov 2026 | The window is extended to Saturday 7 November 2026 | https://iu.edu.sa/announ1448521 |
| (linked) | Admission guide PDF for 1448 | https://snip.sa/IU_CoursesGuide |

What the University says, in summary:
- **Who can apply:** non-Saudis, men and women, **who do not live in Saudi Arabia**. Other conditions: a secondary-school certificate or its equivalent, recognised by the University; never dismissed from an institution for academic or disciplinary reasons; good Arabic, with an Arabic proficiency placement test for anyone whose previous study was not in Arabic; paying fees on time. Admission is provisional until the documents are checked.
- **Where:** only on the distance-learning portal, `distancelearning.iu.edu.sa` (the "apply" link in all three posts resolves there). The University has no offices or agents anywhere, and applicants must apply themselves.
- **Steps:** (1) open the portal and check the conditions; (2) create a student account; (3) pay the application fee, which activates the file and is **not refundable**; (4) the University reviews the file and issues a University number on acceptance; (5) choose as many courses as you want, meeting any prerequisites; (6) pay for the chosen courses to confirm registration.
- **Fees (VAT included):** 230 SAR application and file-activation fee, non-refundable; 230 SAR for the Arabic placement test where needed, non-refundable even if you fail it; 230 SAR registration fee each semester; 230 SAR per credit hour.
- **Seven specialisations:** Qur'an, Hadith, Aqeedah and Da'wah, Sharia, Arabic language, Economics, Regulations. Teaching is in Arabic, and each course lasts one semester.
- **Certificates:** a pass certificate for each course; a micro-certificate for a short programme of two or more related courses; on request, an associate diploma, intermediate diploma or bachelor's by accumulation. A degree by accumulation needs a comprehensive exam taken **in person**, a cumulative GPA **out of 5** (if a course was repeated, the student picks which passing attempt counts), and approval by the equivalency and supervisory committees and the University Council.
- **Selection:** applicants are ranked on their secondary-school grade.
- **Equipment:** a computer with a camera and microphone, with the browser and software the University specifies. Exams taken on a phone or tablet do not count.
- **Grants:** donor bodies may fund places for some students, depending on free seats and the donor's own criteria.
- **Contact:** students use Tawasul; others use the guest form at `helpdesk.iu.edu.sa/newticket_guest`, or email `elc@iu.edu.sa`.
- **Timeline:** applications ran from Monday 19 October to Saturday 31 October 2026, extended to Saturday 7 November. Review, the list of candidates and final admission follow "as announced on the portal".

Compared with MESH (`guides-admin-online-2026-11.ts`, slug `studying-online-at-the-islamic-university`):
- **Already covered:** the seven specialisations; 230 SAR per credit hour plus a 230 SAR registration fee each semester; certificates per course, micro-certificates, then diplomas or a bachelor's; the in-person exam at the end of a bachelor's; exams online on a computer with the camera on; elc@iu.edu.sa and the helpdesk guest form.
- **Conflict 1, where to apply.** MESH says: "Apply on the University's official website, iu.edu.sa." The official guide says: «التقديم عبر البوابة الإلكترونية الخاصة بالتعليم عن بعد (distancelearning.iu.edu.sa)», and «لا يتم استقبال طلبات القبول إلا عن طريق البوابة الإلكترونية المخصصة لذلك». MESH points to the general site, while the official guide names a separate distance-learning portal as the only place to apply.
- **Conflict 2, GPA.** MESH says (first sign-in section, and the common-problems table): "courses earn certificates one by one, so no GPA is worked out there" and "My GPA and current semester show a dash: Normal in the electronic courses." The official guide says a degree by accumulation needs «احتساب المعدل التراكمي من (5) نقاط». The dashboard dash can stay, but the guide should not say no GPA is ever worked out: one is calculated, out of 5, when a student applies for a diploma or bachelor's.
- **New (not in MESH):**
  1. Eligibility: non-Saudis **living outside Saudi Arabia only**. A student or family member living in Madinah cannot take these courses. This is the most useful missing fact.
  2. The 230 SAR application fee, separate from the semester registration fee and not refundable. The guide's research note listed refunds as an open question; for the application fee, this answers it.
  3. The Arabic placement test and its 230 SAR fee for applicants whose previous study was not in Arabic.
  4. Applicants are ranked on their secondary-school grade, and they need a recognised secondary certificate.
  5. Phones and tablets do not count for exams. MESH says to use a computer but not that an exam taken on a phone is invalid.
  6. Grants from donor bodies may cover some e-course students too. MESH mentions donor grants only for the Sharia bachelor's.
  7. No offices or agents, and applicants apply themselves. MESH says this for the scholarship application but not for distance study.
  8. Women are admitted: every post says «الطلاب والطالبات». The handbook research (`iu-pdfs-2026-11/distance-study-handbook.md`, line 88) lists "whether sisters are admitted" as open, and this settles it.
  9. Intake timing: this intake was for the second semester and ran 19 October to 7 November 2026. The research note listed "the application dates and portal page" as left out pending the owner; both are now published.
- **key-dates.ts:** the window closed on 7 November, so there is nothing to add now. When the next intake is announced, it can go in as a `minor` `deadline` row (as the existing ta'jeel and drop-and-add rows do).

### 1.2 Skills Record (السجل المهاري) launched

| Date | Item | URL |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Oct 2026 | The Deanship of Student Affairs launches the Skills Record | https://iu.edu.sa/news14480519 |

The Skills Record is one verifiable electronic record of courses, workshops, seminars, lectures, conferences, volunteering and competitions. Students see the Deanship's programmes there, register and book a seat, have attendance confirmed after the event, and each activity is added with its hours and a verification code. The platform issues electronic certificates with a QR code that an employer can check without an account. The registration link resolves to `https://services.iu.edu.sa/student-skill-record`.

Compared with MESH:
- **Already covered:** FAQ `skills-record` (`faqs-aiakhee-2026-11.ts`) and the volunteering guide in `guides-lane-e-2026-11.ts` give the platform, the address, registration, the 10-minute attendance rule and the 75% pass rule.
- **New:** certificates carry a QR code that employers can check without an account, and each entry records the hours and a verification code. Conferences and competitions also count.
- **Partly outdated:** `guides-student-life.ts` says "Clubs and activities: run from the Deanship's first floor. You join through each club's president or group." Club events are now announced and booked on the Skills Record. This is not a direct conflict, but the line should point to it.
