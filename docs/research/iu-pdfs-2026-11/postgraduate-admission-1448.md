# Postgraduate Admission Guide for the 1448 Academic Year (Islamic University of Madinah)

Official University document: "دليل القبول في برامج الدراسات العليا للعام الجامعي 1448 هـ", prepared by the Deanship of Graduate Studies (عمادة الدراسات العليا), 37 pages, posted 28 October 2026. It covers higher diplomas, master's and PhD programmes at the main campus in Madinah. All 37 pages were read from the extracted text; the fee table (pages 34 to 36) and the enrolment documents table (page 26) were checked against page images rendered from the PDF, because the text extraction scrambled their columns. Page numbers below are the printed page numbers, which match the PDF page numbers. A second official document, the postgraduate admission guide for 1446 ("الدراسات العليا 1446هـ", Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research, 19 pages, all read, with the programme list on pages 17 to 18 checked as images), was used only for points the 1448 guide does not cover and for the "Changes since 1446" list. Findings were compared with the live guide at https://medinahub.org/guides/postgraduate-admission-1448 (fetched 9 November 2026) and with the newer, not yet published seed version of the same guide in `src/server/seed/content/guides-research-2026-11.ts`.

## 1. Summary

The 1448 guide sets out: how to contact the Deanship and which channels count as official notice; warnings about agents, qualifications and employers; the applicant's commitments (including the 300 SAR fee); general conditions to apply and to be accepted; special conditions for every department (grades, previous specialism, Qur'an memorisation, written exam, interview, Qiyas aptitude test and English tests); the enrolment documents for Saudis and non-Saudis; which categories of applicant can apply and who counts as an "expected graduate"; the weighting used to rank applicants in every college; the full list of 60 programmes with per-semester and total fees; and the support services for each type of student. It contains no application dates.

The live MESH guide is accurate on everything it covers: the fee table (all 60 rows checked), the 300 SAR fee, the two portals, the general conditions, the qualification notes and the benefits. Its gap is depth: it sends readers to the Arabic PDF for programme conditions and says nothing about ranking, enrolment documents, which current scholarship students are allowed to apply, or the extra tests in science, engineering and computing.

**The 10 most valuable findings for MESH readers**

1. **Current IU scholarship students can apply through the portal only if they are expected to graduate before the programme starts. A scholarship student who has already graduated cannot apply, except by a special decision of the University President for those who graduated after the start of the first semester of 1447 and whose scholarship is still valid.** (page 27)
2. **Applicants from outside Saudi Arabia with no current scholarship are ranked on 50% previous GPA and 50% personal interview (computing: 50% GPA, 30% interview, 20% English test), and are exempt from the written specialist exam.** (pages 9 to 33)
3. **Every college has its own ranking formula; most theoretical programmes use 40% previous GPA, 30% written specialist exam and 30% interview.** (pages 30 to 33)
4. **Qur'an memorisation is a condition in several departments: Qira'at needs the whole Qur'an; Tafsir 5 juz for a master's and 10 for a PhD; Hadith sciences 4 and 8 juz; Fiqh as-Sunnah 3 and 5 juz (both tested at 80 out of 100); Da'wah 1 juz for the diploma, 3 for a master's and 5 for a PhD.** (pages 13 to 15)
5. **The pass mark for the written exam and interview is 60 out of 100 in most departments, but 70 in Fiqh as-Sunnah, Hadith sciences and the three education PhDs.** (pages 14 to 18)
6. **Linguistics and Literature and Rhetoric need only a "good" (جيد) bachelor's grade in Arabic; most other master's and PhD programmes need "very good" (جيد جدا).** (pages 15 to 16)
7. **Science, engineering and computing master's need an English score (TOEFL PBT 450, TOEFL iBT 45, IELTS 4, or STEP 60; IELTS 4.5 for cybersecurity) no older than two years, a GPA of 3.50 out of 5 or 2.50 out of 4 in engineering and computing, and a Qiyas aptitude score of 60, from which external scholarship students are exempt.** (pages 21 to 25)
8. **To be enrolled, a non-Saudi submits: certificate, transcript, iqama copy, passport copy, the signed and dated admission agreement, aptitude test certificates where required, Saudi embassy attestation of a foreign certificate, and an English certificate where required.** (page 26)
9. **The share of seats for each category (external scholarship, internal scholarship, Saudis) is no longer fixed; the Standing Committee for Graduate Studies sets it according to how many applicants from each category pass.** (page 33)
10. **The higher diploma in Guidance and Counselling is open only to external scholarship students.** (page 18)

### 2.18 Changes since 1446

From the 1446 guide (pages 1 to 19), compared with 1448:

- **Fees:** in 1446 every regular programme was free and only the 300 SAR application fee was charged (1446 page 4). In 1448 most programmes charge tuition.
- **Age limit:** 1446 capped external scholarship applicants at 30 for a higher diploma or master's and 35 for a PhD (1446 page 5). 1448 states no age limit.
- **Grade as a number:** 1446 defined "very good" as at least 3.75 out of 5 or 2.75 out of 4 (1446 page 5). 1448 gives grades as words per department, with numeric GPAs only for engineering and computing.
- **Qiyas aptitude test:** 1446 required 60 in the General Aptitude Test for every applicant, with GRE accepted by science colleges (1446 page 5). 1448 requires it only in science, engineering and computing, with external scholarship students exempt, and drops the GRE options (1446 had GRE quantitative 130 for engineering and computing, GRE verbal plus quantitative 265 for science).
- **Ranking:** 1446 ranked theoretical programmes on 30% exam, 40% GPA, 30% aptitude, and science on 50% GPA, 50% aptitude, with ties going to the higher aptitude score (1446 page 15). 1448 uses the interview instead of the aptitude test and sets a formula per college.
- **Seat split:** 1446 fixed 60% of seats for external scholarship, 20% for internal scholarship and 20% for Saudis (1446 page 15). 1448 lets the Standing Committee set the split by the number who pass in each category.
- **Graduated scholarship students:** 1446 let external scholarship students who graduated in 1445 apply if they had never applied before, and those who graduated after the start of the 1445 first semester with a valid scholarship (1446 page 13). 1448 accepts only expected graduates, with graduates admitted only by special decision of the President.
- **Minimum class size:** 1446 opened a section only with at least 10 students (diploma), 6 (master's) or 4 (PhD), unless the Standing Committee made an exception (1446 page 15). 1448 does not mention this.
- **Education master's:** 1446 required external scholarship students to hold the department's General Educational Preparation Diploma (دبلوم الإعداد التربوي العام) (1446 page 8). 1448 does not mention it.
- **Media and Communication:** 1446 limited all levels to scholarship students (1446 page 8). 1448 does not.
- **Computing English waiver:** 1446 waived English for nationals of English-speaking countries, students taught in English at bachelor's level, or those who undertook to submit it in the first year (1446 pages 10 to 11). 1448 lists no waiver for Data Science or Cybersecurity, and sets IELTS 4.5 for Cybersecurity.
- **Science English waiver:** the undertaking moved from "within the first year" (1446) to "before the thesis proposal" (1448).
- **Programmes:** 1448 adds the Arbitration diploma (Judicial Studies) and the Fiqh as-Sunnah master's; it drops the Computer Science master's; the 1446 "Security Technologies (Cybersecurity)" master's appears in 1448 as the Cybersecurity master's (1446 pages 17 to 18; 1448 pages 34 to 36).
- **New in 1448:** written exam and interview pass marks per department, Qur'an memorisation tests in Da'wah, Fiqh as-Sunnah and Hadith, the enrolment documents table, the donor-funded seats rule, and a Minhati portal for applicants outside the Kingdom.
- **Deanship name:** 1446 was the "Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research"; 1448 is the "Deanship of Graduate Studies". Contact details are unchanged.

## 5. Questions for the owner

1. **Dates:** the 1448 guide gives no application dates. The pending seed lists "16 November to 11 December 2025" (master's and PhD) and "5 to 11 April 2026" (diplomas). These need another source before publishing; they are not in either PDF.
2. **Instalments:** the pending seed says tuition can be paid in instalments through the University's payment partner. Neither PDF says this.
3. **Who pays tuition:** the guide says the first semester's fee is paid "by the targeted category" on paid programmes, and promises a section on fee-exempt categories that is missing. It does not say whether external scholarship students sponsored by the University pay tuition on paid programmes (their benefits list a "study place scholarship", منحة المقعد الدراسي). Confirm with the Deanship before MESH tells scholarship students they will or will not pay.
4. **Age limit:** 1446 capped external scholarship applicants at 30 (diploma, master's) and 35 (PhD). The 1448 guide is silent. The Minhati portal may still apply a limit; confirm before MESH says there is none.
5. **Minimum class size:** 1446 opened a programme only with 10 diploma, 6 master's or 4 PhD students. Not repeated in 1448; leave it out unless confirmed.
6. **Education master's preparation diploma:** 1446 required external scholarship students to hold the General Educational Preparation Diploma for education master's. 1448 drops it; confirm before telling readers it is no longer needed.
7. **Interview for Tafsir and some Arabic programmes:** the conditions and ranking for Tafsir, Linguistics, Literature and Rhetoric and History list no interview for applicants inside Saudi Arabia. Applicants from outside are still ranked 50% on interview. Section 4.3 states this as written; confirm if MESH wants to say "no interview" outright.
8. **Arabic Language Skills diploma:** it is in the fee table and ranking (free, 50% GPA, 50% exam) but has no published conditions. Who it is for is unknown.
9. **Mechatronics English condition:** page 22 repeats the English condition twice (items 4 and 5, identical). Treated as a duplicate.
