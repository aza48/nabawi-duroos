# Guide to the study system at the Islamic University (distance learning): the Student Information System, step by step

**What the source is.** A student compilation, not an official University document: "Guide to the study system at the Islamic University (distance)" (دليل نظام الدراسة في الجامعة الإسلامية (عن بعد)), prepared by the student supervisors of the electronic university courses (مشرفي تفاعلية المقررات الجامعية الإلكترونية). Its opening page says plainly that it is a purely student effort and does not represent the University. It was shared in the students' forum on 8 November 2026; the screenshots show the first semester of 1448 (ف1448/1) and a Tawasul footer dated 2026, so it is current. 60 PDF pages (cover, intro, two index pages, then pages numbered 1 to 56). Printed page n is PDF page n + 4.

**Who it is for.** Written for students of the **electronic university courses programme** (نظام المقررات الجامعية الإلكترونية عن بعد), with some pages added for students of the **distance Sharia bachelor's** (بكالوريوس الشريعة عن بعد). The screenshots come from a distance student's account (college shown as E-Learning Center (مركز التعليم الإلكتروني), specialisation "electronic course" (مقرر إلكتروني), study type "distance" (تعليم عن بعد)). It is **not** written for on-campus degree students, but the systems it shows are the same ones on-campus students use: the unified sign-in at iu.edu.sa, the **Academic System** (النظام الأكاديمي) with its five top menus, **Tawasul** (نظام تواصل) and **Blackboard** ("Zidni" e-learning management, إدارة التعلم الإلكتروني زدني). Where a screen behaves differently for distance students, this file says so.

**Screens it covers.** Unified sign-in and account menu; the student dashboard (بياناتي الطلابية); Tawasul new request; Academic System: Electronic Registration (Instructions and guidance, Courses offered in the plan, Registered courses, Course results, Drop and add), Personal (Update name in Arabic and English, Personal information, Absence, Send email, Official documents, Nationality and birth details request, E-payment), Academic (Academic record, Previous academic movements, Student plan, Passed and Remaining courses in the plan, Course evaluation, Final exam excuse, Enter academic movements, Course withdrawal, Clearance, Contact the academic advisor); Blackboard on computer and phone (login, institution page, activity, courses, course content, calendar, grades, tools, settings, forgotten password); FAQs on the two distance systems; University contact emails.

**How I read it.** The extracted text (p04.txt) in full, then every screenshot page that carries a menu, form or system message, rendered as images and read on screen: PDF pages 1, 6 to 12, 15, 16, 18 to 30, 32 to 37, 39, 40, 42, 53 and 60 (printed 2 to 8, 11, 12, 14 to 26, 28 to 33, 35, 36, 38, 49, 56). The other pages (intro, index, section dividers, payment tips, the Blackboard tour on printed 39 to 48, the FAQ pages 50 to 55) were read from the extracted text, which carries all their wording; their screenshots add no menu labels beyond what the text names. No page was unreadable. Screen labels below are copied from the screenshots, which sometimes differ from the authors' captions (for example the screen says **تحديث الاسم** "Update name" where the caption says "change name").

## 1. Summary

The guide walks a distance student through every screen of the University's online systems: signing in at iu.edu.sa, the dashboard, changing the password and mobile number, raising a Tawasul request, registering courses (drop and add, with the **Confirm** (تثبيت) step), paying fees by card, evaluating courses, reading results, the academic record and study plan, absence, emailing a lecturer, official documents, correcting name, nationality and birth details, academic movements (ta'jeel, i'tithaar, withdrawal), final exam excuses, the academic advisor, and Blackboard. It closes with FAQs about the two distance programmes and a list of University emails.

**The 10 most valuable findings for MESH readers**

1. **You cannot see your results, including your coursework marks, until you fill in the course evaluation** under Academic, then Course evaluation (تقييم المقررات). The results screen itself says so in red. (Printed p18, p19.)
2. **The full Academic System map**: top menus Electronic Registration (التسجيل الإلكتروني), Personal (شخصي), Academic (أكاديمي), Travel Management System (نظام إدارة السفر), Graduation procedures (اجراءات التخرج), with every submenu item named. (p11, p14, p29.) MESH has no single page that lists them.
3. **Drop and add only takes effect after you press Confirm (تثبيت)**; until then the course sits under "Unconfirmed actions" (إجراءات غير مثبتة). (p12.)
4. **The academic movements screen spells out the visa and housing consequences for international students**: one semester of ta'jeel or i'tithaar means an exit and re-entry visa, leaving the hall at once and returning a week before the next semester; more than one semester or withdrawal means final exit, clearance and leaving within two weeks. (p36.) MESH currently says "ask Travel Management".
5. **A ta'jeel or i'tithaar request changes nothing until your academic status changes, and you must keep attending until it does.** (p36.)
6. **The academic record screen is marked "not for official use"** (هذا السجل لا يستخدم للأغراض الرسمية); official papers come from Personal, then Official Documents, which needs pop-ups allowed in the browser. (p25, p28.)
7. **Sign-in uses a verification code shown on the screen** (رمز التحقق, with a refresh button), typed in after the University number and password; Nafath is the other option. (p4.) MESH says "a code sent to you".
8. **Final exam excuses have their own screen**: Academic, then Final exam excuse (تقديم الأعذار عن غياب الاختبار النهائي), opened after the finals, with a tracking link. (p31.)
9. **Absence per course, with lateness and percentage, is under Personal, then Absence** (الغياب); the Academic System figure is the one that counts, not Blackboard's. (p3, p5, p23.)
10. **Name and nationality corrections are self-service with uploads**: Arabic name exactly as in the passport, four parts, no spaces or hyphens in the family name; nationality or birth details need the passport and the new iqama in one PDF. An Arabic name added after the graduation document is issued costs a replacement-document fee. (p21, p26.)

1. **blackboard-login and university-offices-and-contacts.** MESH says: "with your University number, password and the code sent to you". The sign-in page shows a verification code (رمز التحقق) on screen, with a refresh button, which the student types in. Change the room row to "Hand it back once your status changes" for both, and add the visa rules (draft in section 4). Also add that the request takes effect only when the status changes, and that you keep attending until then.
3. **exams-results-and-attendance.** MESH says the academic record shows "your GPA and the courses still left in your plan".
