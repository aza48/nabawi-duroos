# Bachelor's Admission Guide for Full and Partial Scholarship Students, 1448 AH

Official Islamic University document: "دليل القبول لمرحلة البكالوريوس لطلاب المنح الدراسية الكاملة والمنح الجزئية (منحة خارجية ومنح داخلية) للعام 1448 هـ", posted by the University on 10 May 2026, published by the General Administration of Communication and Corporate Identity (iu.edu.sa). 12 pages, all read: the extracted Arabic text in full, plus each page rendered to an image to check the two tables (page 6 programmes, page 9 weighting), the allowance list (page 5) and page 2 (a basmala cover with no text). Page 1 is the cover, page 11 has the Study in Saudi QR code, page 12 is the back cover with the University's site and social accounts. Compared against the live MESH pages applying-to-the-islamic-university, campus-life-islamic-university, ask/how-to-apply-iu, ask/is-housing-included, ask/stipend-amount, ask/defer-my-place and graduating-from-mahad (fetched 9 November 2026), and against the unpublished seed rewrites in `src/server/seed/content/guides-research-2026-11.ts` and `guides-2026.ts` (the applying-to-the-islamic-university rewrite, the is-housing-included and stipend-amount FAQ texts, and the new scholarship-stipend-and-allowances guide, which returns 404 on the live site).

## 1. Summary

The guide is the University's own admission handbook for international bachelor's applicants for the 1448 year. It covers: a short history of the University; what campus offers; the two scholarship types (full or external, partial or internal) and exactly what each pays and does not pay; the full table of colleges, programmes and study lengths; the admission conditions; warnings (no transfers between colleges, Saudi-school graduates, English for the scientific colleges, no agents, email as the official channel); the Arabic placement test; the weighted-score formula used to rank partial scholarship applicants; the declaration students sign; the document list; and how to apply through Study in Saudi.

Ten most valuable findings for MESH readers:

1. **A partial (internal) scholarship covers tuition only: no stipend, no graduation allowance, no housing, no tickets and no living services.** The live MESH pages still say all "scholarship students are housed by default". (Page 5)
2. **A full (external) scholarship pays a monthly stipend, two months' stipend on arrival (بدل تجهيز), three months' stipend on graduating for shipping books (بدل تخرج), and a discounted economy ticket at the end of each academic year.** The live stipend FAQ mentions none of the allowances. (Page 5)
3. **The yearly ticket is only given if the student has not received a travel ticket from another party, and it is by the nearest route to the student's home.** Not on MESH anywhere. (Page 5)
4. **Applicants must provide two scholarly recommendation letters (تزكيتين علميتين).** The live applying guide asks for one and says the portal marks it optional. (Pages 7, 10)
5. **Full table of programmes and lengths: Islamic, Arabic, Law and Business colleges 4 years; Engineering and Computer colleges 5 years; Science 4 years; the Arabic Institute's Intermediate Diploma 2 years and Associate Diploma 1 year.** Not on the live site. (Page 6)
6. **Scientific colleges (Engineering, Computer, Science) teach in English and require IELTS, TOEFL or an equivalent from external applicants whose earlier study was not in English or whose country does not use English as a main language; Islamic and Arabic colleges teach in Arabic.** Not on the live site. (Page 8)
7. **Scholarship students cannot transfer to another college after admission: the college and programme they are nominated for is final.** Not on the live site. (Page 8)
8. **Graduates of schools inside Saudi Arabia are treated as internal applicants even if they apply for an external scholarship, and must submit the General Aptitude Test (القدرات), plus the Achievement Test (التحصيلي) for scientific colleges.** Not on the live site. (Page 8)
9. **Partial scholarship applicants are ranked by a weighted score: theoretical colleges 50% secondary certificate and 50% General Aptitude Test; scientific colleges 30% secondary, 30% General Aptitude and 40% Achievement Test, then by order of preferences and seats available.** New to MESH. (Page 9)
10. **The Arabic placement test is only for full scholarship applicants the University chooses to test, and its result decides whether a student starts in the Arabic programme or goes straight to college; Arabic study is a preparation stage the University assigns, not a track a student picks.** (Pages 6, 9)

## 3. Conflicts with MESH

1. **Partial scholarship housing.** Live is-housing-included says "Yes. Scholarship students are housed by default". The same sentence appears in the live applying-to-the-islamic-university and campus-life-islamic-university. The University states partial (internal) scholarships include no housing, tickets or living services (page 5). Change: say full (external) scholarship students are housed and partial scholarship students are not. The seed text of is-housing-included already does this: publish it, and fix the same sentence in the two live guides.
2. **Partial scholarship money.** Live stipend-amount describes the stipend without saying partial students receive nothing, and programs.ts (iu-madinah overview) calls the second type "partly funded", which implies some funding. The University states a partial scholarship includes no stipend, graduation allowance or other payment (page 5). Change: publish the seed stipend-amount text; in programs.ts replace "partly funded" with "partial (free tuition only)".
3. **Recommendation letters.** Live applying-to-the-islamic-university asks for "A recommendation (tazkiyah)" and says "The portal marks it optional". The University makes a recommendation a condition of admission (page 7) and lists two scholarly recommendations as a required document (page 10). Change: ask for two letters and drop the "optional" line. The seed rewrite already says "Two recommendation letters (tazkiyah)": publish it.
4. **Medical report wording (seed only, not live).** The seed rewrite says "a medical report showing no infectious disease" and "free of infectious disease". The University asks for a report confirming the applicant is healthy and free of diseases and disabilities (page 10). Change the seed to "a medical report showing you are in good health" (owner to decide how to word the disabilities part; see section 5).
