## Guides

### `what-to-bring-to-madinah` (stage getting-started)
"What to bring to Madinah: documents, money, clothes and medicines"

Key facts and sources:
- Plug type G, 230 V, 60 Hz; country moved to 230 V and type G in 2010 and kept 60 Hz. https://houseofsaud.com/travel/saudi-power-plugs/ ; https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plug-voltage-by-country/saudi-arabia/ ; https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/guidance/advice-for-you/when-travelling/travel-adaptor-for-saudi-arabia/ (several agree; one outdated page says 110/220 V).
- Controlled medicines need the SFDA traveller's permit through the Controlled Drugs System (cds.sfda.gov.sa); prescription or medical report uploaded; trade name, active ingredient, strength and quantity per drug; in force from 1 November 2025. https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/news/4292351 ; https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2378719 ; https://my.gov.sa/en/news/26017 ; https://www.sfda.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2025-08/CDS-en.pdf ; https://saudi.klm.com/information/bulletin-board/mandatory-medication-clearence-for-sa
- Foreign prescriptions not accepted, bring a doctor's letter: same as existing FAQ `foreign-prescriptions` and `doctors-hospitals-and-pharmacies`.
- Prohibited goods (alcohol including duty free, pork products, narcotics); restricted goods declared at the port customs office; cash, gold and jewellery above the limit declared. https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-individual/Prohibited-goods.aspx ; https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs_individual/Travel_pages/declare.aspx ; https://houseofsaud.com/travel/saudi-customs-rules/ . The limit itself (60,000 or 40,000 SAR) conflicts, so no number is given: https://saudilifeguide.com/saudi-arabia-cash-declaration-limit/
- Riyal pegged at 3.75 to the US dollar (SAMA peg, also quoted in group-chat-2026.md).
- Passport six months: tourist eVisa rule in `visas-for-family-and-visitors`; returning-student visa rule in study-in-saudi-telegram.md (#245, #363).
- Border number for SIM, Absher and bank: `sim-internet-bank`, `living-on-a-visitor-id`.
- Long-term educational visa needs medical insurance: https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/VisaOptions (community-2026-11-09.md A1).
- Weather by season: `seasons-and-camping` and living-in-madinah.md 2.9 (weatherspark). Conservative dress norm: living-in-madinah.md (medinacampsconsulting, Sep 2026). Thobe tailoring 100 to 300 SAR in 2 to 4 days: `everyday-apps-and-services`.
- Shoes on shelves at the Haram: `masjid-an-nabawi-first-visit`.
- Attestation is written as advice only, because whether IU requires embassy-certified originals is an existing open question in scripts/open-questions.json.

### `exams-results-and-attendance` (stage study)
"Exams, results and attendance at the Islamic University"

Key facts and sources:
- 75% attendance; past 25% absence barred from the final with DN; college may lift a bar on an accepted excuse only if 50% attended: 1445 Undergraduate Student Guide (https://iu.edu.sa/Students/Guidelines), iu-official.md; restated in IU's welcome post to new students 18 Aug 2025 (factcheck-2026-11-09-university.md B1); Common First Year charter https://iu.edu.sa/com11 (search summary: over 25% gives DN; excuses within a week, accepted by the college). Group chat agrees on "past 25%, not at 25%".
- File closed after 20 consecutive days absent; reopened once within four semesters: 1445 guide (iu-official.md).
- Final exam timetable under Academic, then Final Exams, live 8 Nov 2026: factcheck-2026-11-09-university.md A18. Published a few weeks before: group-chat-2026.md (4 posts).
- Finals dates 29 Nov 2026, 25 Apr 2027, summer 27 Jul 2027: official 1448 calendar poster https://iu.edu.sa/Students/AcademicCalendar ; `key-dates.ts`.
- Exam-day rules (10 minutes early, no entry after 30 minutes, no leaving in first 30, one to three hours): 1445 guide.
- Grade scale A+ 5.00 to D 2.00, pass mark 60; honours bands and conditions: 1445 guide. Points for F and DN deliberately not given.
- Results: e-services "نتائج المقررات" and "الاستعلام عن نتائج المقررات" (final exam results, login needed), "استعراض السجل الأكاديمي", "الاستعلام عن المقررات المتبقية في الخطة": https://eservices.iu.edu.sa/ServiceDetail/st-course-results ; https://eservices.iu.edu.sa/ServiceDetail/st-course-result ; https://eservices.iu.edu.sa/ServiceDetail/st-academic-transcript ; https://eservices.iu.edu.sa/ServiceDetail/st-remaining-courses . Stamped documents under Personal, Official Documents: `campus-life-islamic-university`.
- Missing a final: zero in the exam, grade from coursework; substitute exam by end of week two of next semester: 1445 guide. Excuse call each semester: https://iu.edu.sa/announ14471216 (2 Jun 2026), contact 920022042, care@iu.edu.sa.
- Appeal to the college dean within 15 days of the result: 1445 guide. The same 15 day window is used at King Saud University (https://dar.ksu.edu.sa/ar/ReCorrect), consistent with the national regulation. No re-sit other than the substitute exam: Saudi undergraduate system.
- Course withdrawal limits (two a semester, six in total, not in year one, not graduation semester, not when barred): 1445 guide. Window 20 Dec 2026 to 13 Mar 2027: official calendar.
- Dismissal after three consecutive semesters below the graduation minimum, or programme length plus half; one extra chance: 1445 guide. Extra chance window 11 to 14 Dec 2026: official calendar.
- The 2.75 graduation GPA from the 1445 guide was left out pending confirmation.
