# Fact-check, 9 November 2026: Masjid an-Nabawi and everyday-life guides

Scope: Part 1 is the Prophet's Mosque and the city's sacred sites; Part 2 is the everyday-life guides and FAQs in `src/server/seed/content/guides-2026.ts`. This builds on `docs/research/living-in-madinah.md` (7 Nov 2026) and does not repeat its rows unless something new turned up. Everything is paraphrased. Dates are the page's own "last updated" or publish date where visible. Confidence: High means an official page read in full, Medium means official but partial or several agreeing secondary sources, Low means a blog, a single source, or a conflict.

How the research was done, and its limits:
- The Haramain Authority site (alharamain.gov.sa, English and Arabic) was read in full. This is the General Authority for the Care of the Grand Mosque and the Prophet's Mosque. Its service pages carry "last update" stamps from July to August 2025 and its FAQ from 5 August 2025.
- Read directly: haj.gov.sa (Rawdah permit page), amana-md.gov.sa (Madinah municipality), ekram.amana-md.gov.sa (al-Baqi' portal), madinahbus.mda.gov.sa, sar.hhr.sa, rulebook.sama.gov.sa.
- Could not read: nusuk.sa (HTTP 406), wmn.gov.sa (does not resolve), X and Telegram accounts, Saudi Gazette (402), Sabq (403), most SPA English pages (headline only), pr.gov.sa, visa.visitsaudi.com (page text did not render), the Jawazat fee page, stc, Mobily and Zain contact pages, the CHI page body. Where those were the natural source, the finding says so and the confidence is capped.
- Search results were used to find pages, but a claim is marked High only when the page itself was read.
