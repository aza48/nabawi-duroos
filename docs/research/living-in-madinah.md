Compiled 7 November 2026. Everything below is paraphrased. Dates are the source's own publish or "last updated" date where one was visible; "no date" means the page showed none. Confidence: High = official or several agreeing sources; Medium = one decent source or small disagreement; Low = marketing blog, conflicting sources or old.

Sites that blocked or failed: aqar.fm (403), imtilak.sa (403), expatistan.com (403), expatexchange.com (403), travel.state.gov and sa.usembassy.gov (403, read through search summaries only), x.com (402), asmu.info home page (403, but asmu.info/Pages/* worked). ## 0. Biggest thing the site does not mention: the security situation (Oct to Nov 2026)

MESH has no safety or alerts content, and the situation changed sharply in the last weeks.

- Saudi Arabia and the Houthis are exchanging strikes. A strike on Riyadh's King Khalid airport on 10 Oct 2026 reportedly killed at least 13 people and injured about 300. The Saudi-led coalition said it destroyed a Houthi ballistic missile fired toward the Madinah region on 5 Nov 2026 (no damage or casualties reported). The Houthis deny targeting Madinah and say they only hit oil and military sites. Yanbu (west coast, about 3 hours from Madinah) has had repeated warnings. | https://english.aaj.tv/news/330479484/saudi-air-defence-downs-houthi-missile-over-madinah ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/25/saudi-arabia-houthi-missile-drone-interceptions.html | 5 Nov and 25 Oct 2026 | EN | Medium to High (several outlets agree on the Madinah interception; details come from the coalition)
- The US ordered non-emergency embassy staff and families to leave Saudi Arabia (effective 23 Oct 2026), kept the country at Level 3 "Reconsider travel", and suspended routine consular services in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dhahran. | CNN https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/21/politics/us-orders-departure-embassy-staff-saudi-arabia ; embassy pages sa.usembassy.gov (blocked, read via search summary) | 21 Oct to 7 Nov 2026 | EN | High
- Practical advice repeated by the embassy: enrol in your government's traveller alert scheme, check flights with the airline, follow Saudi Civil Defense alerts, and do not film or post interceptions or impact sites, because people have been detained for it. | same | 7 Nov 2026 | EN | Medium (embassy page not opened directly)
- Airports: Riyadh partly reopened after the strike; Jeddah and Dammam reported delays; a Houthi claim about Dammam airport on 5 Nov was unverified. I found no confirmed Madinah airport (MED) closure. | https://english.news.cn/20261106/72ee29c823a547c99f3303a3f31eade0/c.html | 6 Nov 2026 | EN | Low to Medium
- Suggestion: add a short, dated "Safety and alerts" guide (see section 3) that links only to official sources and tells people to switch on alerts, and keep wording calm and factual. Re-check before publishing; this is moving daily.

## 4. Top 10 findings (also given in the final message)

1. Security: Houthi strikes and a missile the coalition says it intercepted over the Madinah region on 5 Nov 2026, US ordered departure, Level 3 advisory. MESH has no safety content.
2. Bus fare is 3.45 SAR (airport route 400 is 11.50), cash is not accepted, passes 10, 60 and 240 SAR.
3. Haramain base fares are 162 economy and 455 business Makkah to Madinah before 15% VAT; kids 50% off; luggage 25 kg; route 300 bus links the station and the Haram since 1 Oct 2026.
4. MESH's UK e-Waiver, US 10-year and EU business visa lines are outdated; the tourist eVisa is SAR 535 online with built-in insurance, and its stay rule (90 days total or per visit) is disputed.
5. Visit visa extensions are filed by the host on Absher, about 100 SAR, 180-day cap, plus a 30-day exit grace period since June 2025.
6. Visitor bank accounts: SAMA rule needs Saudi address, home address and purpose of visit, ends with the visa, and Alinma lists branch-only applications.
7. Ejar: landlords must now register leases since 25 Sep 2025 (the freeze is Riyadh only); Madinah rents are annual with post-dated cheques.
8. Rents confirmed by 2026 data: 1-bed about 2,000 and 3-bed 2,150 to 2,870 (Numbeo, 2 Oct 2026); 2-bed MESH ceiling should rise to about 4,500; family budgets may reach 8,000 to 16,000 including schools.
9. stc visitor SIM: 100 GB for 190 SAR with branch activation; fingerprint and border number needed; home internet without an iqama means a prepaid 5G router (70 GB 138 SAR, unlimited 391 SAR).
10. Student families: the old "Masters and PhD only" family-permit rule (2017) conflicts with a Jan 2025 note about a third-semester route, and student iqamas cannot sponsor family visit visas.
