# `graduate-procedures`: Leaving Madinah for good (written 9 November 2026)

Same slug and stage (`leaving`), new title and summary. The ten University steps of the 9 November rewrite are kept (their sources are in `docs/research/legacy-rewrites-2026-11-09.md`) and the guide now also covers what the owner asked for: the final exit, the ticket, shipping books, selling furniture and the car, the bank account, SIMs, the flat and its deposit, utilities, and the children's school papers, with a timeline and a last-week checklist.
