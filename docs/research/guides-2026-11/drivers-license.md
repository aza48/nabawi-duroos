## READY-TO-PASTE DRAFT

```html
<p>Checked 9 November 2026. Whether you can drive, and with which licence, depends on your status. Visitors and students on a Visitor ID drive on their home licence. Iqama holders must hold a Saudi licence, and get it either by swapping a recognised foreign licence or by training at a driving school. Women follow exactly the same rules and fees.</p>

<h2>Who can drive on what</h2>
<table><thead><tr><th>Your status</th><th>What you drive on</th><th>For how long</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>Visit visa, tourist eVisa or Umrah visa</td><td>Your valid home licence, with an International Driving Permit if the licence is not in Arabic or English</td><td>One year from the day you entered, or until the licence expires, whichever comes first</td></tr>
<tr><td>Student on a Visitor ID (educational visa)</td><td>Your valid home licence and an International Driving Permit</td><td>The same one year from entry. A Saudi licence and a car in your name both need an iqama</td></tr>
<tr><td>Iqama holder, licence from a recognised country</td><td>Swap it for a Saudi licence without a test</td><td>Start within the first weeks after your iqama is issued</td></tr>
<tr><td>Iqama holder, other countries</td><td>Train at an approved driving school and pass the tests</td><td>Plan for several weeks from assessment to card</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Always carry the original licence, not a photo. The vehicle class on your licence must match the car. Rental companies ask for a licence of at least a year's standing, a passport or iqama, a credit card, and usually an International Driving Permit from visitors.</p>
<blockquote><p>Tip: An International Driving Permit is a translation of your licence, not a licence in itself. It is valid only with the original. Permits issued in Gulf countries are not accepted in Saudi Arabia.</p></blockquote>

<h2>What it costs</h2>
<table><thead><tr><th>Item</th><th>Cost (SAR)</th><th>Paid to</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>Private licence fee, 40 a year</td><td>80 for 2 years, 200 for 5, 400 for 10</td><td>Government fees in your banking app (Sadad)</td></tr>
<tr><td>Medical check (eyes, blood group, general)</td><td>100 to 200</td><td>The approved medical centre</td></tr>
<tr><td>Certified Arabic translation of your licence</td><td>50 to 100</td><td>A certified translation office, often fully online</td></tr>
<tr><td>Driving school training, if you need it</td><td>From about 700 for a short course to about 2,800 for a 30-hour beginners' course</td><td>The school, after its assessment sets your hours</td></tr>
</tbody></table>

<h2>Route 1: swapping a recognised foreign licence (iqama holders)</h2>
<p>Licences from the Gulf states, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the EU countries, Switzerland, Norway, Japan, South Korea and South Africa, among others, are swapped without a driving test. Absher shows whether your country qualifies when you book. Your foreign licence must still be valid.</p>
<ol class="steps">
<li>Have your licence translated into Arabic by a certified office. Online offices send a signed, stamped PDF within a day or two.</li>
<li>Do the medical check at an approved centre. The centre tests your eyes, takes your blood group and does a short general check, then uploads the result to the Traffic Department's Efada system. You receive an SMS when it is in. Take your iqama and a passport photo.</li>
<li>On absher.sa (the website works better than the app for this) open Appointments, then Traffic, then Replacement of driving licence (استبدال رخصة القيادة), and choose the Model Driving School (المدرسة النموذجية) in Madinah. Upload the translation and both sides of your licence as PDFs.</li>
<li>Pay the licence fee in your banking app under Government payments, Traffic, Driving licence, Issue licence: enter your iqama number, choose private and the number of years.</li>
<li>Go to the school at your appointment with the original licence, the translation, a photo and your iqama. The school checks everything and issues the card. The school keeps your foreign licence in most cases, so photograph both sides first.</li>
<li>Your new licence appears in Absher under My Services, Traffic, Driving licences, and the card is handed to you or sent to your National Address.</li>
</ol>
<blockquote><p>Note: People outside the school offer to book your slot for a small fee. They ask for your Absher password or the code sent to your phone. Never share either. Book it yourself on Absher.</p></blockquote>

<h2>Route 2: the driving school (all other countries, and beginners)</h2>
<ol class="steps">
<li>Do the medical check as above.</li>
<li>Register at an approved school in Madinah through Absher (Appointments, Traffic, Driving school), or at the school itself. The Model Driving School is in Al-Aqool on Airport Road and has a separate women's section with female instructors; it takes bookings and questions by WhatsApp and sends the fees and available dates.</li>
<li>Sit the school's assessment: a theory check and a short drive. The result sets your training hours, from a few hours for experienced drivers to a 30-hour course for beginners.</li>
<li>Complete the hours, then the computer theory test and the practical test. Free practice tests for the theory exam are online in English.</li>
<li>Pay the licence fee as in Route 1 and collect your card.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Sisters</h2>
<p>Women have driven in Saudi Arabia since 2018 and the licence is identical. The Model Driving School's women's section in Madinah trains women separately with female instructors, the medical centres have women's sections, and the licence swap in Route 1 works the same way: the appointment is at the school's women's section. A husband or mahram is not needed for any step.</p>

<h2>Renewing, losing and reading your licence</h2>
<ul>
<li>The licence cannot outlast your iqama. Renew it in Absher (Traffic, Renew driving licence) after a fresh medical check; the fee is the same 40 SAR a year.</li>
<li>A lost or damaged card is replaced in Absher (Traffic, Replacement of lost licence) for a fee shown before you pay.</li>
<li>A number printed under restrictions means a condition, for example glasses or contact lenses must be worn.</li>
<li>If you held a Saudi licence before and came back on a new iqama, transfer it to the new iqama in Absher instead of starting again.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Cars, insurance and fines</h2>
<ul>
<li>Buying or registering a car in your name needs an iqama. The steps, the ownership transfer in Absher and the periodic inspection are in <a href="/ask/buying-a-used-car">Buying a used car</a>.</li>
<li>Third-party insurance is compulsory and is checked electronically; comparison sites show every insurer's price for your car in one search.</li>
<li>Fines appear in Absher under Violations and can be paid through Sadad. Unpaid fines block iqama renewal, exit and re-entry visas and the final exit visa. See <a href="/ask/traffic-fines">Traffic fines</a>.</li>
<li>Petrol: 2.18 SAR a litre for 91 and 2.33 for 95.</li>
</ul>
```
