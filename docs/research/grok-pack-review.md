# Review of the Grok data pack (November 2026)

The owner shared a pack Grok compiled from X and the web. It is mostly general. What we kept, and what we did not trust:

## Agrees with our own research (safe to use)
- Madinah Bus fare 3.45 SAR; airport route 400 is 11.50 SAR.
- Haramain economy from about 162 SAR plus VAT; the station is about 9 km from the Haram.
- Stipend about 840 to 900 SAR; apply only through studyinsaudi.sa or iu.edu.sa, never through paid agents; distance learning portal distancelearning.iu.edu.sa.

## New and useful (check before publishing)
- Rawdah through Nusuk (reported from an October 2026 announcement): men 2 am to Fajr and 11:20 am to Isha; women after Fajr to 11 am and after Isha to 2 am. Fridays: men 2 am to Fajr and 9 am to Isha; women after Fajr to 9 am and after Isha to 2 am. One regular booking per 365 days, plus "Instant Access" slots near the mosque.
- Delivery apps: HungerStation (HPlus subscription), Jahez (Prime), Keeta (frequent free delivery), Mrsool (any errand), Ninja, The Chefz.
- More Telegram duroos channels: dr_elbukhary (Abdullah al-Bukhari, active) and Duroos_alhramain (active). Both are now watched.
- Places to visit and markets: useful as a checklist for a future "Places in Madinah" guide.

## Not trusted
- "Iqama issued soon after arrival": true for most current students, but the newest intake arrives on a digital Visitor ID with no iqama (group chat 2026). Guides must cover both paths.
- "Free healthcare at the University hospital", "arrival allowance of two months", "subsidised meals about 90 SAR": no source given; verify first.
- Prayer times in the pack are approximate and partly wrong (Asr). The site computes its own.
