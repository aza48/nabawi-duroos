## Q2. "Shaykh Maher al-Qahtani" at Masjid Dar al-Hijrah

Slug: `maher-al-qahtani` (lessons.ts id `hijrah-qahtani-tawhid`).

Answer: the teacher is **Shaykh Maher ibn Dhafir al-Qahtani** (الشيخ ماهر بن ظافر القحطاني), the same scholar the positions file names. He comes to Madinah each month for this lesson. The Madinah branch of the Ministry of Islamic Affairs runs the lesson through its Da'wah and Guidance Centre. He is based in Jeddah, not Makkah: his weekly lessons are at Jami' al-Malik Sa'ud on Madinah Road, al-Sharafiyyah, Jeddah, and at Jami' al-Malik Fahd, al-Aziziyyah, Ta'if.
