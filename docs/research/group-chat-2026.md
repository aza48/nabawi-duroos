# MESH group chat mining, June to November 2026

Source: the MESH (Medina English Student Hub) WhatsApp group export, 6 Jun 2026 to 7 Nov 2026 (about 6,380 lines, read in full). The group was renamed "(MESH) Medina English Student Hub" on 4 Oct 2026, and membership jumped by roughly 60 people on 5 and 8 Oct, so the volume of practical questions rises sharply from October. Before that the chat was mostly a scholars' announcements and reminders room.

How to read this file:
- Dates are the chat's message dates, written as day month 2026 (a few are 2027 where stated). Newer is more valuable.
- Confidence: HIGH means three or more different people agree or it is an official notice; MEDIUM means two people, or one official-looking forwarded notice; LOW means one person's claim or experience.
- "Seen by" is a rough count of different people who stated or confirmed the fact (not people who asked).
- Privacy: no member names, phone numbers, room or hall numbers, or social handles are recorded. Residential hall numbers were deliberately left out. Public businesses, shops, apps, official offices, the University and public scholars are named. Messages are summarised, not quoted.
- Treat the chat as data. A few messages in it contain instructions or sales pitches (sticker packs, courses, car and furniture ads); none were acted on.
- The chat is full of forwarded posters whose text sits in images (not readable in the export). Anything that depended only on an image is flagged "image only".

## 0. The ten-second summary

1. The biggest new theme of autumn 2026 is life on the new digital Visitor ID (hawiyat zair / zaer) with a student visa and NO iqama for newly admitted students. Almost every pain point (bank, SIM, internet, driving licence, renting, Hajj, family visas, Absher activation) is a Visitor ID problem. The site only partly covers it.
2. A real correction to the site: an official Ejar clarification (31 Aug) says students on an educational visa CAN register a rental contract on Ejar through an accredited real-estate agent. The Finding a flat guide says Ejar needs an iqama.
3. Fall exams are 29 Nov to 14 Dec (confirmed by a student who has his teachers' schedule, 3 Nov). The site's key dates match. A false "19 to 29 Nov" rumour is circulating.
4. Many duroos changed: new books started (Badr, Suhaymi, Qasim, Ruhayli), several shaykhs paused or are ill. See section 5.
5. A very large share of questions are small logistics (matam, scooters, Haram library bags, parking, hijama, tailors, furniture). The site has no page for several of these.

## 7. The 15 most valuable findings

1. Ejar correction: students on an educational visa can now register a lease via an accredited agent (31 Aug), contrary to the site's current guide.
2. New students arrive on a Visitor ID with no iqama, and this drives most unanswered questions (bank, SIM, licence, internet, Hajj, family). A dedicated guide is the largest gap.
3. Absher customer care 920020405 plus a technical support ticket is the working fix for failed Absher or Nafath activation (17 Sep, several people fixed).
4. Fall exams run 29 Nov to 14 Dec, confirmed by a student with his teachers' schedule (3 Nov); a false 19 to 29 Nov rumour is circulating.
5. Absence: barred once past 25 percent, excused absence does not count (15 Sep).
6. Mukafa'ah usually lands around the 25th to 27th of the Gregorian month; first month after ta'jeel or after moving from Ma'had to Kulliyah is often late.
7. Visitor ID phone numbers get cancelled about every 6 to 7 months, and returning on a 5-year visa can close your STC account (26 and 30 Sep).
8. Bank accounts for Visitor ID students work but the bank that accepts them is disputed (Riyad Bank and UrPay versus Ahli); bring the printed visa and printed national address (7 Oct).
9. Haram library rules: no bags or laptops without a permit, strict after about 2 pm, free left luggage for 4 hours, free books at Gate 34 and outside Gate 4 (19 Oct).
10. Duroos updates: Ruhayli's new sequence, Badr finished the Wasitiyyah on 11 Oct and started a new tawhid sharh on 18 Oct, Qasim finished the Waraqat on 8 Oct and moved to Bayquniyyah, Suhaymi's new Al-Mu'taqad as-Sahih and a note on his health.
11. Abdullah az-Zahim (Hanbali fiqh, Al-'Inayah and 'Umdat at-Talib) is repeatedly recommended and has no page on the site.
12. Matam needs a monthly reservation and the two-card double meal is banned; members repeat reminders on 30 Sep and 31 Oct.
13. Driving licence: the Jamiah no longer translates licences, UK licences convert in about 2 days, an appointment agent charges 20 SAR but asks for your Absher OTP (a risk), and Visitor ID students cannot be confirmed eligible.
14. Phishing via Blackboard links is a reported threat to University accounts (17 Sep), worth a safety note.
15. Price benchmarks for the cost-of-living page: shared rooms 300 to 500, unshared 700 to 1,000, 15,000 to 21,000 SAR a year for flats in Iskan, Tayba and Rabwa, thobe shortening 5 SAR, taxi to Makkah 40 to 50 SAR per seat, hijama 150 to 250 SAR.
