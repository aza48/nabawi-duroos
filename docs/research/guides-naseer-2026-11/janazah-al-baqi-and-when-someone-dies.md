### Repatriation (practical steps)
All from embassy guidance, which agree with each other: UK FCDO pack, US Embassy, Indian Embassy Riyadh (https://www.eoiriyadh.gov.in/page/disposal-transportation-of-mortal-remains/). Embassy no objection certificate; next of kin consent (power of attorney / written consent); Civil Affairs death certificate; Jawazat exit for the deceased after fines are cleared; embalming; airlines want several (US: five) certified copies. "Sealed coffin": Arabic sources (dalielsaudi, makkahnewspaper on the coffin) and air-ambulance pages; low risk.

## Conflicts and how they were settled
- **Who registers a resident's death:** some blogs say Jawazat, others Civil Affairs. my.gov.sa says Civil Affairs through Absher for citizens and residents, so the guide says Civil Affairs.
- **Old in-person route** (sponsor takes hospital and police reports to MOH, then MOI; saudi-expatriates.com 2015, lifeinsaudiarabia.net) is superseded by the electronic hospital report and Absher. Left out.
- **The 30 SAR Civil Affairs fee and police clearance** in a 2024 pilgrim's account (theharamainguide.com) are single, unofficial and possibly outdated. Left out.
- **4,000 SAR for preparation, coffin and embalming of a body flown abroad** (dalielsaudi.com, quoting media) is unofficial. Left out.

## Open questions for the owner (left out of the guide)
1. **Cost.** Are transport, washing, shrouding and burial in al-Baqi' or another Madinah cemetery free when arranged through the municipality (Ekram) or the charities? No official page states a fee or that it is free.
2. **Who may be buried in al-Baqi'.** An Ekram page lists an approval letter, burial permit, proof the person lived in Madinah, and IDs. Does that apply to every burial, or only to deaths outside Madinah (Balady has an FAQ on Emirate approval for that case)? Are visitors on a visit visa buried in al-Baqi' or sent to another cemetery?
3. **The janazah slot at Masjid an-Nabawi.** How does a family get the body onto a given prayer: does Ekram set it automatically when it books transport, or does the family ask? Does the Ekram home page list the janazahs due at each prayer (a search summary says so)?
4. **Women at the janazah.** Confirm that sisters pray the janazah from the women's areas (the guide says "men and women pray it from their own prayer areas"). Delete the line if unsure.
5. **Closing the iqama.** What exactly does Jawazat need to close a deceased dependant's or student's iqama, and must the deceased resident's dependants leave the Kingdom? (A Jawazat clarification reported by visitsaudia.net says dependants on the deceased's file must leave: not confirmed on an official page.)
6. **A visitor who dies** (family on a visit visa): who acts as "sponsor" for Jawazat and the certificate, and does the host have any duty?
7. **What the University does** when a student dies: does Student Affairs or the Deanship contact the family, help with burial or repatriation, issue the death paperwork as sponsor, pay any remaining stipend or graduation-type allowance, or return belongings? Nothing official found.
8. **Repatriation cost and time** from Madinah, and whether insurance (the student's or the visitor's) covers any of it.
9. **Washing by the family.** Can family members take part in the washing at the municipality's mortuary (the guide stays silent; it is also partly a fiqh matter).
10. **Verify the five (*) official pages** in a browser that can reach them: my.gov.sa/en/services/152960, balady.gov.sa request-transport-and-preparation-deceased, amana-md.gov.sa NewsCenter/Details/51, spa.gov.sa/w1842672, and ekram.amana-md.gov.sa (and allow those hosts in the session's network settings for future agents).
