## Conflicts and how they were settled

1. **Who runs Madinah's slaughterhouses.** The brief says municipal slaughterhouses under Madinah Municipality (amana-md.gov.sa). Every 1447 (2026) report names the Madinah branch of the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture as the operator of the 17 slaughterhouses and the source of the price list. The municipality's slaughter news is older (2020 temporary slaughter points; 1440 and 1444 permits for restaurants and kitchens through Balady). The guide follows the 2026 reports and names the Ministry.
2. **"17 slaughterhouses" vs "17 slaughter halls in 6 main slaughterhouses".** SPA (N2602318) words it as 17 halls across 6 main slaughterhouses; the readiness report and most outlets say 17 slaughterhouses in the region, 6 main ones in the city. The guide uses the readiness wording.
3. **Adahi udhiyah price.** Some secondary sites call 720 SAR "the price of the hady and udhiyah voucher"; the official SPA and Saudi Gazette items name it only as the Hajj hady voucher. The guide gives 720 SAR as the hady price and says the udhiyah price is shown at purchase.
4. **Sheep prices.** No 2026 Madinah-only figures were found. Secondary sites gave national ranges (1,400 to 2,000 SAR, Ehsan breed prices of 450 to 1,300 SAR) that could not be verified. The guide uses GASTAT's official national averages for May 2026 and labels them as Kingdom averages.
5. **Street slaughter fine.** Egyptian results (prison, fines) do not apply and were ignored. The Saudi figure comes from the official municipal penalties table. The row sits in the commercial activities chapter under "general violations" and names the violator or licence holder as responsible; the guide states the violation and the fine range without saying who is usually fined.
6. **Eid al-Adha crowding at the Haram.** Only a member report says it is lighter; the guide grounds it in the fact that pilgrims are in Mina on Eid day and keeps the claim comparative (less crowded than Eid al-Fitr).
7. **Private sector start date.** A blog gave 25 May 2026; Argaam and HRSD say the holiday starts on the Day of Arafah. The guide gives the rule (four days from Arafah), not a 1448 date.
