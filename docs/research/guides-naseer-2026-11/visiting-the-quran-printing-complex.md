## Conflicts and how they were settled
- **Visiting hours.** Several schedules exist: January 2026 SPA (N2480859/N2480960) had evenings until 9 pm; an older SPA item said 6 am to 10 am and 4 pm to 7 pm; Visit Madinah and an X reply from 2023 give 8 am to 11:30 am Sunday to Thursday (with Saturday evening too); Ramadan 1447 had 9 am to 12 noon and 9 pm to 1 am. The March 2026 and June 2026 SPA announcements agree with each other, and June 2026 is the newest, so the guide uses it. Ramadan and Hajj timetables are mentioned without times.
- **Translation count.** 74 (Al Jazeera, 2021), 78 (SPA, Saudipedia), "55 translations in 39 languages" (English Wikipedia, old). Guide says "more than 70".
- **Annual output and totals.** 10 to 18 million a year, 286 to 361 million in total depending on source and date. Left out.
- **Sales hours.** The sales policy gives collection 7:30 am to 1 pm; the marketing and sales page gives Sunday to Thursday 4 pm to 7 pm; the translations and books sales pages say 7:30 am to 10 pm. Left out: the guide tells readers to confirm the collection time on WhatsApp.
- **Sales kiosks.** An official page says the Quba and Miqat kiosks closed on 14/9/1444 AH; the Tan'im kiosk in Makkah is still listed. Left out as not current enough to trust.

## Open questions for the owner (left out of the guide)
1. Is booking compulsory, or are walk-ins still let in? Travel reviews disagree. The guide says visits are by appointment, which matches the official app's description.
2. Is there a separate booking path for groups (a halaqah, a class, an Umrah group) versus individuals? Only a third-party guide (visitalmadinah.com, 2 Sept 2026) describes entering other group members' details.
3. Sisters and families: are there any separate times, routes or rules for women? One travel site (Rehlat) says women cannot enter some parts; others call it family friendly. Nothing official found.
4. Children: any minimum age or rules? Nothing official found.
5. Photography: allowed or not, and where? Only travel blogs, which disagree. The guide just says ask the staff.
6. How long does a visit take? No official figure. Reviews range from "very short" to about an hour including queueing.
7. Distance from the Islamic University campus. Not found from a reliable source, so left out.
8. Does any city bus route (Madinah Bus) stop at the Complex? Not found, left out.
9. Are the apps, fonts and desktop programs free? Very likely, but no snippet said "free" for the current apps, so the guide does not say it.
10. The font licence (third-party font sites quote a KFGQPC notice: free to use and distribute, not to be sold or modified without written approval). Not confirmed on the official site, left out.
11. Can individuals or centres abroad request free Mushafs (through the Complex or the Ministry of Islamic Affairs)? Nothing official found.
12. Mushaf prices at the Mushaf Services Centre and its collection hours (sources conflict, see above).
13. Is the gift limited to one copy per visitor, and can visitors choose a translation in their language? Not stated.
14. The fonts' official download address (fonts.qurancomplex.gov.sa is cited only by community repositories).
