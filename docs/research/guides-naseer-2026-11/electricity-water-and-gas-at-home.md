## Conflicts and how they were settled
- **Company name / app name.** The brief says "SEC, app Alkahrabaa". The company rebranded to Saudi Energy in Feb 2026 and the app is now listed as "Saudi Energy". The guide uses Saudi Energy and mentions the old names once. `graduate-procedures` already says "Saudi Energy app", so this is consistent.
- **Current water tiers.** Secondary sites disagree (5 tiers 0.10/1/3/4/6 water-only versus 4 tiers 0.15/1/3/6 including sewage). Only the points they all share are in the guide: the first 15 m³ at 0.10 SAR, wastewater at half. The full 2026 ladder is left out.
- **2027 electricity fixed charge ladder.** Only 70 SAR (under 100 A) is in SE's own example; the 80/90/100 SAR steps come from one secondary site, so the guide says "more for larger breakers".
- **Emergency number for gas leaks.** The `emergency-numbers` FAQ lists Civil Defence as 998, with 911 working in Madinah. Civil Defence's own 2026 statements say to call 911 in Madinah, so the guide uses 911.
- **Bills in cost-of-living.** Water 30 to 70 SAR a month will no longer hold after 1 Jan 2027: at 1 SAR/m³ plus 30 SAR fixed charges, even 15 m³ comes to 52.50 SAR before VAT. Not changed here (another guide's area); flagged for the owner.

## Open questions for the owner (left out of the guide)
1. **Visitor ID and Saudi Energy.** Can a Visitor ID holder (no iqama) be the beneficiary of a meter, by the Ejar Financial Liability Transfer or by Add account in the app? No official source found either way. The guide only says that anyone can pay a bill by account number, and that without the transfer the account stays with the owner.
2. **Current electricity meter fee amounts** (meter reading, maintenance and billing fee by breaker size, in force until 31 Dec 2026). Not published in anything I could read.
3. **2027 breaker ladder** above 100 A (80, 90, 100 SAR?) needs SE's official table.
4. **Full current water tariff ladder** (to 31 Dec 2026) and whether NWC prices are before VAT.
5. **Gas delivery apps covering Madinah.** Gasable, Gas App (gas.wallet.com.sa), Gas Time, Quick Gas and Souq Gas exist, but none confirmed coverage of Madinah in 2026. The brief asked for an alphabetical list; it is left out until coverage is confirmed. Group members could name the ones they use.
6. **Price of buying a new cylinder** (rather than a refill) and of a first gas connection. One chat mentions 240 SAR for a first installation; single source.
7. **Water tanker prices in Madinah** (19 t and 30 t). Secondary sites give 114 to 135 SAR plus VAT for 19 t and 225 SAR for 30 t (2022); the guide says the price shows in the app before payment.
8. **SADAD biller code for Saudi Energy** (002 in secondary guides) and whether bank apps now show "Saudi Energy" or still "Saudi Electricity". Guide avoids the code.
9. **cost-of-living water range** should be widened from January 2027 (see conflicts).
10. **University halls rule on gas cylinders.** The 2018 "Your Safety First" inspections removed gas cylinders from rooms; current rule unconfirmed, so the guide covers city flats only.
