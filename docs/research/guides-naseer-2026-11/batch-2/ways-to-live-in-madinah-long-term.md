## Conflicts and how they were settled

1. **Premium Residency fees.** MESH's `visas-for-family-and-visitors` (guides-2026.ts line 409) says "about 100,000 SAR a year, or 800,000 SAR once". Arab News (Jan 2024), the earlier fact-check (`docs/research/factcheck-2026-11-09-haram-and-daily-life.md` row A3.6) and many private advisers agree on those figures, plus a 4,000 SAR application fee for the five newer products. But no official page (pr.gov.sa, sa.gov.sa, my.gov.sa, SPA, the law) confirmed the amounts this session: official-domain searches in English and Arabic returned the products and the law, never the fee. The task said to leave fees out unless an official page confirms them, so the guide says only that each product has its own government fee shown on the portal. **The two guides now differ in detail; the owner should decide whether the visas guide keeps its figures.**
2. **"A visit visa cannot become residency" vs children's conversion.** Jawazat says visit visas do not convert to iqamas, yet MESH's visas guide documents moving children of two iqama-holding parents from a family visit visa to an iqama at Jawazat. Settled by saying you cannot turn a visit visa into **your own** iqama, and naming the children's exception with a link.
3. **"Ownership gives no residency" vs the Real Estate Owner product.** The property guide's line is true of ordinary ownership; a home worth 4 million SAR or more can qualify for the Real Estate Owner Premium Residency, granted by the Center, not by the purchase. The guide says "Buying an ordinary home gives no residency" and lists the product separately. The Saudistics notes (proposal 5b) suggest adding the same line to the property guide; that is lane E's file and was not edited.
4. **Saudistics video "Premium Residency renewable up to 5 years".** Not stated on an official page reached; the regulations' indexed text says "one year or more, renewable". The guide uses the official wording.
5. **Gomedine source "4,000 SAR a year" for Premium Residency** (gomedine part 1, item 9): wrong per every other source; it is the one-off application fee of the newer products. Not used.

## Open questions for the owner (left out of the guide)

1. **Premium Residency fees:** 100,000 SAR a year (Limited) and 800,000 SAR once (Unlimited), plus the 4,000 SAR application fee for the other five, and any fee for Real Estate Owner, Investor, Entrepreneur, Talent and Gifted. Needs a session with `pr.gov.sa`, `sa.gov.sa` and `my.gov.sa` allowed, then a line in the Premium Residency section and the table.
2. **Premium Residency details left out:** minimum age (21 in private sources), health insurance, the Investor threshold (7 million SAR in private sources), the Entrepreneur funding thresholds, Special Talent's 5-year term, whether holders pay the dependant fee (a private source says they do not), and the "2% yearly discount" claim. "No sponsor" rests on news coverage and the law's design rather than a quoted official line.
3. **Can a dependant work?** Whether a wife on her husband's iqama can take a job (through a work permit, Ajeer or a transfer to an employer) was not confirmed, so the guide only says a dependant iqama is not a work iqama.
4. **Family visa conditions for a worker:** any minimum salary or list of professions eligible to bring a family (a private source mentions about 200 eligible professions). Left out.
5. **Labour Law Article 40(1)** (employer pays recruitment, iqama and work permit fees): stated from the long-standing law text, not re-fetched this session. Re-check at laws.boe.gov.sa or hrsd.gov.sa.
6. **Expat levy of 800 SAR a month** and the December 2025 cancellation for licensed industrial establishments: kept at 800 for consistency with the company guide; the current amount was not confirmed on an official page this session.
7. **Students working:** whether a student on the educational visa (Visitor ID) or a student iqama may take any paid work with University permission. The guide says "no paid work outside the scholarship".
8. **After graduating:** whether an IU graduate can move from University sponsorship to an employer inside the Kingdom rather than leaving on final exit. Not covered.

## Review

Applied after the facts, rules and site review (November 2026).

### Changes
- **Property in Madinah and Makkah (Premium Residency):** the old "right of use only" line was out of date. The Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law has been in force since 22 January 2026 (REGA notice, SPA https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2496274), and its zones were approved on 23 June 2026. The line now says a Muslim holder can own inside the approved zones and holds a right of use only outside them, matching `owning-property-as-a-non-saudi` (guides-lane-e-2026-11.ts line 18). The source row "Holders: right of use only in Makkah and Madinah" above is superseded.
- **Students working:** "No paid work outside the scholarship" replaced with "Ask the University before taking any paid work" (see open question 7).
- **Expat levy:** now "700 or 800 SAR a month", per the HRSD levy rules (https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2021-11/24112021.pdf): 800 when foreign workers outnumber Saudis, 700 when they do not.
- **University family residency:** the "since January 2025" date removed from the table and the Studying section. The closure stays (students' reports); the start date conflicts with the repo's own Telegram research.
- **Work visa steps:** removed "You give fingerprints at a visa centre abroad". The text now says the work visa is issued while you are abroad, then the medical and fingerprints are done here.
- **Family of a worker or company owner:** the table and the bullet now say you can apply for dependant visas once your iqama is issued, if your recorded salary meets the minimum for family sponsorship, matching the company guide (`src/features/guides/company/content.ts`, FAMILY).
- **Scholarship eligibility:** the 17 to 25 age range now applies to the bachelor's degree only.
- **Visit visa exception:** the children's exception is now limited to children under 18 on a family visit visa whose parents both hold iqamas, as in `visas-for-family-and-visitors`.
- **Dependants link:** relabelled "Family visit visas, and moving children from a visit visa to an iqama", since that guide covers visit visas and not dependant residence visas.
- **Second person:** rewrote the dependants paragraph, the partial scholarship sentence, the company setup sentence, the scholarship and company sentence (which also wrongly said "his iqama" for Visitor ID holders), and the Premium Residency holder lines. Removed the hedge "usually".
- **Intro:** "links to the full guide for each" became "points you to the detail for each", because Premium Residency has no MESH guide.

### Removed facts, now open questions
10. **Start date of the University family residency closure.** A January 2025 Telegram post (#411, community-2026-11-09.md line 68) describes istiqdam as open with a 10,000 SAR deposit, a November 2025 post quotes 30,000 SAR, and closure reports date from January, May and October 2026. No official source gives a date.
11. **Students and paid work (replaces open question 7).** Outside reports and study-in-saudi-telegram.md line 173 say the educational visa launched in January 2024 allows part-time work. No official text was found either way, so the guide states neither answer.
12. **Biometrics abroad for a work visa.** Whether a visa centre (Tasheer) visit is needed depends on the applicant's country, and MOFA's Saudi Visa Bio app takes fingerprints remotely for some visas. Left out.
13. **Family visa conditions (extends open question 4).** The guide now names only the salary minimum from the company guide. The amount, and any profession list, are still unconfirmed.
14. **Postgraduate scholarship age limits.** The University gives full master's and doctoral scholarships to international applicants with different age limits. The limits were not checked and are not stated.
