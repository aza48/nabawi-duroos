## Conflicts and how they were settled

1. **330330 is not a Saudi Central Bank number.** The task suggested "330330 for the Saudi Central Bank if official". It belongs to the Communications, Space and Technology Commission and takes forwarded scam SMS. The guide gives it as that. SAMA's own line is 8001256666, for complaints the bank does not resolve.
2. **SAMACares website.** SAMA's rulebook says the "SAMACARES" system was replaced by a Complaint Management System under SAMA Circular 1185 of 1 July 2025, https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/samacares-announcement-0 . It is unclear whether this affects the public website samacares.sa, so the guide gives only the phone number, which SAMA's own channels still used in 2026, and no website.
3. **1966.** MESH `women-at-masjid-an-nabawi` (guides-research-2026-11.ts line 157) says 1966 is the unified number of the General Authority for the Care of the Two Holy Mosques. The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah's own 2026 news items say 1966 is its Guests of Allah Care Centre. The guide follows haj.gov.sa. **The owner should check line 157 of guides-research-2026-11.ts.**
4. **911 for fake Hajj adverts in Madinah.** The Ministry's 2025 warning lists 911 in Makkah, Riyadh and the Eastern region and 999 elsewhere, without naming Madinah. MESH's `hajj-as-a-student` and `emergency-numbers` say 911 works in Madinah. The guide says 911, consistent with MESH.
5. **Kollona Amn on my.gov.sa.** The old National Portal listing is under a long `wps/portal` URL; the app's publisher is the Ministry of Interior (Public Security). Treated as official.
6. **Concealment fine ceiling.** A 2018 ministry release gives 2 years and 1 million SAR (old law); the 2020 law and releases to December 2025 give 5 years and 5 million SAR. The guide uses the current law.
7. **Broker commission.** The Saudistics notes cite a 2.5% default commission. A REGA-restricted search this session did not find the figure in the law text, so it is left out.

## Review

### Changes made
1. **FAL app removed.** There is no official REGA app called "FAL" (FAL is the licence and REGA's brokerage platform). The broker check now points to rega.gov.sa or REGA's app, Aqari (عقاري).
2. **Ownership date.** "Since January 2026" became "Under the law in force since 22 January 2026". The approved zones and the implementing regulation were only approved on 23 June 2026 (as `owning-property-as-a-non-saudi` says), so the old wording suggested buying inside zones was possible from January.
3. **Ejar 199011.** Body text and table no longer say disputes "go to" Ejar. 199011 is now given as Ejar's contact line for a problem with a registered contract.
4. **Ejar bullets merged.** The two bullets that restated `finding-an-apartment` (SMS approval link, iqama/Visitor ID, mada/SADAD proof of payment) became one scam-focused bullet: register on Ejar, pay only through Ejar, cash or a personal transfer is no proof of payment. The procedure stays in the linked guide.
5. **1966.** The number is no longer attributed to the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah in the body or the table. It is given as the unified number for Hajj and Umrah service problems, which avoids contradicting `women-at-masjid-an-nabawi`, `university-offices-and-contacts`, guides-2026.ts and faqs-aiakhee-2026-11.ts.
6. **Company route for students.** Added that on a University iqama the company route means leaving University sponsorship first, matching the company guide's student note.
7. **Grammar.** "even to someone who says" became "even with someone who says" in the OTP bullet.

### Removed facts (now open questions)
8. The "FAL app" as an official REGA app: removed. If the owner wants a named app, Aqari (عقاري) is REGA's own app; confirm it lists the broker inquiry before naming more of its features.
9. Ejar as the body that decides rental disputes: removed. Official sources give REGA mediation and arbitration, or the courts and enforcement via Najiz. Should the guide name a dispute route?
10. 1966 as the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah's care centre: the attribution is removed from the text. Open question 4 still stands: which body owns 1966, so all MESH pages can name one owner consistently.
11. Detail removed with the merged Ejar bullets (SMS approval link, works on iqama and Visitor ID, mada or SADAD counts as proof): not wrong, but duplicated `finding-an-apartment` and is left to that guide.
