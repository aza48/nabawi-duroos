# Mashayikh: current positions, 12 more teachers of Masjid an-Nabawi (research, November 2026)

Checked: 9 November 2026. Status: complete for the 12 teachers who appear in the official Masjid an-Nabawi lesson schedule (`docs/research/duroos-official-schedule-2026-11.md`) and who were not covered in `docs/research/mashayikh-positions-2026-11.md`.

## Summary

- Covered: 12 teachers, in the order the owner listed them (sections 1 to 12). None of them has a profile card in MESH yet; for all 12 a role line and facts are proposed.
- Every one of the 12 is confirmed as a current teacher at the Prophet's Mosque by the Presidency of Religious Affairs schedule (sittings from 9 to 30 November 2026).
- The titles below follow the Presidency's own wording (أ.د. = Prof. Dr., د. = Dr.).

### Most important findings

1. Dr. Abdullah al-Bu'ayjan: Imam and Khatib of the Prophet's Mosque (imam by royal order of 4/12/1434 AH, 9 October 2013; khatib since October 2016; still delivering the Friday khutbah in August and October 2026), and faculty member in the Department of Judicial Studies, College of Regulations and Judicial Studies, at the Islamic University (IU faculty page).
2. His Excellency Dr. Salih al-Abbud: the "Ma'ali" title comes from his years as President (then called "Director") of the Islamic University of Madinah, appointed by royal order on 3/3/1416 AH (1995); his successor took office on 3/3/1428 AH (2007). Earlier he was Dean of the College of Da'wah and Usul al-Din (1413 to 1414 AH) and head of its Aqidah department. Teacher at the Prophet's Mosque by royal order since 22/11/1416 AH (1996), and still teaching there in October 2026, aged about 90.
3. Prof. Dr. Badr ibn Nasir al-Badr: graduate studies professor and former head of the Department of the Qur'an and its Sciences, College of Usul al-Din, Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University (Riyadh). This is the teacher of MESH's `badrbadr-sequence` lesson (see the schedule file).
4. Prof. Dr. Muhammad Sa'd al-Yubi (Usul al-Fiqh), Prof. Dr. Abdullah al-Zahim (Fiqh) and Dr. Abdulaziz al-Sa'idi (Fiqh al-Sunnah, College of Hadith) are current Islamic University faculty (each has a live IU faculty page).
5. Prof. Dr. Mas'ud al-Muhammadi is a Professor at Taibah University (Department of Islamic Studies), not at the Islamic University; he was named a Friday khatib of Masjid Quba in April 2014 (current status not confirmed).
6. Prof. Dr. Abdullah ibn Muhammad al-Amin is al-Shinqiti, the son of the author of «Adwa' al-Bayan»; his official channel calls him a former Islamic University faculty member and a teacher at the Prophet's Mosque.
7. Prof. Dr. Muhammad ibn Sidi al-Amin was head of the Department of Qira'at at the College of the Noble Qur'an (IU); he is no longer on the College's faculty list.
8. Dr. Ali al-Sudais was an imam of the Prophet's Mosque (around 1416 AH) and is described as a judge at the Court of Appeal in Madinah; the judicial post could not be confirmed from an official source.
9. Shaykh Ibrahim ibn Ibrahim al-Turki and Prof. Dr. Abdulaziz al-Ubayd: no current post found other than teaching at the Prophet's Mosque. Al-Ubayd served as Secretary of the IU Scientific Council (renewed June 2011).

## Method and limits

- All searches and fetches were run on 9 November 2026 (the "checked" date for every item below).
- Reachable from this session: iu.edu.sa (faculty pages and faculty lists), the Presidency's lesson API cpd.prh.gov.sa, midad.com, khzanah.com, saaid.org, al-madina.com, al-jazirahonline.com, alwatan.com.sa, expertsgulf.com, hdaiat.com, t.me public previews, and X posts through the public embed endpoint. Blocked: prh.gov.sa, manaratalharamain.gov.sa, alharamain.gov.sa, spa.gov.sa, okaz.com.sa, sabq.org, alriyadh.com, taibahu.edu.sa, qubaa.mda.gov.sa (and YouTube and Wikipedia rate-limited part of the time). For those, items rest on search-result titles and snippets; an admin opening each linked page once is enough to confirm.
- IU faculty pages show the college, department and specialty, but list only the highest degree (دكتوراه), not the rank. Ranks (Prof. Dr.) come from the Presidency.
- The IU college faculty lists (Qur'an, Shariah, Hadith, Regulations) were read in full. A senior teacher who is absent from his college's list is taken as no longer on the faculty (retired), at medium confidence.
- Dates of X posts are read from the post itself or decoded from the post ID.
- Lessons: from the Presidency API (`https://cpd.prh.gov.sa/api/v1/courses?mosque=madinah`, re-read on 9 November 2026; 212 sittings, 1448-05-28 to 1448-06-19 tabular, about 9 to 30 November 2026). Weekdays are those of `docs/research/duroos-official-schedule-2026-11.md`.
