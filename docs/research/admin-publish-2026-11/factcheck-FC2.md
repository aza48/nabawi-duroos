The forwarded official notice in the Telegram forum says 29/2 to 2/3/1448, and members say it opened on Tue 11 Aug and closed on Sat 15 Aug (c1 #2, #3, #4). `key-dates.ts` says 10 to 30 Aug. Is that the right route for a lost card, and does it take the government replacement fee (1,000 or 2,000 SAR, gomedine part-1 #5) or only the printed-card fee of about 58 SAR? (Same as owner question B2 #6.)
3. **University buses to the Haram.** Are the times posted anywhere students can check (halls, the DSA account)? The guide now says only that times change during the year.
