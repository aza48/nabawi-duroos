# Islamic University of Madinah: official facts for MESH

Researched 7 November 2026. Written in our own words. No dashes used as punctuation.
Confidence key: high = read on an official iu.edu.sa / eduportal / minhatee2 page or the university's own PDF; medium = official news item seen only as a search snippet or via a reliable news outlet; low = third party or inference.

## Limits of this research (read first)

- The 1448 calendar on iu.edu.sa is only a JPEG image (https://iu.edu.sa/media/عمادة القبول والتسجيل/التقويم الأكاديمي 1448.jpeg). I could not read an image, so I could not compare the full year against the official picture. I did read the official eduportal calendar page for semester 1 only.
- The 1445 Undergraduate Student Guide (PDF, 30 pages) is the best primary source for rules. It is the third edition dated 1445 AH, so rules may have been tweaked since. I read its text, not a formal "study and exams regulation", which iu.edu.sa does not list under that name.
- The 1448 scholarship admission guide for internal and external bachelor's students is a web page (https://iu.edu.sa/Students/Guide). I did not manage to read its body. The stipend amount, housing terms, medical cover and ticket rules are therefore NOT confirmed from an official source. Only the Study in Saudi listing says a stipend, accommodation, medical insurance and tickets are included, without amounts.
- x.com returned HTTP 402, so official X posts were only seen through search snippets.
- No official page on buses, clinics, library, sports club prices, housing allocation or exit/re-entry procedures could be read. Gaps are marked below.

### Ma'had and study rules (1445 Undergraduate Student Guide, 3rd edition)

All from https://iu.edu.sa/Students/Guidelines (PDF "دليل الطالب الجامعي، الإصدار الثالث 1445"), Arabic, high confidence. Rules may have changed since 1445.

- Arabic study before the degree is capped at two years, shorter depending on your level. Degree length is four or five years depending on the programme, with two semesters a year and an optional summer semester.
- First year of the programme: no dropping or withdrawing from courses, no ta'jeel or i'tithaar except for compelling circumstances, and no transfer between colleges.
- Attendance: if you attend fewer than 75% of lectures and practicals in a course you are barred from the final and recorded as failed with grade DN. The college may accept an excuse and lift the bar, but only if you attended at least 50%.
- Missing a final exam gives zero in that exam and your course grade is worked out from coursework. With an accepted excuse you can sit a substitute exam, no later than the end of week two of the next semester.
- Exam rules: finals last one to three hours, be inside 10 minutes early, no entry after 30 minutes, no leaving in the first 30 minutes. Cheating is handled under the student conduct code.
- Grade appeal: submit to the dean of the college within 15 days of the result being announced.
- Grading scale out of 5: A+ (95 to 100) = 5.0; A (90 to 94) = 4.75; B+ (85) = 4.5; B (80) = 4.0; C+ (75) = 3.5; C (70) = 3.0; D+ (65) = 2.5; D (60) = 2.0; below 60 is a fail. Honours: first class 4.75 to 5.00, second class 4.25 to under 4.75, but only if you never failed a course, finished within the average of the minimum and maximum time, and did at least 60% of the credits at IU.
- Graduation needs a cumulative GPA of at least 2.75 out of 5. If you pass courses but fall short on GPA, the college can have you retake courses for at most two semesters.
- Ta'jeel (deferral): request before the semester, in the calendar window, with college approval. Ta'jeel and i'tithaar together cannot exceed three semesters (on the two-semesters-a-year system). Ta'jeel time does not count toward your maximum study time.
- I'tithaar (excuse from a whole semester): same window rules and same three-semester combined cap. You are treated as withdrawn from every course (grade W) but not failed. The semester counts toward your maximum study time.
- Course withdrawal: at most two courses in one semester and six in the whole programme, never below the minimum load, not from a course already withdrawn from, not in your expected graduation semester, not if you are already barred in it.
- File closed (طي القيد): if you are absent 20 days in a row without ta'jeel or i'tithaar. The semester still counts against you and you fail courses where you hit the absence limit. You can reopen once, within four semesters, with an accepted excuse, if you were not dismissed, are not in breach of residency rules, and can still graduate in time.
- Academic dismissal: three consecutive semesters with a cumulative GPA below the graduation minimum, or not finishing within the programme length plus half again. One extra chance can be granted (for GPA, only if one semester of 14 units could lift you to the minimum). Scholarship students cannot have their time extended by more than half the programme.
- Disciplinary dismissal: after three years the student may be re-admitted if their plan is still available.
- Transfers inside IU: only within the same track, once, requested by one semester into year two (theoretical colleges) or the end of year two (scientific). Transfer in from another university is not allowed for students on an external scholarship.
- Visiting-student rules (studying a course elsewhere): up to 20% of credits, grade C+ or better, no scholarship students except scientific colleges, and the monthly stipend is only paid if the deanship sees proof you are attending. This confirms a monthly stipend exists but gives no amount.

## Ten most valuable findings

1. The international bachelor's window for 1448 entry was 19 April to 21 May 2026 and has closed; applicants must be 17 to 25, with a certificate under five years old, living outside Saudi Arabia.
2. Required documents: police clearance, a recommendation from an organisation or academic, medical fitness, plus an employer letter for partial scholarships.
3. Manhati preliminary acceptance is conditional; only the Study in Saudi final acceptance counts, then visa fee, medical tests and fingerprints.
4. Barred from finals below 75% attendance (excuse allowed only above 50%); missing a final gives zero in that exam; substitute exam by week two of next semester (1445 guide).
5. Ta'jeel and i'tithaar together are capped at three semesters; ta'jeel does not count toward your time limit but i'tithaar does; first year allows neither except for compelling reasons.
6. A file is closed after 20 days of unexcused absence; reopening is once only, within four semesters.
7. Dismissal comes after three consecutive semesters below the graduation GPA or exceeding programme length plus half; scholarship time cannot be extended more than half.
8. MESH's calendar dates match an unofficial aggregator, not the eduportal; the official excuse/withdrawal deadline is 6 November not 5 November, and the official 1448 calendar is only a JPEG that still needs a human check.
9. The 840 SAR stipend and the "25 programmes" claim are not confirmed by any official page; the old studyinsaudi.moe.gov.sa address should become studyinsaudi.sa.
10. There is a free distance BA in Sharia open to women (July 2026), a fee-based online courses track (applications closed 31 Oct 2026), and an October 2025 official PDF on arrival steps (SIM, Absher, digital ID, bank, visitor ID).
