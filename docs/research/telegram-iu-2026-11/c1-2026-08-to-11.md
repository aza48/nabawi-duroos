## 1. Summary

**What the period is like.** This is the first semester of 1448: timetables, add/drop, deferral and transfer windows in August; settling in, cards and the first stipend in late August and September; then attendance warnings, course withdrawal (closed 5 Nov) and the run-up to finals. Most posters are Saudi undergraduates (Sharia, Law, Economics, Da'wah, Arabic, plus Engineering and Computing), with a steady minority of international scholarship students and a few postgraduates. About a third of all traffic is "which doctor is best". Practical answers are often given by experienced students; official notices appear as forwards from the University, the Deanship of Student Affairs X account (@dsa_iu_edu), colleges and the Student Fund.

**Biggest themes.** (1) Add/drop chaos: the window closed on 15 Aug with most instructor names still missing, and a petition for an extension went nowhere. (2) The stipend: when it lands (around the 21st to 25th, earlier before holidays), new students' first month being paid double the next month, and how to move money off the University Al Rajhi card through Barq. (3) Attendance: the 25% bar, warnings at 10/15/20%, uploading excuses. (4) Course withdrawal and semester withdrawal paths and limits. (5) Internal and external transfer. (6) Housing for Saudis and internal scholarship students in Unit 13, which is paid. (7) Getting to the Haram (University buses, Gate 4 shared taxis, city bus 150).

**What changed in 1448 (flagged).**
- Economics now sits under the College of Business, and Law is its own College of Law (both taught in the Sharia buildings). See section 3.
- External transfer into IU in 1448 was offered only for Sharia and Law (Anzima).
- The absence bar for short courses is reported to have tightened: two years ago the bar came at the 5th absence, now at the 4th.
- No summer semester was run last year (summer 2026), and students do not expect one to be guaranteed.
- New student services: free Gemini AI through the University email; a reply bot in the forum; an unofficial student-run guide site (ygtiu.com) now answers many questions.
- Paid in-person study for Saudi women (Sharia and Law) has run in a women's building for about two years; paid distance study for women outside the Kingdom.

**The 10 most valuable findings**
1. 1448 first semester dates: add/drop and section change Tue 11 Aug to Sat 15 Aug 2026 (28/2 to 2/3/1448), opening in batches by student number; classes from Sun 16 Aug; deferral deadline Sat 15 Aug; course and semester withdrawal until Thu 5 Nov 2026 (25/5/1448); last class day about Tue 17 Nov (7/6); final exams end Tue 6/7/1448, about 15 Dec 2026.
2. First-year students get a fixed timetable and cannot add, drop or withdraw from a course in their first semester; from the second semester they can rearrange their timetable.
3. Absence: the bar comes once absence passes 25%; the system sends warnings at about 10%, 15% and 20%; absence appears in the app as soon as it is recorded; for a two-hour course each absence is about 6.67%, so the 4th absence (26.7%) bars you. Excuses go in the Academic System under Academic, then "Submit excuses for lecture absences", within a set window; after that, through Tawasul (helpdesk.iu.edu.sa) to your college.
4. The stipend arrives around the 21st to 25th of each month (earlier before National Day and Founding Day). New students get nothing the first month and double the next (in 2026: two payments of 840 SAR on 19 and 20 Sept). It lands on a University-issued Al Rajhi card that cannot take deposits or Apple Pay; students move it with the Barq app (add money from the card, then transfer to their own bank, instant and free).
5. University ID and bank cards: the ID is digital in Tawakkalna; a printed card and the stipend bank card are issued and renewed at Unit 22 (ground floor, Comprehensive Services Office, about 8 am to 2 pm, bring a 4x6 photo).
6. Unit 13 housing for Saudi and internal scholarship students is paid: first-timers pay before getting the room; renewal for the second semester was 1,000 SAR transferred to the Student Fund, with the receipt and proof of enrolment taken to the supervisor.
7. Student loans: the Student Fund (Unit 22) lends up to 2,000 SAR, repaid at 200 SAR a month, once, not in your final year, and a good GPA helps.
8. Withdrawal from a course: Academic System, Academic, Enter academic actions, then withdrawal (اعتذار). It is a request, usually approved, gives W, does not touch the GPA, and the same course can be withdrawn only once. Leaving a whole semester without doing this turns into a closed file (طي القيد) after 20 days.
9. Haram transport: University buses link campus, the Rabwa halls and the Haram (Friday morning runs about 9:30 and 10:30, none before Maghrib, return after Isha waits for the janazah prayer); shared taxis at Gate 4 charge 3 SAR cash to Bab al-Salam; city bus 150 runs from the Taibah University B stop (750 m from IU) to the Manakhah tunnel, then a 1 km walk.
10. Honours: under Article 39 of the Study and Exams Regulations, a single failed course (including DN for absence, which counts as a fail under Article 14) rules out honours even after a pass on retake; both grades stay in the GPA.

## 3. Changes over time
- **College structure (circular shared 14 Aug 2026, #618050):** Economics moved from the College of Anzima and Economics to the College of Business; Law (Anzima) is the College of Law, having earlier been part of Sharia. Classes for both still meet in the Sharia buildings.
- **External transfer into IU, 1448:** only Sharia and Law offered (#614556), where earlier years listed more.
- **Absence bar for short courses:** members say that two years ago the bar came at the 5th absence and now comes at the 4th (#627251).
- **Bank card desk:** stipend card issue and renewal moved from the Deanship of Student Affairs building to Unit 22 (#626551, #627201).
- **Summer semester:** none was run in summer 2026 (#627705); students treat it as uncertain for 1448.
- **Add/drop extension:** extended last year, not this year (#618985, #620637).
- **Women's study:** in-person study for Saudi women in a dedicated building for about two years (#630153).
- **New services:** free Gemini through the University email (#630100); the second Postgraduate Week on 8 Nov 2026; the University's partnership with Mutqen running workshops at the Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre (#629778).
- **Library hours:** now 8 am to midnight Sun to Thu, 24 hours in finals (#630725), against MESH's 6 am to midnight.
