8. **Housing for Saudis and internal-scholarship students:** one male unit, priority to Saudis, 1000 SAR per semester plus a 500 SAR deposit refunded on handing back after graduation; shared rooms; apply in person at the Deanship of Student Affairs housing office early; wait from a week to a term. ### 2.2 Attendance, hirman and absence excuses (how-many-classes-can-i-miss, exams-results-and-attendance)
12. **Hirman needs absence above 25% of a course's hours; exactly 25% is not hirman.** In a normal weekly course about 4 absences are allowed and the 5th bars you; for a 1-hour course the 4th may reach 25%; many teachers count a 2-hour block as two absences.
    Posted Jan to Apr 2026, msg #595823, 600794, 603160, 603849, 603850. Source: several members agree. Confidence: high. Status: CONFIRMS how-many-classes-can-i-miss.
13. **Hirman is a penalty grade DN (ح) with zero points; an F (هـ) still adds points.** Both remove honours; hirman hurts the GPA more.
    Posted Jan and Apr 2026, msg #596854, 603688, 603790. Source: several members. Confidence: high. Status: CONFIRMS exams-results-and-attendance.
14. **Check your status:** Academic System > Personal > Absence shows "reached limit" or "hirman" against each course; the app marks hirman in red.
    Posted Apr to May 2026, msg #605840, 605349. Source: members. Confidence: medium. Status: NEW.
15. **Absence excuses moved online in 2026.** Academic System > Academic > "Submit excuses for lecture absence" (تقديم أعذار غياب المحاضرات). Under the old system students could get absences removed in person; now they cannot. Some colleges use their own form (Sharia: "Muwazib service" (خدمة مواظب) Google form for sem 2 1447) or the Wakeel's office; Da'wah college refers to its absence section.
    Posted 12 Jan to 19 Apr 2026, msg #596152, 596197, 598265, 599513, 599699, 604356, 604195. Source: several members. Confidence: high. Status: CONFLICTS exams-results-and-attendance: MESH says take a medical report to your college within a week.

    Posted Jan to Apr 2026, msg #597803, 602761, 601411, 595819. Source: several members. Confidence: medium. Status: NEW.
17. **A sick leave from the Sehhaty platform is needed;** a prescription counts only as an informal excuse. Submit sick leave before the excuse window closes (usually the last teaching week).
    Posted Feb to Mar 2026, msg #600588, 599250. Source: members. Confidence: medium. Status: NEW.
