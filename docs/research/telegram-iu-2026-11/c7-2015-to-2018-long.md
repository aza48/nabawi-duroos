## 1. Summary

**What the period is like.** The group was mostly Saudi Sharia College students in these years. Most long posts are noise: dhikr forwards, adverts (real estate, honey, DXN, phones), lost-wallet notices, blood donation appeals, patriotic posts, and above all long lists of lecturers and exam "tahdeed" (what to study for each lecturer's exam). Useful posts are few. They are mostly forwarded official notices (Deanship of Admission and Registration, Deanship of Graduates' Affairs, the Medical Center, the Rector's office) and student explanations of how the rules work.

**Biggest themes.**
- How registration works, and the 2018 move of all registration to the Deanship.
- Maximum study period and "exceptional chances" for students near graduation.
- Substitute exams and the summer term.
- The long campaign for the University to reopen the Educational Diploma (الدبلوم التربوي).
- The 2018 housing safety inspections.
- Weather suspensions.
- Ijazah and duroos programmes at Masjid an-Nabawi.

**Ten most valuable findings.**
1. Registration rules, officially stated in Jan 2018: register yourself on the Academic System. Stay within the maximum and minimum load. Full sections are not opened, clashing courses are not registered, and a prerequisite (zero-level) course cannot be dropped to take the next one. Go to the Registration Committee in person only in two cases (finding 2.3.1).
2. Housing safety rules, Apr 2018, programme "Your Safety First" (#سلامتك_أولاً): inspectors remove gas cylinders, non-standard extension leads, cooking appliances and curtains. Tidy books may stay in the room. Books boxed for shipping are stored in University warehouses (2.6.1).
3. Non-Saudi children of Saudi mothers who live permanently in the Kingdom receive the stipend like Saudis. They apply at the Stipend Office in the Deanship (Ministry decision, Jan 2018) (2.4.1).
4. When a stipend payment date coincides with the start of the year, it may be split into two payments. New students receive their first month's stipend later (Sept 2018) (2.4.2).
5. Substitute exams (اختبار بديل): the paper is set together with the main exam and can be in a different format. Students say it is usually harder (2.2.3).
6. Students past the maximum study period could be given one or two "exceptional chances" (فرص استثنائية) by the Academic Committee. A graduating student could carry up to 28 credit hours (2.2.6).
7. The summer term was opened per course only when enough students asked for it. There was a "course request" step on the portal before actual registration in Ramadan (2.3.4).
8. The University suspends classes for dust or rain warnings, including evening classes and its institutes in Madinah and Makkah. The Dean of Student Affairs told students to follow @iu_edu and @IUStudentAffair on X (Twitter) for official news (2.1.3).
9. Masjid an-Nabawi ijazah sessions (مجالس السماع) are run by the Department of Mutun and Sunnah. Conditions, place and registration are in 2.8.1.
10. University Medical Center hours in Ramadan were published in 1439 (2018) and differ from normal hours (2.5.1).

### 2.2 Exams, grades, warnings and study period (exams-results-and-attendance, failed-a-course, missed-a-final-exam)
1. **The usual mark split was monthly (coursework) exams plus a final.** Many lecturers used 40 coursework and 60 final. Some set the monthly exam out of 30 and gave 10 for participation or a research paper.
   Posted 2015 to 2018, many msgs (e.g. #5934, #56223, #75164). Source: several members agree. Confidence: medium (practice varied by lecturer). Status: CONFIRMS exams-results-and-attendance (out of 100, pass 60).
2. **GPA is on a 5-point scale and weighted by credit hours.** Members worked it as A+ = 5, A = 4.75, B+ = 4.5, B = 4.25, multiplied by the hours. Graduation uses the cumulative GPA, not the semester GPA.
   Posted 2017-01-27 and 2018-05-12, msgs #67818, #133795. Source: single members. Confidence: medium. Status: CONFIRMS exams-results-and-attendance (GPA out of 5, A+ = 5.00).
3. **Substitute exams (اختبار بديل) are set at the same time as the main exam and are often harder.** The exams department requires each lecturer to hand in the main paper and the substitute paper together, so the substitute may be in a different format (essay instead of multiple choice). Graduates who failed one course have petitioned the College Dean together for a substitute exam. One lecturer required at least 6 students.
   Posted 2016-05-31 and 2017-05-17, msgs #37189, #87607. Source: single members. Confidence: medium. Status: CONFIRMS missed-a-final-exam (a substitute exam needs an accepted excuse). The graduate group petition is NEW but dated.
4. **Attendance bars (حرمان) apply in the summer term too.** A student who registered for summer and then stopped attending was barred a week before exams. The Dean, the Vice Rector and the acting Rector all said the system could not reverse it. Registering for summer is a commitment.
   Posted 2016-06-27 and 2016-08-06, msgs #42713, #44962. Source: single members. Confidence: medium. Status: CONFIRMS how-many-classes-can-i-miss in spirit; summer-semester could add this warning.
5. **A cumulative GPA below 2.75 brought warnings, then dismissal.** In 2018 members said new Sharia College students fell under a stricter rule: three warnings for a cumulative GPA below 2.75, then dismissal. It did not apply to older cohorts.
   Posted 2018-09-14, msg #154659. Source: single member. Confidence: low. Status: UNCHECKED (MESH's guide on graduating from the Ma'had says the minimum GPA for graduation; the 2.75 figure is not in MESH). See Questions.
6. **Students past the maximum study period could get "exceptional chances".** These were one or two extra terms granted by the Academic Committee, through the Vice Rector for Educational Affairs, to finish graduation requirements. A graduating student could register up to 28 credit hours a term. IT staff said the summer term does not count toward the maximum study period; it was unclear whether it used up a chance.
   Posted 2017-03 to 2017-05, msgs #82390, #83059, #83753, #84184, #86883. Source: single members (several posts by the same student). Confidence: medium. Status: CONFIRMS graduating-from-mahad / exams pages (MESH: dismissal for going past the programme length plus half, and one extra chance can be requested). The 28-hour graduating load is NEW.
7. **From Term 1 1438 (2016) the Qada department set one common exam for all sections.** It covered all its courses (history of the judiciary, Qada 1 and 2, Siyasah 1 and 2). Other departments moved the same way later. In 2018 some Qur'an courses were examined by a committee rather than the class lecturer.
   Posted 2017-01-05 and 2018-09-14, msgs #64190, #154659. Source: single members. Confidence: medium. Status: NEW (shows that final exams are increasingly unified).

### 2.3 Registration, add/drop, summer term (find-my-timetable, summer-semester)
1. **Registration rules, official, Jan 2018.** From Term 2 1438/1439, all registration and add/drop were run by the Deanship of Admission and Registration. Its Executive Registration Committee sat in the reception hall on the ground floor of the Deanship building. Rules:
   - The student registers and does add/drop online on the Academic System.
   - Do not exceed the maximum load, and do not go below the minimum.
   - A section closed because it is full is not opened.
   - No registration with a timetable clash.
   - You cannot drop a prerequisite (zero-level) course and take the course after it.
   Go to the committee only if (a) you are graduating or expect to, and the hours you need were not registered automatically, or (b) you could not reach the minimum load because of failed courses or clashes.
   Posted 2018-01-02 and 2018-01-09, msgs #114413, #115374. Source: official notice. Confidence: high (for 2018). Status: CONFIRMS find-my-timetable and exams pages (minimum load). The list of rules and the two "go in person" cases are NEW and worth a short section.
2. **Before 2018, colleges ran schedule changes themselves.** Example: the College of Sharia used an online form in the first week of term (24 to 28/12/1437). Moving between morning and evening sections used a transfer form.
   Posted 2016-09-23 and 2017-02-15, msgs #53033, #79224. Source: official notice and a member. Confidence: high. Status: OUTDATED (replaced by item 1). History only.
3. **The College of Sharia has morning and evening programmes.** Evening lectures are double length, two hours back to back, so a course meets fewer days. From 2017 a new Rector allowed morning students to take evening sections.
   Posted 2016-12-05 and 2017-09-21, msgs #60945, #104587. Source: members. Confidence: medium. Status: NEW (mostly relevant to Saudi students).
4. **Summer term works by demand.** In 2018 students expecting to graduate in summer or the next Term 1 could submit a "course request" (طلب مقررات) on the portal for one week. The path was Academic, then Course Request, then choose, Next, Save. The University then opened only the courses enough students asked for, and actual registration was in Ramadan. In 2017 the College of Sharia took summer applications on a form until 18 Ramadan 1438. Offered courses were shown on Edugate (edugate.iu.edu.sa). A course with too few requests was not offered.
   Posted 2017-06-11, 2017-06-28, 2018-03-08, msgs #91729, #93395, #127291, #127301. Source: official notice and members. Confidence: high for the pattern. Status: UNCHECKED against summer-semester (worth checking that it says courses open on demand and that you register in Ramadan).

   Posted 2017-08-07, msg #96578. Source: single member (forwarded). Confidence: medium. Status: NEW for the applying-to-the-islamic-university page if a similar "confirm your seat" step still exists for any track. Expiring: dated.

## 3. Changes over time
- **Registration:** before 2018, colleges handled schedule changes on their own forms. From Term 2 1438/1439 (Jan 2018), the Deanship of Admission and Registration ran all registration centrally, online, with a committee for exceptions only.
- **Morning and evening sections:** before 2017 Sharia students could not mix them. From 2017 under a new Rector, morning students could register evening sections.
- **Unified exams:** from Term 1 1438 (2016), the Qada department set common exam questions. By 2018 some Qur'an courses were examined by a committee.
- **Ministry calendar 1437/1438:** final exams were moved before Ramadan (Term 2 finals from 15/8/1438, 4 June 2017). Year 1438/1439 started 10 Sep 2017. Whether IU followed every date is UNCHECKED.
- **Educational Diploma:** stopped around 2015/2016. Graduates campaigned to restore it until at least 2018. All parallel programmes ended by Ministry order in July 2018.
- **Children of Saudi mothers:** stipend extended to them in Jan 2018.
- **Housing:** strict safety inspections began in April 2018 (no gas, no cooking appliances, no non-standard wiring, no curtains, no boxed books in rooms).
- **New college:** Judicial Studies and Regulations opened around 2018.
- **GPA warnings:** members reported that new Sharia students from 2018 got warnings below a 2.75 cumulative GPA (low confidence).
