## 1. Summary

**What 2024 was like.** The group is dominated by Saudi undergraduates (Sharia, Law and Economics, Hadith, Da'wah) choosing instructors each add/drop week, with a steady minority of international scholarship students. Peaks: January (S2 1445 add/drop), May (finals, graduation), August (start of 1446, new intake, add/drop chaos), December (S1 1446 finals). Practical content is mostly about registration, absence and حرمان, the stipend (مكافأة) and its card, transfers between majors, alternative exams, and the start of 1446.

**Biggest themes:** the stipend (amount, day, card, cut-off GPA), absence limits and excuses (new online excuse upload in March 2024), add/drop and the portal crashing, change of major rules, the August 2024 mat'am reform, admission moving to the Study in Saudi platform, and Student Fund and Scholarship Fund support schemes.

**The 10 most valuable findings**
1. **Stipend facts as shared in July 2024:** 990 SAR a month for science majors, 840 SAR for humanities; paid on the 25th (Friday moves to Thursday 24th, Saturday to Sunday 26th); paid only to Saudis and external scholarship students; onto a University-issued Al Rajhi card, not your own account (#513519, #521923). MESH now describes payment to your own IBAN (a later change).
2. **Stipend cut-off rose from GPA 2.00 to 2.75** during 2024 (S2 1445): Student Affairs told students a "new decision" stops the stipend below 2.75 (#512391, #513519, #524736, #526602). MESH does not mention a GPA cut-off. NEW.
3. **Mat'am reform, 22 Aug 2024:** fines for unused bookings abolished and the subscription price cut; before that, students stayed up at night to grab the cheapest "first category" tier, queued in the sun to pay, and paid fines. You may send your meal barcode to a friend if you cannot come (#521470).
4. **Online absence excuses launched 25 Mar 2024:** Deanship of Admission and Registration opened upload of lecture absence excuses through e-services portal > Academic System > Academic (#506018). Absence percentage is under Academic System > Personal > Absence (#524086). The "Muwathib (مواظب)" service page is iu.edu.sa/خدمة-مواظب (#526756).
5. **Alternative exams moved to right after finals:** in January 2024 accepted excuses were tested in week 3 of the next semester (#488323); for S1 1446 the excuse deadline was Thu 26 Dec 2024 and alternative exams ran Sun 29 Dec 2024 to Thu 2 Jan 2025, before S2 began (#531037). CONFLICTS with MESH's "by end of week 2 of next semester" (likely changed again; check).
6. **Admission went through Study in Saudi:** on 28 May 2024 the University had not opened its own admission site and pointed applicants to the Study in Saudi (ادرس في السعودية) platform (#510064). Saudi results: 16 Jul 2024; non-Saudi: 24 Jul 2024 (#512791).
7. **Internal (resident) scholarship gives no housing, stipend or allowances** and needs Qudurat plus secondary certificate; employed applicants need employer approval (#524788). CONFIRMS MESH.
8. **Exit and re-entry trap:** a student abroad whose re-entry visa expires before he can defer shows as "left and did not return" and needs a brand new visa as if a new student; advice is to come back, defer, then travel (#513467). Mid-year break: the University issues exit/re-entry but the student pays his own ticket; annual tickets are issued through Etimad (#530477).
9. **Scholarship Fund (صندوق المنح) support for married scholarship students living with family in Madinah:** emergency cash aid form (5 Mar 2024, #505171) and a ration card programme (3 Dec 2024) including families on separate iqamas or visit, tourist or Umrah visas (#529026, #529027). MESH mentions ration cards; Scholarship Fund by name not found.
10. **Ramadan 1445 on campus:** classes 11 to 28 March with shortened lectures from 10 am; the University served 3,000 free iftar meals a day in the lobby of housing units 24 and 25 and warned students against queuing for car hand-outs or taking cash (#505076, #505082, #505660).
