## 1. Summary

**What the period is like.** Five years of a busy forum: 2019 is normal campus life (unified exam questions row, Sharia course choices, clubs); March 2020 to January 2022 is the COVID era (remote teaching on Blackboard, branded "Zidni", remote finals, Ministry relief rules, international students stuck abroad); 2022 to 2023 is the return to campus, a new Sharia study plan, a common first year, a three-semester national calendar (1444), and a college restructuring (1445). Roughly half the long posts are religious forwards, adverts and "how is Dr X?" threads, which were skipped. The useful material is official notices forwarded by colleges, the Deanship of Student Affairs, the Deanship of Admission and Registration and the Sharia College Student Guidance Unit, plus long practical answers by senior students.

**Biggest themes:** registration and withdrawal mechanics; exams and grading under normal and COVID rules; the Sharia new plan; housing rules; student clubs and Deanship activities (free Umrah trips, enrichment courses); Masjid an-Nabawi circles and Ramadan; scams.

**The 10 most valuable findings**
1. **Withdrawal consequences (2020 guidance notice):** withdrawing from a whole semester stops the stipend, means handing back your room, tickets at your own cost and an exit and re-entry visa with travel when allowed; withdrawing from single courses does not touch stipend or housing, as long as you stay above the minimum load. Two steps: a Tawasul request to the college, then the academic system's "academic transaction" (finding 3.4).
2. **December 2023 housing and conduct rules** from the Dean of Student Affairs: students who brought wives on visit visas must hand back their single-housing rooms; no trading, no e-scooters in rooms or corridors, no stockpiling books, no unofficial money collections; the high family "istiqdam" fee to be reduced (8.3).
3. **College restructuring (1445):** Da'wah and Usul al-Din became the College of Aqidah and Da'wah; Islamic History moved to Arabic Language and Humanities; full department list recorded (2.1).
4. **Common first year (al-sanah al-mushtarakah)** existed by 1444; a member says you must reach GPA 2.50 to pass it and become a regular Sharia student (1.9).
5. **New students cannot add or drop in their first term,** only switch sections; exact menu paths in the Academic System for timetable, offered courses and add/drop (3.1, 3.2).
6. **Graduation GPA classification out of 5:** Excellent 4.50+, Very good 3.75 to under 4.50, Good 2.75 to under 3.75, Pass 2.00 to under 2.75 (5.9).
7. **Absence-excuse rules** (College of Law 2023, mirrors IU practice): proof file required, week-1 absences are recorded, decision in 2 to 3 days, then the college guidance unit; Sharia College's "Muwazib" excuse service (5.7, 5.8).
8. **Lost property procedure:** hand finds to campus security or Student Relations, first floor of the Deanship of Student Affairs (9.12).
9. **IU Hajj aid** from the Scholarships Fund for students who have never done Hajj, alongside registering on the Ministry portal in the cheapest category; Ministry payment by SADAD biller 161 within about a day (13.1, 13.2).
10. **Deanship of Student Affairs runs free (weekly in 2023) Umrah trips** for students, leaving early morning from Housing Unit 13 or the University grocery (9.9).
