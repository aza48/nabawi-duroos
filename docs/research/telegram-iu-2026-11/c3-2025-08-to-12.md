## 1. Summary

**What the period is like.** This is the first semester of 1447 (fall 2025), from the release of the 1447/1448 calendar on 3 Aug 2025 to the start of add/drop for the second semester on 1 Jan 2026. The forum is mostly Saudi bachelor's students (Sharia, Law/Systems, Economics, Da'wah, Quran, Hadith, Arabic), with some international scholarship students. Roughly 60% of the traffic is "which lecturer is best for course X" (skipped as gossip); the rest is practical: add/drop mechanics, absence limits, withdrawal, the stipend and its bank card, the excellence bonus, the mat'am, the new college structure, finals and make-up exams, and the extra-chance window.

**Biggest themes.** (1) Registration: add/drop opened in waves by level, lecturers left "not set" for weeks, closed sections. (2) Absence and barring (حرمان): the per-credit absence table and how to lift a bar. (3) Withdrawal (اعتذار), deferral (تأجيل), file closure (طي القيد) and the extra chance (فرصة إضافية). (4) Stipend: payday patterns, the University card, the 2.75 GPA and nominal-duration cut-offs, loans, the excellence bonus. (5) A new academic structure: College of Law, College of Business, Foreign Languages and Translation, Applied College. (6) Mat'am reopening under a new caterer.

**The 10 most valuable findings**
1. Absence limits per course: 1 credit hour allows 4 absences (5th bars you), 2 hours 8 (9th bars), 3 hours 12 (13th), 4 hours 16 (17th). A barred course shows "محروم" in the status column of the final exam timetable and is recorded as zero. (Topic exams, items 30 to 33.)
2. The stipend stops if the cumulative GPA falls below 2.75 (of 5) or you pass the nominal length of your plan; extra semesters are unpaid except at the officer's discretion. MESH's "what stops the stipend" list has neither rule. (Stipend items 52, 53.)
3. Excellence bonus (مكافأة/بدل التفوق): 1000 SAR once a year, paid onto the University card, for a semester GPA of 4.50 or more in each of two consecutive semesters; the cumulative GPA does not matter. Paid in batches (summer, Aug, Sept). (Stipend item 55.) NEW.
4. Official 1447 pattern: add/drop 19 to 25 Aug 2025, opened day by day for levels closest to graduation first; classes started Sun 24 Aug 2025; finals began Sun 7 Dec 2025; semester 2 add/drop 1 to 4 Jan 2026 (from 14:00 on the first day) and classes Sun 4 Jan 2026. (Calendar items 1 to 8.)
5. Official: extra-chance (فرصة إضافية) requests 20 Dec 2025 to 3 Jan 2026 (29-6 to 14-7-1447) through the Academic System; "academically dismissed" appears once 8 semesters are used. Confirms MESH's December pattern. (Item 47.)
6. New academic structure (Oct 2025): College of Systems and Economics renamed College of Law (Civil Law, Administrative Law, International Relations); new College of Business (Economics, Accounting, Finance, Business Administration); new College of Foreign Languages and Translation; new Applied College; Aqeedah and Da'wah gains a Comparative Religions and Contemporary Thought department. (Structure items 96, 97.)
7. Mat'am 1447: new caterer; reopened Sun 31 Aug 2025; breakfast 6:00 to 8:00, lunch 11:00 to 14:00, dinner 19:30 to 22:30; booking open to all from 15 Sept; booking "categories" 1/2/3 are capacity tiers with the same food. MESH has prices only. (Campus items 79, 80.)
8. Official: the September 2025 stipend was paid early on 21 Sept for Saudi National Day, as the year before; payday is around the 24th/25th, about midnight, moved to Thursday if it falls on Friday and to Sunday if Saturday. (Stipend items 56, 57.)
9. Official: postgraduate admission for 1448 opened 16 Nov 2025 with an announced close of 6 Dec 2025 (MESH says 11 Dec: possible extension, see Conflicts). Fee 300 SAR, non-refundable. (Postgrad items 98, 99.)
10. University bank card: replacement or renewal goes through the stipends section of the Deanship of Student Affairs (card office in Unit 22); takes about two weeks; stipend is not lost; you can move money from the card to the Barq wallet and on to any bank without an ATM; University ID card also lives in Tawakkalna. (Stipend items 60 to 64; systems item 69.)

**Conflicts with MESH found:** postgraduate 1448 closing date (6 Dec announced vs MESH 11 Dec); mat'am payment "on collection" vs MESH "book monthly on restaurant.iu.edu.sa" (probably both true, booking then pay at the counter). Details in sections 2 and 6.
