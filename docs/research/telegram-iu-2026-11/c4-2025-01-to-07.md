### Registration, add/drop (Jan 2025, spring term 1446)
- Add/drop opens by seniority/student number: graduating students first, then 3rd year etc.; opens at 12 midnight. Spring 2025 add/drop: opened ~Jan 7-8 for graduates, most others at midnight Jan 8/9; last day Jan 11-12 (closes 12 midnight, sometimes earlier ~10am; extensions sometimes granted). msgs #537195 #536065 #537505 #540303 #543005 #543462.
- First-year (new) students cannot change their schedule in their first term; they can from the next level; first-term students contact the college via Tawasul. #535041 #539052
- Evening to morning schedule: no one-click switch; look up sections under Academic System > Electronic Registration > Offered Courses per plan (المقررات المطروحة وفق الخطة), note morning sections, then drop and re-add during add/drop. If no sections, ask the Deanship of Admission and Registration. #535861 #536754 #544060
- Courses that will not add: raise a request on Tawasul and they add it. #532663
- Drop request path: Academic System > Electronic Registration > Course add/drop request (طلب حذف وإضافة مقرر) > choose course > Remove > choose a reason from list (no need to upload file) > confirm > wait; usually approved next morning, up to 2-3 days. #536700 #536706 #536750
- Cannot go below the minimum load (members say 18 hours in Sharia; max about 25-26). #539427 #539921 #544374 (low/medium)
- A course of your current level cannot be dropped without an excuse; a course cannot be dropped twice. #537272 #541140
- Course withdrawal (اعتذار عن مقرر) may be refused especially if you already dropped before. #537924
- See your registered courses and instructors: Academic System > Academic info > Registered courses. #532222 #532445 #540307
- Elective or compulsory: Academic System > Academic > Remaining courses in plan. #536779
- After a major transfer is approved and new schedule issued, add/drop is reopened for you. #544222. After add/drop closes, changes only via the college administration, about a week. #544553
- Graduating students with 28 hours left sometimes only get 17-18 hours loaded and "no sections available". Recurring Q. #532567 #534887

### Notes L1766 to L2582 (13 Jan to 3 Mar 2025)
- Absence excuses: each college posts an excuse link/QR code at the start of every term (Sharia: QR code at the vice-dean's office); get a doctor's paper, scan the code, upload it naming the course; or the instructor removes it. #545822 #547010 #549045 #550195 #548549
- Hospital appointment note counts as an excuse if it clashes with the lecture; for an exam bring a sick-leave report. #548328
- Instructors have about 10 days to enter absences before the field locks. #550176 (low)
- Absence limit rule of thumb: 16 weeks; each credit hour allows about 4 absences (2-hour course 8, 3-hour 12), i.e. 25%. #550764 (member)
- حرمان = barred from the final, lose the 60 final marks; records as zero for the course; worse than a fail; also costs honours (attendance or conduct cases). #549006 #550266 #550229
- Add/drop can be reopened if many students petition; after it closes, changes only with an accepted excuse or if graduating; Deanship of Admission and Registration is on the 3rd floor. #546118 #546482 #546484

- Course withdrawal (اعتذار عن مقرر): Academic System > Academic > Course withdrawal (also under Academic Services). Status "مدخل" until decided (days to 2 weeks); keep attending until accepted; once accepted grade shows "ع" in course results, course may still show in timetable. Rejections can be reversed via the college vice-dean; prerequisite chain courses (Nahw 1, 2) harder. Withdrawal does not affect GPA. #547471 #547868 #547869 #549213 #549244 #549444 #550027 #550028 #549612
