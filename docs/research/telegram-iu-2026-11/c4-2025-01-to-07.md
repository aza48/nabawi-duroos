## 1. Summary

**What the period is like.** Spring term 1446 (Jan to May 2025), Ramadan and Eid al-Fitr, finals moved forward before Eid al-Adha, then the summer term 1446 (June to July). January is dominated by add/drop and "which doctor" chatter; February to April by course withdrawals, absences and money (stipend, allowances, loans); May by finals, the graduation ceremony and missed-exam excuses; June and July by summer registration, GPA, the excellence allowance and admissions for 1447.

**Lines read:** all 4,303 (to 31 Jul 2025).

**Biggest themes:** add/drop and course withdrawal mechanics; the 25% absence rule and Mawazeb excuses; the stipend (date pattern, amount, GPA cut-off, University ATM card); excellence and graduation allowances; summer term mechanics; alternative exams; Student Affairs services (loans, card replacement, iqama card, airport transport, Umrah trip); the family visit suspension (Jan 2025); a women's section announced for 1447.

**The 10 most valuable findings**
1. Stipend for undergraduates was 840 SAR a month (June 2025), paid around the 23rd to 25th of each Gregorian month, usually 10 to 10:40 pm, moved earlier for weekends and holidays (official notice: paid Thu 20 Feb 2025 for Founding Day). (#560933 #550661)
2. Stipend stops if the cumulative GPA falls below 2.75 (most members; a minority said below 2). MESH does not state a GPA cut-off. (#559085 #560977)
3. Excellence allowance (بدل تميز): about 1,000 SAR for a semester GPA of 4.50 or more in both terms of the academic year; paid in batches in July (2025: from about 29 Jul); lost by حرمان. Only for external scholarship and Saudi students. Not in MESH. (#550904 #561001 #562914 #561518)
4. Graduation allowance 2,550 SAR, external scholarship students only, paid 1.5 to 2 months after graduation. Consistent with MESH "three months' stipend". (#554893 #560890)
5. In 2025 the stipend was loaded onto a University ATM card (Al Rajhi), not a personal account; replacing a lost card cost 57 SAR and took 1 to 2 weeks. MESH now says the stipend goes to your own IBAN: likely a change, worth confirming. (#549849 #561387 #562694)
6. The family visit and recruitment service (الاستقدام والزيارة) was reported suspended by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs from 21 Jan 2025, with pending requests rejected; still not reopened in May 2025. (#547828 #555316)
7. Student Affairs services: interest-free loan up to 2,000 SAR (3rd floor, IBAN and iqama printout); plastic iqama card for 57.5 SAR via the Travel Administration director's secretary; free airport transport at the end of the year (official, from 26 May 2025); Umrah trip in Ramadan. (#551127 #554844 #558699 #551759)
8. Absence: more than 25% is حرمان (exactly 25% is not); 1-hour course 4 absences, 3-hour course 13th absence; excuses go through the college QR code / Mawazeb Google form; حرمان records as zero and costs honours. (#552145 #562903 #555315 #551377 #550266)
