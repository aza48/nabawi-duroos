# French-language community website on living in Madinah: part 4 of 4 (pages 168 to 214)

**README.** Source: a third-party, French-language community website for French-speaking expatriates, families and students in Madinah. It is not an official document. Crawled on 9 November 2026 and stripped to text. This part covers group 4 of 4: 47 pages, files `168-post.txt` to `214-post.txt` in the crawl's `clean` folder. Every file was read in full. Pages are a mix of dated news posts (2024 to 2026), how-to articles on Absher, visas, driving and work, and announcements of study programmes. Each page's publication or update date is given with its findings. Promotional parts (the site's own groups, channels, newsletter, a translator's private number, private businesses) were skipped. Findings were checked against the live MESH guides (fetched 9 November 2026) and against the seed files in `src/server/seed/content/` (some seed guides are newer than the live pages; where they differ, both are named). Citations give the file name and the URL path only.

## 1. Summary

The group covers: driving on foreign licences and the traffic fine bands (168 to 171, 178, 181); family iqama services on Absher (174 to 177, 183, 187, 190, 200, 207, 208); visit, tourist, Umrah and family visas (184, 189, 194 to 196, 201, 202, 204, 205, 210, 213); housing before the iqama (206); births and embassy registration (183, 185); study programmes at Masjid an-Nabawi and the Haram institute (180, 186, 188, 191, 193, 197, 198); Islamic University announcements (179, 182); and work rules for iqama holders (172, 173, 199, 203, 209, 211, 212, 214). Most of the work material concerns employees, not students, and is of low priority for MESH.

## 2. Findings by MESH topic

### 2.1 Driving, licences and cars (`drivers-license`, `drive-on-visit-visa`, `traffic-fines`, `buying-a-used-car`)

1. **A visitor may drive on a valid foreign licence or an International Driving Permit for up to one year from the date of entry, or until the licence expires, whichever comes first (Traffic Law, article 42). This applies to tourists, Umrah visitors and people visiting relatives.**
   `169-post.txt`, /fr_fr/conduire-en-arabie-saoudite-regles-relatives-aux-permis-etrangers-pour-les-visiteurs/ (updated for 2026/27, dated 17 June 2026). Status: CONFIRMS drivers-license (seed): MESH says "for one year from the day you entered or until the licence expires, whichever comes first". The live FAQ `drive-on-visit-visa` only says "Yes, with your own licence if Saudi Arabia recognises it" and could add the one-year limit.
2. **Any licence not issued in Arabic should be carried with an International Driving Permit or an Arabic translation from an approved office, together with the original.**
   `169-post.txt` (same page). Status: CONFLICTS drivers-license (seed): MESH says "with an International Driving Permit if it is not in English or Arabic". The page itself admits sources differ on whether English alone is enough.
3. **Gulf citizens drive on their national licence for its whole validity, but International Driving Permits issued in the Gulf states are not accepted.**
   `169-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS drivers-license (seed): "permits issued in the Gulf states are not accepted here".
4. **A new resident may use the foreign licence for about three months after the iqama is issued, then must convert it; licences from about 48 approved countries convert without a test, others go through a driving school (theory and practical test). The process runs through Absher: appointment, medical fitness check, fee.**
   `169-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS drivers-license: "plan to switch within three months of your iqama being issued"; live page says "around 47 countries".
5. **A car cannot be rented on a foreign licence once an iqama is already being processed.**
   `169-post.txt`. Status: NEW.
6. **The licence class must match the vehicle (a car licence does not cover a motorcycle or a heavy vehicle). Insurance follows the car, not the driver.**
   `169-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS drivers-license (seed): "the class on it must match the car".
7. **Traffic fines fall into seven bands from 100 to 10,000 SAR. Items that matter to families: not using a child safety seat, 300 to 500 SAR; leaving a child under 10 in the car with no adult, 300 to 500 SAR; driving on an expired licence, 300 to 500 SAR. Others: no seat belt 150 to 300; phone while driving 500 to 900; parking in a disabled space 500 to 900; red light 3,000 to 6,000; overtaking a stopped school bus 3,000 to 6,000; driving against traffic 3,000 to 6,000.**
   `170-post.txt`, /fr_fr/le-prix-des-amendes-pour-infractions-routieres-en-arabie-saoudite/ (21 September 2024). Status: NEW for the child seat, child left alone, expired licence and school bus fines (the seat belt, phone, disabled space and red light amounts match the seed drivers-license table).
8. **Paying a single violation early gives a 25% discount.**
   `169-post.txt` (cites a secondary legal site). Status: NEW, unverified (see section 5).
9. **The 50% discount on traffic fines committed before 18 April 2024 was extended to 18 April 2025. Fines are paid through SADAD or Efaa; ignore links, calls and sites offering to pay fines for you.**
   `181-post.txt`, /fr_fr/il-ne-reste-plus-que-3-mois-pour-beneficier-de-la-reduction-de-50-sur-les-amendes-de-circulation/ (20 January 2025). Status: EXPIRED (the discount). The payment warning matches traffic-fines: "Pay: in your banking app under SADAD government payments".
10. **Expatriates may own no more than two private vehicles in their name (General Directorate of Traffic, answer on X).**
    `171-post.txt`, /fr_fr/nombre-de-voitures-que-les-expatries-peuvent-legalement-posseder-en-arabie-saoudite/ (2 October 2024). Status: NEW (fits `buying-a-used-car`).
11. **Residents may import one private car every three years through ZATCA's self-service import platform (log in with the iqama number and Nafath). The car cannot be sold for three years from import unless the resident leaves on final exit. Residents may not import buses or transport vehicles.**
    `178-post.txt`, /fr_fr/larabie-saoudite-lance-une-plateforme-libre-service-pour-limportation-de-vehicules-personnels-pour-les-residents/ (12 January 2025). Status: NEW (low priority).
12. **After an accident, use the Najm app to report it.**
    `169-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS drivers-license (seed): "report it through the Najm app or on 920000560".

### 2.2 Iqama and family records on Absher (`iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees`, `renew-passport-in-saudi`, proposed new guide)

13. **A head of family can renew a dependant's iqama in Absher even while that dependant is abroad: Family Members, then Resident ID services, choose the person, then Renew Resident ID, pay. Conditions: the residence fees for the period are paid; no unpaid traffic fines on that person; the person's passport is valid; the person is not reported absent from work; fingerprints and photo are registered for the person and for family members over 6; every family member has their own passport; the iqama has not been expired for more than 6 months.**
    `174-post.txt`, /fr_fr/renouvellement-de-la-carte-de-resident-iqama-pour-les-accompagnants-depuis-letranger/ (10 January 2025). Status: NEW.
14. **The physical iqama card of a family member can be delivered home by Saudi Post: request it in Absher (Resident ID services), check that the address matches the National Address held by Saudi Post, pay the delivery fee, and track it with the number given. The card must already be ready (issue or renewal approved) and there must be no unpaid fines.**
    `175-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-recevoir-liqama-physique-des-membres-de-votre-famille-a-domicile-en-arabie-saoudite/ (11 January 2025). Status: NEW.
15. **A misspelt Arabic name on a family member's iqama is corrected in Absher (Family Members, Resident ID services, choose the person, Start Service) by the head of family, or at a Jawazat office with the passport, the iqama and the correction form. The correction itself is free.**
    `176-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-corriger-le-nom-et-le-prenom-traduit-en-arabe-sur-liqama-des-membres-de-votre-famille-en-arabie-saoudite/ (10 January 2025). Status: NEW.
16. **Updating passport details in Absher is possible only when the old passport has 12 months or less left, with no unpaid traffic fines, the person not reported absent from work, the person inside the Kingdom, and the fee paid. The page adds: only for people over 18, and only once every five years.**
    `187-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-mettre-a-jour-les-informations-du-passeport-pour-les-residents-en-arabie-saoudite/ (25 May 2025). Status: CONFIRMS renew-passport-in-saudi: "Absher accepts it once the old passport has 12 months or less left, and not while traffic fines are unpaid". The "over 18", "once every five years" and "inside the Kingdom" conditions are unverified (see section 5).
17. **The same update in the Absher app: My Services, Passports, Resident Services. It needs an active Absher account, a mobile number registered in your own name, a valid iqama and no administrative restrictions.**
    `208-post.txt`, /fr_fr/guide-complet-comment-mettre-a-jour-mise-a-jour-les-informations-du-passeport-via-absher/ (21 February 2026). Status: CONFIRMS renew-passport-in-saudi (path: "Passports, then Resident Identity services, then Update passport information").
18. **A wife who already holds an iqama under another sponsor (a company or another person) moves to her husband's sponsorship by a transfer at Jawazat, not by istiqdam. It needs a no-objection letter from the current sponsor, the marriage certificate (attested, translated into Arabic if needed), both iqamas and passports, photos, and a request letter in Arabic. The transfer does not give her the right to work.**
    `207-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-transferer-le-parrainage-de-lepouse-vers-son-mari-en-arabie-saoudite/ (24 January 2026). Status: NEW (low priority).

### 2.3 Exit and re-entry (`iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees`)

19. **Before requesting an exit and re-entry visa in Absher: be inside Saudi Arabia; the iqama must stay valid for the visa length plus at least 90 days (a 30-day visa needs 120 days on the iqama); the passport must be valid well beyond the visa (one page says more than 90 days at the time of request, another says 3 months beyond the visa); no unpaid traffic fines; no travel ban; no huroob or istiqdam violation; fingerprints registered (men 18 and over); no visa already issued and still unused and uncancelled.**
    `212-post.txt`, /fr_fr/fin-de-la-kafala-en-arabie-saoudite-ce-qui-a-vraiment-change/ (28 June 2026); `200-post.txt`, /fr_fr/conditions-a-remplir-avant-lemission-dun-visa-de-sortie-et-retour/ (23 July 2025). Status: NEW. MESH says only "Extra months must fall within the validity of your iqama."
20. **Fees: single exit and re-entry from 200 SAR; multiple from 500 SAR. The page adds that you must travel within 3 months of issue, and that any visa can be checked without logging in on the public Muqeem visa page (vv.muqeem.sa).**
    `212-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees for the fees (200 single, 500 multiple). The 3-month travel window and the Muqeem check are unverified (see section 5).
21. **Two ways to set the visa: by a number of days (counted from the day you travel), or by a fixed last return date. Beyond 90 days, extra fees apply for each added period.**
    `200-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees (extra month fees).
22. **A family member's exit and re-entry visa can be extended from Absher while they are abroad: Family Members, Visa Services, choose the person, pay the extension fee, then Extend. Conditions: the person is outside Saudi Arabia; neither sponsor nor family member is reported absent or in breach of istiqdam rules; the return date is at least 7 days before the iqama expires; for a "days" visa the iqama must cover the visa length plus 90 days; if Hijri and Gregorian dates differ, the Gregorian one applies; the total extension cannot exceed 180 days.**
    `177-post.txt`, /fr_fr/prolongation-du-visa-de-sortie-entree-pour-les-familles-avec-une-iqama-des-residents-ce-trouvant-a-letranger/ (10 January 2025). Status: NEW. MESH only says "higher fees for extending a re-entry visa while you are abroad".
23. **An unused exit and re-entry visa (or final exit visa) for a family member can be cancelled in Absher (Family Members, Services, Visas). The visa must still be valid, the sponsor must be inside the Kingdom, and cancellation must be done within 90 days of issue (60 days for final exit) or before the planned return date, whichever comes first, or penalties apply.**
    `190-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-annuler-le-visa-de-sortie-et-retour-ou-de-sortie-definitive-en-ligne-via-absher/ (15 June 2025). Status: NEW, unverified (see section 5).
24. **For private-sector workers who request their own exit and re-entry visa, the employer has 10 days to object; silence counts as approval.**
    `212-post.txt`. Status: NEW (only for work iqamas; low priority).

### 2.4 Visas for family and visitors (`visas-for-family-and-visitors`, `bringing-parents-to-visit`)

25. **Holders of a valid US, UK or Schengen visa already used once, or of a valid US, UK or EU residence, get a tourist visa on arrival or an eVisa: one year, multiple entries, up to 90 days per stay; about 480 SAR on arrival (insurance included) or about 535 SAR online; card payment at the airport.**
    `195-post.txt`, /fr_fr/arabie-saoudite-comment-obtenir-le-visa-a-larrivee-avec-une-carte-de-sejour-europeenne/ (26 June 2025); `202-post.txt`, /fr_fr/arabie-saoudite-les-titulaires-de-visas-ou-de-residences-us-uk-ou-schengen-peuvent-desormais-voyager-avec-leurs-proches-de-toute-nationalite/ (updated 16 June 2026). Status: CONFIRMS visas-for-family-and-visitors: "SAR 535 online or SAR 480 on arrival". (195 says card or cash; 202, newer, says card only, as the MESH seed does.)
26. **Under that scheme, the holder's first-degree relatives (spouse, children, parents) can travel with them on the same terms, whatever their nationality.**
    `202-post.txt`. Status: NEW.
27. **The no-preparation visa on arrival is meant mainly for passengers on Saudi national airlines; passengers on foreign airlines should get the eVisa before flying or risk being refused boarding.**
    `202-post.txt`. Status: NEW.
28. **In 2025, family and tourist visit visas for some Arab nationalities were capped at 30 days and business and "Charsiya" visas were suspended for them. A 2026 page adds that around the Hajj season (often April to mid-June) visit visas are suspended every year for about 14 nationalities.**
    `184-post.txt`, /fr_fr/arabie-saoudite-nouvelles-restrictions-sur-les-visas-pour-certains-pays/ (3 February 2025); `202-post.txt`. Status: HISTORY (2025 measures); the yearly pattern is unverified (see section 5).
29. **A single-entry tourist eVisa is valid 3 months from issue and allows one stay of up to 90 days; it ends when you leave. No tourist visa can be extended inside the Kingdom.**
    `201-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-utiliser-le-visa-touristique-plusieurs-fois-par-an-pour-larabie-saoudite/ (28 August 2025). Status: NEW (the single-entry option; the "cannot be extended" part matches visas-for-family-and-visitors).
30. **No visit, tourist, business or Umrah visa allows working, renting an empty flat, buying a car or enrolling children in a state school; all of these need an iqama.**
    `201-post.txt`. Status: CONFIRMS visas-for-family-and-visitors ("What an iqama adds": Ejar, a car in your name, no work on a visit visa).
31. **Family visit, personal visit, business and work visas now require an in-person visit to a Tasheer visa centre for fingerprints and a photo before the visa is issued; tourist and Umrah eVisas do not. Children under 6 give no biometrics. Any Tasheer centre in the world can be used: when applying online, enter that country's capital as the place the application will be processed ("Coming place"). The passport can be collected by a representative with a signed authorisation, or sent by courier.**
    `204-post.txt`, /fr_fr/visa-arabie-saoudite-nouvelle-etape-biometrique-obligatoire-avec-tasheer-hors-visas-touristique-et-omra/ (20 November 2025). Status: NEW.
32. **Documents for a family visit visa at the visa centre: original passport valid at least 6 months with two consecutive blank pages; photo 51 x 51 mm, white background, under 3 months old; the Ministry of Foreign Affairs approval; proof of the relationship (marriage certificate, children's birth certificates naming both parents); a copy of the host's iqama; for a visitor living outside their own country, a copy of their residence permit there.**
    `204-post.txt`. Status: NEW.
33. **Expatriates resident in a Gulf state (residence valid at least 3 months, passport at least 6 months) can get a Saudi eVisa on ksavisa.sa: single entry up to 90 days, or multiple entry valid a year with 90 days in total; usually issued at once, up to 3 working days. Support line 920011114.**
    `194-post.txt`, /fr_fr/comment-les-expatries-aux-emirats-ou-du-ccg-peuvent-obtenir-un-evisa-dun-an-pour-larabie-saoudite/ (22 June 2025). Status: NEW (low priority).
34. **Overstay penalties: 100 SAR a day on a tourist visa after 90 days plus 3 days of grace; about 500 SAR a day on an Umrah visa, with an entry ban.**
    `195-post.txt`; `213-post.txt`. Status: NEW, unverified (see section 5).
35. **A one-off 30-day extension let holders of expired visit visas leave legally, from 1 Muharram 1447 (26 June 2025), requested through Absher's Tawasul service.**
    `196-post.txt`, /fr_fr/initiative-de-prolongation-des-visas-de-visite-expires/ (27 June 2025). Status: EXPIRED.

36. **Umrah season 1448: visas issued from 31 May 2026 (14 Dhul Hijjah 1447); entry to Makkah with a Nusuk permit from 1 June 2026; last day of visa issue 9 March 2027 (1 Shawwal 1448); last entry 23 March 2027 (15 Shawwal); departure by 7 April 2027 (30 Shawwal).**

### 2.6 Housing (`finding-an-apartment`, `rent-on-student-visa`, `living-on-a-visitor-id`)

    `206-post.txt`, /fr_fr/etudiant-en-arabie-saoudite-comment-se-loger-legalement-avec-un-visa-etudiant-longue-duree/ (19 January 2026). Status: CONFLICTS rent-on-student-visa: MESH says "Yes. ... It matches the live finding-an-apartment: "Ejar ... needs an iqama".
47. **Before the iqama, legal options are: University housing; licensed furnished residences, serviced apartments and aparthotels on monthly or yearly accommodation contracts, which accept a passport and valid visa (sometimes an enrolment letter) and need no Ejar; hotels. Once the iqama is issued, a normal flat with an Ejar contract becomes possible.**
    `206-post.txt`. Status: NEW.
48. **Avoid undeclared private lets, contracts in another person's name, informal sublets, and any Ejar contract made before you hold an iqama: they give no legal protection.**
    `206-post.txt`. Status: NEW.

50. **The digital birth certificate can be viewed and printed in Absher: Services, Civil Affairs services, Birth certificate services, View birth certificate. More on ahwal.gov.sa.**

51. **The hospital's birth notification is issued only once, as an original; keep copies. A home embassy needs it translated by a translator it accepts before registering the birth and issuing a passport.**

    `185-post.txt`. Status: NEW (unverified for English-speaking embassies).
