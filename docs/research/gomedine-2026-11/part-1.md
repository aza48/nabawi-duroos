# French-language Madinah community website, part 1 of 4 (guides, site pages and 2023 to 2025 posts)

README. This is a third-party, French-language community website for French-speaking expatriates, families and students living in Madinah. It was crawled on 9 November 2026 and stripped to text, one file per page. This part covers group 1: 55 files (`001-guide.txt` to `055-post.txt`), all read in full. Six are long "guides" updated on 19 April 2026; thirteen are the site's own pages (home, about, shop, booking terms, privacy, contact forms), several of them empty forms; thirty-six are blog posts dated 22 March 2023 to 21 December 2025, most from 2023. The site sells furnished flats, guided visits, business set-up help and runs paid or member groups: all of that was skipped. The content mixes durable procedures with dated news, and some figures contradict each other inside the site (see section 5), so every number below carries its page and date and was compared with MESH's live guides (`iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees`, `visas-for-family-and-visitors`, `drivers-license`, `phone-sims-and-absher`, `sim-internet-bank`, `cost-of-living`, `kindergartens-and-schools`, FAQs `check-health-insurance`, `istiqdam-deposit`, `bank-account-without-iqama`, `drive-on-visit-visa`, `absher-nafath-activation`) and with the seed text of `student-iqama-renewal`, `traffic-fines`, `buying-a-used-car`, `renew-passport-in-saudi` and `bringing-parents-to-visit`. Citations give the file name and the URL path only.

Page dates: 001 to 006 updated 19/04/2026; 007 privacy 31/10/2026; 015 and 017 terms 13/10/2026; 018 lists headlines to 28/06/2026; 020, 021, 029, 030 dated 22/03/2023; 022 and 025 30/03/2023; 024 29/03/2023; 023 28/03/2023; 031 08/04/2023; 032 to 035 13/04/2023; 036 08/05/2023; 037 04/06/2023; 038 05/06/2023; 039 10/06/2023; 040 12/06/2023; 041 13/06/2023; 042 15/06/2023; 043 25/06/2023; 044 and 045 02/07/2023; 047 and 048 05/07/2023; 049 06/07/2023; 050 08/07/2023; 051 to 053 09/07/2023; 054 22/07/2023; 055 23/07/2023; 026 22/07/2024; 046 15/02/2024; 028 31/07/2025; 027 21/12/2025. ## 1. Summary

Group 1 covers: opening a bank account, getting and renewing an iqama, enrolling children in school, the cost of living, updating a passport in Absher and renewing family visit visas (the six 2026 guides); then posts on Absher, Nafath, SIM checks, border numbers, Muqeem checks, exit and re-entry visas, visit visa extensions, the permanent family visa (istiqdam), health insurance checks, driving licence fees and swaps, traffic fine objections, vehicle inspection, travel restriction checks, MOFA document attestation, and several 2023 news items (transit visa, eVisa for US, UK and Schengen visa holders, business and student visas, Nusuk Hajj, citizenship rules). Most worker-only content (work permits, Nitaqat colours, huroob, sponsor transfer) matters little to students.

**The 10 most valuable findings for MESH readers**

1. **Iqama renewed late: 500 SAR fine the first time, 1,000 SAR the second, deportation the third.** The fine starts if the iqama is not renewed within 3 days of expiry. (002, 044) NEW.
2. **A family visit visa extended in the 3 days after it expires costs an extra 500 SAR on top of the 100 SAR fee.** (029, 054) NEW.
3. **Iqama renewal (non-student) can be paid for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months: 163, 325, 488 or 650 SAR.** (045) NEW, 650 CONFIRMS.
4. **Replacing a lost iqama costs 1,000 SAR the first time and 2,000 SAR the second, after a police report.** (002) NEW.
5. **The border number (رقم الحدود) can be looked up on Absher without logging in, with the visa number and the visa issue date.** (053) NEW.
6. **Muqeem has a free public check, no login, for visa validity and for exit and re-entry visas (status, type, issue date, return-before date).** (025) NEW.
7. **Traffic fines can be objected to in Absher within 30 days of the violation; status can be tracked, and a stuck objection is taken to the traffic department by appointment.** (043) NEW detail.
8. **Absher shows whether you have any travel restriction or suspended service, from the logged-in home page.** (035) NEW.
9. **Iqama holders pay a dependant levy of 400 SAR a month per dependant, owed while the dependant is on the iqama, and it must be cleared before renewal.** (002, 048) NEW amount.
10. **Saudi school enrolment: the school registers the child on Noor, parents follow reports and attendance through Nafath, and changing school mid-year needs a transfer certificate and a Noor transfer.** (003) NEW.

## 2. Findings by MESH topic

### A. Iqama fees, renewal and penalties (`iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees`, `student-iqama-renewal`)

1. **A non-student iqama can be renewed for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months; the fee is 163, 325, 488 or 650 SAR.**
   045-post, `/fr_fr/comment-payer-les-frais-de-renouvellement-de-liqama-en-ligne/` (2 July 2023, figures labelled 2024/2025). Status: NEW (the 650 CONFIRMS `iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees`: MESH says "Worker of a company or establishment 650").
2. **The fee is paid in a banking app under SADAD, Government payments, Alien Control (Al Rajhi: Payments, Government Payment, Alien Control (Resident Service)), service Renew Iqama, with the iqama number and period; payment shows in Absher's public "Available Funds" query.**
   045-post, same path. Status: NEW.
3. **The sponsor renews a worker's iqama in Muqeem (المعاملات الإلكترونية, then تجديد الإقامة), and a worker's or a dependant's iqama can be renewed while they are outside the Kingdom.**
   044-post, `/fr_fr/comment-renouveler-son-iqama-en-arabie-saoudite/` (2 July 2023). Status: NEW (worker iqama only; student iqamas follow `student-iqama-renewal`).
4. **Late renewal (more than 3 days after expiry): 500 SAR the first time, 1,000 SAR the second, deportation the third.**
   044-post (2023) and 002-guide, `/fr_fr/guides/obtenir-et-renouveler-son-iqama/` (19 April 2026). Status: NEW.
5. **Lost iqama: report it to the police first; replacement costs 1,000 SAR the first time and 2,000 SAR the second.**
   002-guide (19 April 2026). Status: NEW.
6. **A son aged 18 to 25 stays on the iqama with a request form, a school or university certificate, a photo, copies of both iqamas and a 500 SAR fee.**
   044-post (2023). Status: CONFIRMS `iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees` ("Dependent aged 18 and over, issue or renewal 500").
7. **Dependant levy (رسوم المرافقين): 400 SAR a month per dependant, owed as long as the person is listed on the iqama even before arrival, and cleared before renewal; no levy is charged for the first 3 months after a dependant arrives.**
   002-guide (2026), 048-post, `/fr_fr/comment-payer-les-frais-de-visa-iqama-permanent-pour-la-famille/` (5 July 2023). Status: NEW (MESH mentions "the monthly levy for dependants" with no amount). Applies to iqama holders who sponsor family; not confirmed for student iqamas (section 5).
8. **No new card is needed at renewal if the card has no printed expiry date; the digital iqama in Absher or Tawakkalna is used meanwhile.**
   044-post, 002-guide. Status: NEW (minor).
9. **Premium Residency is quoted at "4,000 SAR a year (temporary) to 800,000 SAR (permanent)".**
   002-guide. Status: CONFLICTS `visas-for-family-and-visitors`: MESH says "about 100,000 SAR a year, or 800,000 SAR for life". The source is wrong on the yearly figure; MESH stays.
10. **The source gives two different iqama fees: "100 SAR issue or renewal fee" in one guide and 650 SAR a year in another.**
    002-guide versus 045-post and the related-guide line in 004-guide. Status: internal conflict; MESH's 650 stands.
11. **Worker-only checks: work permit and iqama number by border number on the labour ministry site, Nitaqat colour, huroob status, sponsor transfer fees (free, then 2,000, 4,000, 6,000 SAR).**
    039, 049, 051, 052 (June and July 2023), 002-guide. Status: HISTORY for workers (the site's own 28 June 2026 headline says the sponsorship system changed in October 2025); not relevant to students.

### D. Permanent family visa through istiqdam (iqama holders) (`iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees`, possible new guide)

33. **The permanent family visa fee is 2,000 SAR per family request: one visa number for the whole family. Paid in Al Rajhi under Government Payment, Residents Services, Dependants Entry visa Fee Collection, or in SNB under SADAD, Labour importation (استقدام), Family visa.**
    048-post (5 July 2023), 046-post (15 February 2024). Status: CONFIRMS `iqama-and-exit-reentry-fees` ("Adding a dependent or newborn born outside Saudi Arabia: 2,000 SAR"), but "per family" versus "per dependant" needs checking (section 5).
34. **Absher (Expatriate services, New family visa request) accepts online requests mainly for doctors, engineers and accountants registered with their professional bodies; others book an istiqdam office appointment.**
    046-post (2024). Status: NEW (worker iqama).
35. **Office request documents: the istiqdam form stamped by the employer, iqama copy, residence visa in the passport, passports of all family members, degree matching the iqama profession, marriage and birth certificates attested and translated into Arabic, salary certificate stamped by the Chamber of Commerce, GOSI certificate, company registration.**
    046-post (2024). Status: NEW.
36. **The issued family visa (the "yellow sheet") is valid one Hijri year (354 or 355 days); a baby born after issue is added at the embassy with the mother's passport, with no change to the visa.**
    046-post (2024). Status: NEW.
37. **Istiqdam appointments: Absher, Appointments, Expatriate affairs, then the reason: التقدم بطلب إستقدام عائلة مقيم (permanent family visa), التقدم بطلب زيارة للأقارب من الدرجة الأولى (first-degree family visit), طلب فتح ملف إستقدام (domestic worker). Green slots are free; one active appointment per person.**
    047-post, `/fr_fr/comment-reserver-un-rendez-vous-istiqdam-pour-un-visa-familial/` (5 July 2023). Status: NEW.

    033-post (13 April 2023). Status: CONFIRMS `phone-sims-and-absher`.

### H. Driving (`drivers-license`, `traffic-fines`, `buying-a-used-car`)

49. **Private licence fee: 40 SAR a year, so 80 SAR for 2 years, 200 for 5, 400 for 10; motorcycle 20 SAR a year; replacing a lost licence 100 SAR.**
    034-post (13 April 2023). Status: CONFIRMS `drivers-license` ("5 years (200 SAR) or 10 years (400 SAR)"); 2-year and replacement NEW.
50. **Licence swap: medical check (eyes and blood, about 120 SAR) at an approved centre, result by SMS in 2 to 24 hours; European licences skip the practical test.**
    022-post (30 March 2023). Status: CONFIRMS `drivers-license`.
51. **Objecting to a traffic fine: Absher, Services, Traffic, Dispute traffic violation, within 30 days of the violation; use a computer to see the camera photo; no special characters in the explanation; track it under "View disputed violations"; an accepted objection removes the fine within about a week.**
    043-post, `/fr_fr/comment-contester-une-violation-de-la-circulation-en-arabie-saoudite/` (25 June 2023). Status: NEW detail for `traffic-fines` (MESH says object "as soon as you see it").
52. **If the objection stays "in process" for more than 7 days, book a traffic department (Muroor) appointment with the reason إجراءات الهيئات المرورية and bring the violation number. Repeated rejected objections can suspend the service (the source says 5 rejections in a year).**
    043-post (2023). Status: NEW (the 5-a-year figure to confirm).
53. **The periodic technical inspection (Fahas, MVPI) expiry is in Absher under Vehicles, Vehicle management; a new car's first inspection falls due 3 years after registration.**
    041-post (13 June 2023). Status: NEW for `buying-a-used-car` (the 3-year rule to confirm).
54. **Madinah's used car market opens daily; sales are transferred through Absher.**
    037-post (4 June 2023). Status: CONFIRMS `buying-a-used-car`.

### Target: new FAQ, proposed slug `find-border-number-and-visa-dates`, title "How do I find my border number and check my visa dates?"

```html
<p>Your border number (رقم الحدود) is the 10 digit number you get when you first enter the Kingdom on a new visa. It is written by hand in your passport next to the entry stamp. You need it for Absher, for your SIM, for insurance and for many fees.</p>
<h3>Find your border number online</h3>
<ol>
<li>Open absher.sa. You do not need to sign in.</li>
<li>Choose the border number inquiry, then Non-GCC citizens.</li>
<li>Enter your visa number and the visa issue date, then the captcha.</li>
<li>Press Inquire. Your name and border number appear.</li>
</ol>
<h3>Check your visa or exit and re-entry visa</h3>
<p>Muqeem (muqeem.sa) has free public checks, with no sign-in:</p>
<ul>
<li>Visa validity: enter your iqama or visa number with your date of birth or passport number. You see the visa type, issue date and validity.</li>
<li>Exit and re-entry visa: enter your iqama number and date of birth. You see whether it is valid, its number and length, and the date you must return before.</li>
</ul>
<p>A family host sees each visitor's border number in Absher under Family Members Services, then Extend Visit Visa.</p>
```

### Target: `traffic-fines` (replace the "Object" line with this section)

```html
<h2>Objecting to a fine you think is wrong</h2>
<p>Object within 30 days of the date of the violation. After that, the fine stands.</p>
<ol>
<li>Before you object, check the date, time and place of the violation and the camera photo.</li>
<li>Use a computer, because the photo does not open in the phone app. Sign in at absher.sa.</li>
<li>Open Services, then Traffic, then Dispute traffic violation, and accept the terms.</li>
<li>Open the fine, choose the reason, and explain in a few plain words. Leave out punctuation and symbols, or the form will not send.</li>
<li>Submit and confirm.</li>
</ol>
<p>Follow it on the same page under the disputed violations list. When an objection is accepted, the fine disappears from your record within about a week.</p>
<p>If it still says "in process" after 7 days, book a traffic department appointment in Absher with the reason إجراءات الهيئات المرورية and take the violation number with you.</p>
<p>Object only when you are sure. Rejected objections count against you, and repeated ones suspend the service.</p>
```

<h2>Enrolling in a Saudi school</h2>
<p>Saudi schooling runs in four stages: kindergarten (روضة) from age 3 to 6, primary (ابتدائي) for 6 years from age 6, intermediate (متوسط) for 3 years and secondary (ثانوي) for 3 years. School days are Sunday to Thursday, from about 7:00 to 13:00 or 14:00.</p>

<li>The leaving certificate from the last school.</li>
<li>Several passport photos.</li>

<h3>Noor</h3>
<p>Once your child is accepted, the school registers him or her on Noor, the Ministry of Education's system. Reports and moves up a year are recorded there. Parents sign in with Nafath to see results, attendance and assessments.</p>

<ol>
<li>Ask the current school for a transfer certificate.</li>
