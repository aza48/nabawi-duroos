# MESH: what is left, and every idea worth considering (9 November 2026)

Written by cloud agent 2 for the owner. The actionable split for four agents is `docs/WORKPLAN-2026-11-09.md`. ## Part 2: the shortlist, ranked

These are the strongest ideas across all four research areas, merged where the areas overlap. Effort is S (a day or less), M (a few days) or L (a week or more). The file and number in brackets lead to the full description.

### A. Do first: big value, fits what MESH already has
1. **Now and next on the duroos page.** A top strip with the dars in progress ("25 min in", with a progress bar) and the next ones after the coming prayer, with countdowns. It uses one set of live states everywhere: starting soon, live, ended, cancelled, moved, paused until. M. (schedules 1 to 5, islamic 1)
2. **My duroos, without accounts.** Star a dars, a shaykh or a book on the phone. A "My duroos" tab, push alerts only for what you follow, a lead time per dars, quiet hours, and a personal calendar feed from a short code. M. (schedules, islamic 14 and 15)
3. **Works offline.** Cache today's duroos, the week and the guides in the service worker, which today only handles push. Add "Save offline" for guides and library books, and a tiny text-only page for the weak signal inside the Haram. M. (design 37 and 38, schedules)
4. **Share cards for every page type.** WhatsApp-sized images for each dars, shaykh, guide and answer, a red "Cancelled today" card, and a native Share button. Most MESH traffic arrives through WhatsApp. M. (design 47 to 50)
5. **Event markup on lesson pages and Person markup on shaykh pages.** Cancellations are set automatically from the watcher. Google still shows Event results; FAQ results ended in May 2026. S. (design 52 to 56)
6. **Location inside the Haram for every dars.** Gate number and area, with a link to the official interactive Haram map and a "nearest gate" line. A sisters' gates and sections page goes with it. M (content). (islamic 5, 6 and 58)
7. **First 100 days timeline from the student's own arrival date.** Before you fly, the first 72 hours, the first week, month, semester and year. It links to existing guides, the ticks stay on the phone, and each step shows a real "do by" date. M. (student-expat A1 and A2)
8. **"Who are you?" in four taps.** Brother or sister, single or family, Ma'had, Kulliyah, postgraduate, visitor, applicant. It reorders the home page, guides and checklist, and each path has a `/start/<path>` page to share. M. (student-expat B1 and B2, islamic 3)
9. **Report a problem in one tap, on every page.** It opens WhatsApp to Admin with the page link filled in. Add a "Did not happen today?" report on each dars, and "Suggest an edit" and "Submit a dars" forms that go to the admin "Needs you" queue for one-tap accept. M. (schedules, student-expat I, design 71)
10. **Ramadan and Hajj season modes from the Hijri date.** Iftar and suhoor countdown, Taraweeh and Tahajjud times, duroos paused or online for the season, countdowns to I'tikaf and Nusuk windows, and night colours. Ramadan 1448 starts in about three months. M. (islamic 7, design 6 and 7)

### B. Next: strong, a little more work
11. **Document wallet with expiry reminders.** Passport, iqama, exit re-entry, insurance and each dependant's dates, in Hijri and Gregorian, kept on the phone only. Reminders by push or .ics at 90, 30 and 7 days. Never stores numbers or scans. M. (student-expat C1 to C3)
12. **Question flows for the most repeated WhatsApp questions,** in the GOV.UK style. "Can I bring my family?", "Can I work?", "Do I need a visa for this trip?", "Which guide is mine?". M. (student-expat B4)
13. **Exit re-entry trip checker.** Travel dates in, then the visa type, the fee, and whether the return falls after the iqama or passport expires. Fees must be confirmed first. S. (student-expat C4)
14. **Glossary of local terms, linked automatically inside guides.** Iqama, jawazat, ta'jeel, i'tithaar, mukafaa, zaer, Nafath, Ejar. With it, a phrasebook and full-screen Arabic cards to show a clerk. M. (student-expat G1 to G4)
15. **Help centre polish.** Ask opens on search, every answer starts with a one-line short answer, and readers vote "Was this useful?" with "What was missing?". Add "Copy for WhatsApp", and a weekly owner report of searches that found nothing. M. (schedules, design 73 and 77)
16. **Where we are in the book.** Current chapter, recordings by session and the edition to bring, so a student can join a series halfway. M (content and admin field). (islamic, Bayyinah pattern)
17. **Quran and Mutun circles directory,** with countdowns to their Hijri registration windows. M (content). (islamic 11 and 12)
18. **Quran and hadith references become links** to quran.com and sunnah.com, with optional Arabic pop-up cards. S. (islamic)
19. **Calendar feeds that carry changes.** `STATUS:CANCELLED` for cancelled duroos and reminders built into the feed, so a phone's own calendar shows the change. S. (schedules)
20. **Weekly "Week ahead in Madinah" digest and a MESH WhatsApp Channel** mirroring updates, with a share image for each dars. M. (schedules)

### C. Polish and reach
21. **App icon and install.** Use the gate M as the favicon and app icon, add maskable and monochrome icons, complete the manifest with screenshots and more shortcuts, and show a gentle install card with iPhone steps. S. (design 1, 39 and 40)
22. **Loading, error and empty states.** Skeletons, error pages with Retry, and empty states that always offer a next step. S. (design 26 to 28)
23. **Print.** Guides print as checklists with a QR code, and the week's duroos as an A4 poster or PNG. A printable arrival pack can be handed out by QR at orientation. S to M. (design 51, 69 and 70, student-expat K1 to K4)
24. **Large text mode and a "simple today" view** for older visitors and visiting parents. Adds a text size control and the WCAG 2.2 focus rules. S. (islamic, design 61 to 64)
25. **Fill the empty home column on wide screens** with a "Next up" rail, and add line drawings of Madinah places in place of empty areas. S. (design 4 and 29)
26. **Budget calculator by path** using MESH's own figures, with a home-currency view and a one-off setup cost list. Names no shops. M. (student-expat D1, D2 and D4)
27. **"What changed" page and a one-line "Latest update" at the top of each guide,** with a way to follow a guide for changes and freshness colours on last-checked dates. S. (student-expat I1 to I3)
28. **Mutun memorisation tracker on the device,** with spaced revision and an export button. M. (islamic, Tarteel pattern)
29. **The assistant shows dars cards with buttons** and reads today's live schedule. S to M. (schedules)
30. **Leave-by reminders.** "Home" saved on the phone gives walking time and a "Leave by 5:48 pm" alert. M. (schedules)

### Left out on purpose
Any idea that breaks the house rules: "best of" rankings, partner offers or ads, open forums without moderation, public attendance counts, and storing document numbers or scans. The full lists name them.
